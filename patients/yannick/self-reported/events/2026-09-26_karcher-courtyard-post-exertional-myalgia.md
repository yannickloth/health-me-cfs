## Pattern Analysis

### Key Observations

1. **No headache.** The 2026-09-26 event was only a *suspicion*. It did not develop. There is no headache.
2. **Rather good day on 2026-09-26 overall.** The single notable event was the early arm/hand pain during exertion.
3. **Disproportionately early exertional pain.** Lactic-acid-type pain in the arms/hands arrived far earlier than the workload warrants. Normal exercise lactate kinetics do not explain an early, disproportionate onset.
4. **Correct self-termination on suspicion.** The patient stopped at the first doubtful headache sign. This likely limited severity.
5. **Delayed, generalized spread.** At ~24h, pain spread to sites NOT involved in Kärcher work (arms/hands). This is the main finding to watch.
6. **Character shift.** Exertion-day pain = localized "lactic acid" (arms/hands). Next-day pain = generalized "courbatures" with a stretching urge.
7. **Aripiprazole is effective but incomplete.** Patient clarification: LDA IS effective but not perfect, and is indispensable ("l'aripiprazole est efficace mais pas parfaite et je ne pourrais m'en passer"), yet it does NOT cover the muscle-pain axis ("l'aripiprazole n'améliore rien de ce côté-là").

### PEM Assessment

PEM is **not confirmed**. Current evidence is muscle-pain-dominant, with NO headache and NO reported systemic features (no flu-like state, no brain fog, no functional collapse). Do not assert PEM yet. Confirm or refute over the next 24-72 h.

## Hypotheses

1. **ROT:** Early, disproportionate lactic-acid-type pain during exertion is a marker of impaired oxidative metabolism / early energy-envelope breach, not normal lactate accumulation.
2. **ROT:** Generalized delayed myalgia spreading to uninvolved sites ~24h post-exertion is a central/systemic response, distinct from local muscle damage.
3. **ROT:** Aripiprazole (LDA) is effective but incomplete — essential and indispensable on the sleep/decoupling domain, but it does NOT cover the muscle-pain axis. The muscle axis is a separate, LDA-uncovered dimension.
4. **ROT:** Sustained arm/hand-dominant work (grip, vibration, repetitive motion, forward posture) is a stronger trigger for this patient than heavy intermittent work.
5. **ROT:** The stretching urge (courbature character) belongs to the "rigid after intense training" myalgia layer, distinct from the vascular forearm pain layer (see `notes/gpcr-muscle-pain.md`).

---

## Clinical Significance

- Arm/hand-dominant sustained manual work may be a distinct trigger class for this patient.
- LDA 1mg is effective but incomplete: essential/indispensable overall, yet it does not prevent post-exertional muscle symptoms and does not improve the muscle-pain axis.
- Early self-termination on a doubtful prodrome was correct.
