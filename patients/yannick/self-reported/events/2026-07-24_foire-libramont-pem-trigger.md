## Clinical Significance

### Magnitude Comparison

| Event | Date | LDA Dose | Trigger | PEM Outcome |
|-------|------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Garden work | 2026-07-17 | 1 mg | 5h heavy chainsaw labour | **No PEM** |
| Foire Libramont | 2026-07-24 | **0.5 mg** | Full-day fair (walking, standing, heat) | **PEM — ongoing at day 3** |

The Libramont fair, while high in orthostatic demand and sensory stimulation, is subjectively less physically intense than 5h chainsaw work. Yet PEM occurred, whereas the chainsaw session (at 1 mg LDA) produced none.

### Possible Explanations

1. **Dose-dependent protection:** 0.5 mg is below the therapeutic threshold for microglial D2 modulation. The PEM protection observed at 1 mg is absent at 0.5 mg, consistent with a steep dose-response curve — either D2 occupancy is too low at 0.5 mg to raise microglial activation threshold sufficiently, or the 5-HT1A contribution at 0.5 mg is insufficient.

2. **Exertion type mismatch:** Libramont involved prolonged orthostatic stress + heat + sensory overload — a different PEM trigger profile from the 5h garden work (which was high muscle load but self-paced, outdoor shade, no crowds). POTS/orthostatic intolerance may be a distinct PEM mechanism not fully covered by D2 microglial modulation.

3. **Weather compounding:** Heat and atmospheric pressure changes are known ME/CFS triggers via autonomic stress (thermoregulation, vasodilation, baroreflex). These may have independently lowered the PEM threshold on Friday.

4. **Cumulative LDA dose effect reversal:** The abrupt halving may have been worse than never taking LDA — the microglial system may have adapted to 1 mg tone, and the sudden reduction could produce a rebound sensitization (D2 tone withdrawal → lowered activation threshold).

### Virage Obsessionnel Concern Assessment

- Geneviève's concern about "virage obsessionnel" (bipolar switch) — patient self-assesses as fine, and prior change-log entries (2026-07-23) document preserved function without pathological activation
- The return of PEM at 0.5 mg dose may be confounded by the event itself (full-day fair) rather than reflecting mood switching
- Patient's decision to return to 1 mg on Jul 25-26 suggests self-assessed mood stability
