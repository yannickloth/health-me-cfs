# Event: LDA 0.75mg Sleep Trial — Failure + PEM with Severe Migraine

**Dates:** 2026-09-12 to 2026-09-16 (5 days at 0.75mg), then 2 days at 1.5mg, then back to 1mg
**Context:** Trial agreed with Dr. Nicolas: test whether LDA 0.75mg gives better/more sleep than the established 1mg dose.

---

## Trial Timeline

| Dates | LDA Dose | Rationale |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Sep 12–16 | 0.75 mg | Sleep trial per Dr. Nicolas — better/more sleep than 1mg? |
| Sep 17–18 (2 days) | 1.5 mg | Escalation after 0.75mg failed — test a higher dose |
| from ~Sep 19 | 1 mg | Return to established dose |

## Outcome

| Domain | Result at 0.75mg |
|--------|------------------|
| Sleep | **FAILED** — no improvement in sleep quality or quantity vs 1mg |
| Fatigue | **Extreme fatigue** during the trial |
| PEM | **PEM triggered by "just" 3h driving (2×1h30)** — accompanied by **very strong migraine** |
| 1.5mg escalation | **No effect** (2 days, no change) |
| Back at 1mg | **Much better** — still tired, still needs sleep, but clear improvement vs 0.75/1.5mg |

## Key Observations

- **0.75mg is subtherapeutic for this patient.** Sleep did not improve AND PEM protection was lost — moderate exertion (3h driving, split into 1h30 blocks) triggered PEM with very strong migraine. Contrast: at 1mg, 5h heavy garden work (chainsaw, 2026-07-17) produced no PEM.
- **Very strong migraine accompanying the PEM** — the sleep-deprivation→migraine cascade seen pre-LDA returned at the reduced dose, consistent with loss of the sleep-deprivation/symptom-crash decoupling documented at 1mg (change-log 2026-07-23).
- **1.5mg also failed** (2 days, no benefit) — the useful dose band appears narrow: 1mg works, 0.75mg and 1.5mg do not. Note the 1.5mg trial was only 2 days — weak evidence, but combined with failure at 0.75mg, dose-search away from 1mg showed no gain.
- **Confirmatory dose-response data point:** second instance (after the 2026-07-24 Libramont episode at 0.5mg) where reducing LDA dose → PEM protection lost; restoring 1mg → protection returns. Strengthens the "1mg is the therapeutic threshold" hypothesis.
- Driving (prolonged seated, cognitive-vigilance load) reconfirmed as a non-trivial exertion class for this patient.

---

## Clinical Significance

- Dose-response curve is steep and peaky at 1mg for both PEM protection and the sleep-deprivation decoupling.
- 0.75mg trial failed on its own outcome measure (sleep) AND caused regression in the PEM domain — clear negative result, trial closed.
- Patient correctly self-terminated the experiment and returned to the effective dose.
