ABSTRACT
Comment on Roberts E, et al. (Lancet 2016;387:1638-43), the CRIS/SLaM register study of ME/CFS mortality. Kapur & Webb interpret the finding that the proportion of ME/CFS deaths attributed to suicide was elevated relative to the general population, and discuss the challenges of establishing suicide risk in ME/CFS (small numbers, ascertainment, confounding by comorbid depression), arguing for vigilance about suicidality in this patient group while cautioning against over-interpretation of register-based cause-of-death data.
