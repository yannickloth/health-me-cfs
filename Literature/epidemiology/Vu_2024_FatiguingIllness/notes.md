# Estimates of Incidence and Predictors of Fatiguing Illness after SARS-CoV-2 Infection

**Authors:** Vu QM, Fitzpatrick AL, Cope JR, Bertolli J, et al.
**Journal:** Emerging Infectious Diseases, 2024, vol 30(3), 539-547
**DOI:** 10.3201/eid3003.231194
**PMID:** 38407166
**PMCID:** PMC10902536

## Key Points
- n=4,589 COVID-19 patients + 9,022 propensity-matched controls, EHR-based
- Median follow-up 11.4 months (IQR 7.8–15.5)
- COVID-19 associated with increased incidence of post-COVID fatigue and chronic fatigue
- Fatigue incidence rates quantified vs matched controls
- CDC study (same team as Unger 2024)

## Methodology
EHR data from a US healthcare system. Fatigue and chronic fatigue identified via ICD codes. Propensity score matching for controls. Incidence rate comparison.

## Acronym Definitions
- EHR: Electronic Health Records
- ICD: International Classification of Diseases
- PASC: Post-Acute Sequelae of SARS-CoV-2
- PCC: Post-COVID Conditions

## Certainty Assessment
- **Study Quality:** Medium-High (Emerg Infect Dis, CDC-led, matched controls)
- **Sample Size:** n=13,611 total (4,589 + 9,022)
- **Replication:** Consistent direction with Hadidchi 2025, Unger 2024
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Government study
- **Raw Certainty:** 0.68 (outcome is fatigue, not ME/CFS-specific; retrospective EHR)
- **Population Weight:** 0.85 (post-viral fatigue, not ME/CFS-specific)
- **Discounted Certainty:** 0.578
