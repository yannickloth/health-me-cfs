# Key Findings: Vu 2024 — Post-COVID Fatiguing Illness

## Main Findings
1. **Elevated fatigue incidence:** COVID-19 patients had significantly higher incidence of post-COVID fatigue and chronic fatigue compared to matched controls.
2. **Quantified additional risk:** Study provided incidence rate estimates and confidence intervals for fatigue after COVID-19.
3. **12-month follow-up:** Median 11.4 month follow-up showed sustained fatigue burden.

## Clinical Implications
- Fatigue after COVID-19 is a measurable epidemiological signal in EHR data
- Supports systematic post-COVID fatigue screening

## Limitations
- Outcome is fatigue (ICD codes), not ME/CFS specifically
- EHR-based (underascertainment, coding variability)
- Pre-Omicron era (Feb 2020–Feb 2021)
- Single healthcare system
