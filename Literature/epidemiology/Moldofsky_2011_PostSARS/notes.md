# Chronic widespread musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, depression and disordered sleep in chronic post-SARS syndrome

**Authors:** Moldofsky H, Patcai J
**Journal:** BMC Neurology, 2011, vol 11, 37
**DOI:** 10.1186/1471-2377-11-37
**PMID:** 21435231

## Key Points
- n=22 post-SARS patients (Toronto 2003 SARS-CoV-1 outbreak) vs 7 fibromyalgia patients vs 21 healthy controls
- Assessed 1–3 years after SARS-CoV-1 infection
- Post-SARS patients had chronic widespread pain, fatigue, depression, and disordered sleep
- Sleep EEG showed alpha-wave intrusion during NREM sleep — identical to fibromyalgia
- Established precedent: coronavirus infection (SARS-CoV-1) causes chronic post-infectious fatigue/pain syndrome resembling ME/CFS/fibromyalgia

## Methodology
Case-control study. Clinical assessment, sleep EEG (polysomnography), pain/fatigue questionnaires. Post-SARS patients compared to fibromyalgia and healthy controls.

## Acronym Definitions
- SARS: Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS-CoV-1, 2003)
- EEG: Electroencephalography
- NREM: Non-Rapid Eye Movement sleep
- FM: Fibromyalgia

## Certainty Assessment
- **Study Quality:** Medium (BMC Neurology, small n, historical controls)
- **Sample Size:** n=22 post-SARS, n=7 FM, n=21 HC
- **Replication:** Consistent with general post-infectious fatigue literature (Hickie 2006)
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Disclosed; no major COI
- **Raw Certainty:** 0.50 (small n, historical SARS-CoV-1, no ME/CFS-specific diagnostic criteria applied)
- **Population Weight:** 0.85 (post-viral fatigue/fibromyalgia-like, not ME/CFS)
- **Discounted Certainty:** 0.425
