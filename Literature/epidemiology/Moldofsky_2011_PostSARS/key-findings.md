# Key Findings: Moldofsky 2011 — Chronic Post-SARS Syndrome

## Main Findings
1. **SARS-CoV-1 causes post-infectious syndrome:** 1–3 years after SARS-CoV-1, patients had chronic widespread pain, fatigue, and sleep disturbance.
2. **Sleep pathology identical to fibromyalgia:** Alpha-wave intrusion during NREM sleep in post-SARS patients — same EEG pattern as fibromyalgia.
3. **Historical precedent:** Establishes that coronavirus infection can cause chronic post-infectious fatigue/pain syndromes lasting years — directly foreshadows post-COVID ME/CFS.

## Clinical Implications
- Coronavirus-induced post-infectious syndromes are not a new phenomenon
- SARS-CoV-2's ME/CFS-triggering potential was precedented by SARS-CoV-1
- Sleep EEG abnormalities may be a biomarker of post-coronavirus syndromes

## Limitations
- Very small n (22 post-SARS patients)
- Historical controls, not concurrent
- No formal ME/CFS diagnosis (used fibromyalgia framework)
- Toronto SARS outbreak 2003 was geographically/epidemiologically specific
