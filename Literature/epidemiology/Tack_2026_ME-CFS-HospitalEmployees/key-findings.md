# Key Findings: Tack 2026 — ME/CFS Incidence in Hospital Employees

## Main Findings
1. **Lower-bound incidence:** 3.2% (CI95% 1.3–6.4) of SARS-CoV-2-infected hospital employees developed ME/CFS (CCC criteria) at ~1–2 year follow-up.
2. **High biomarker positivity:** GPCR autoantibodies in 66.6%, possible EBV reactivation in 86.7% of assessed ME/CFS patients.
3. **Diagnostic pathway:** Study demonstrates a practical clinical pathway for post-COVID ME/CFS assessment in occupational health settings.

## Clinical Implications
- 3.2% represents a lower-bound incidence (only those reporting fatigue were assessed)
- GPCR autoantibodies and EBV reactivation are common in post-COVID ME/CFS
- Occupational health programs should include ME/CFS assessment

## Limitations
- Very small sample (7 ME/CFS cases from 221 employees)
- Single hospital in Germany
- Only assessed those who reported fatigue (selection bias toward lower bound)
- GPCR AAB measurement methodology variable
