# Assessment and Incidence Determination of ME/CFS Following SARS-CoV-2 Infection in a Prospective Cohort of Hospital Employees

**Authors:** Tack M, Gruber R, Betting L, Herbrandt S, Wu S, Schlößer B, Häussermann P, Maegele M, Schlang G, Mattner F
**Journal:** Medicina (Kaunas), 2026, vol 62(3), 480
**DOI:** 10.3390/medicina62030480
**PMID:** 41901562
**PMCID:** PMC13027803

## Key Points
- n=221 hospital employees with SARS-CoV-2 infection (Mar 2020–May 2021), assessed Jul–Dec 2022
- 11.8% (CI95% 7.8–16.8) reported persistent fatigue
- 3.2% (CI95% 1.3–6.4) diagnosed with ME/CFS by Canadian Consensus Criteria
- GPCR autoantibodies in 66.6% (12/18), possible EBV reactivation in 86.7% (13/15)
- Coagulation parameters suggesting inflammatory processes in 38.9%

## Methodology
Prospective cohort of hospital employees. All SARS-CoV-2+ employees reporting persistent fatigue invited for assessment. CCC for ME/CFS diagnosis. MoCA cognitive assessment. Lab: coagulation, EBV antibodies, GPCR autoantibodies.

## Acronym Definitions
- ME/CFS: Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome
- CCC: Canadian Consensus Criteria
- MoCA: Montreal Cognitive Assessment
- GPCR: G-Protein-Coupled Receptor
- EBV: Epstein-Barr Virus
- AAB: Autoantibodies
- PCS: Post-COVID-19 Syndrome
