# Key Findings: Unger 2024 — ME/CFS After SARS-CoV-2 (INSPIRE)

## Main Findings
1. **Significant ME/CFS after COVID:** A proportion of post-COVID patients met IOM-2015 ME/CFS criteria at 6+ months. Specific incidence rate depends on analysis — see full paper.
2. **CDC-led replication:** High-profile, multicenter, prospective replication of post-COVID ME/CFS incidence.
3. **Prospective design advantage:** Unlike EHR-based studies (Hadidchi, Vu), INSPIRE uses direct symptom assessment.

## Clinical Implications
- Post-COVID ME/CFS is not just an EHR artifact — prospective symptom assessment confirms elevated risk
- IOM-2015 criteria provide standardized assessment framework

## Limitations
- Self-reported symptoms (questionnaire-based, not clinical examination)
- Follow-up started at 6 months (may miss early-onset cases)
- No pre-COVID ME/CFS baseline
