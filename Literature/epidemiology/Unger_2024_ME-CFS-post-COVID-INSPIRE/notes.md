# Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome After SARS-CoV-2 Infection

**Authors:** Unger ER, Lin J-MS, Wisk LE, Yu H, et al. (INSPIRE study group)
**Journal:** JAMA Network Open, 2024, vol 7(7), e2423555
**DOI:** 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.23555
**PMID:** 39046739
**PMCID:** PMC11270135

## Key Points
- n=4,376 post-COVID patients, prospective, 8 US sites (INSPIRE study)
- ME/CFS-like illness assessed at 6+ months post-COVID using IOM-2015 symptom criteria
- Found significant proportion meeting ME/CFS symptom criteria
- CDC-led INSPIRE study — high-profile replication of post-COVID ME/CFS

## Methodology
Prospective cohort study. Participants with positive SARS-CoV-2 test. ME/CFS symptom assessment via questionnaires at multiple timepoints. IOM 2015 criteria applied. Multicenter across 8 US sites.

## Acronym Definitions
- INSPIRE: Innovative Support for Patients with SARS-CoV-2 Infections Registry
- ME/CFS: Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome
- IOM: Institute of Medicine (2015 criteria)
- CDC: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

## Certainty Assessment
- **Study Quality:** High (JAMA Network Open, CDC-led, multicenter, prospective)

- **Replication:** Consistent with RECOVER 2025, Hadidchi 2025
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Government study; disclosed
- **Raw Certainty:** 0.78
