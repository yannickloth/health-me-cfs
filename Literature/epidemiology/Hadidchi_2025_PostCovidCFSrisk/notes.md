# Elevated risk of new-onset chronic fatigue syndrome/myalgic encephalomyelitis up to four years after SARS-CoV-2 infection

**Authors:** Hadidchi R, Patel B, Madan J, Liu A, Henry S, Duong TQ
**Journal:** Journal of Translational Medicine, 2025, vol 23(1), 815
**DOI:** 10.1186/s12967-025-06625-w
**PMID:** 40702518
**PMCID:** PMC12288244

## Key Points
- n=147,377 (10,667 hospitalized COVID-19 + 25,409 non-hospitalized COVID-19 + 111,301 controls), Montefiore Health System, 4-year follow-up
- HR 1.46 (hospitalized) and HR 1.56 (non-hospitalized) for new-onset CFS/ME vs COVID-negative controls
- Females, liver disease, autoimmune disorders, anxiety disorders at higher risk
- Re-infection NOT associated with increased CFS/ME risk
- None of the acute blood biomarkers (ferritin, D-dimer, LDH, CRP, etc.) associated with future CFS/ME risk
- COVID-19 vaccination in initial rollout associated with increased CFS/ME risk

## Methodology
Retrospective cohort study using electronic health records (EHR) from Montefiore Health System. ICD codes for CFS/ME. Cox proportional hazards models. Sensitivity analyses with varying definitions.

## Acronym Definitions
- CFS/ME: Chronic Fatigue Syndrome/Myalgic Encephalomyelitis
- HR: Hazard Ratio
- EHR: Electronic Health Records
- ICD: International Classification of Diseases

## Certainty Assessment
- **Study Quality:** High (large n, well-established methodology, J Transl Med)
- **Sample Size:** n=147,377
- **Replication:** Not yet independently replicated (Tack 2026 small prospective, RECOVER 2025 larger)
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Checked; noted no major COI
- **Raw Certainty:** 0.75 (retrospective EHR limits, single health system, ICD-code underascertainment)
- **Population Weight:** 1.00 (ME/CFS-specific outcome)
- **Discounted Certainty:** 0.75
