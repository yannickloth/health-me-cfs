# Key Findings: Poomkudy 2024 — Joint Flexibility and ME/CFS After EBV

## Main Findings
1. **Joint hyperflexibility as risk factor:** Joint hypermobility may predispose to ME/CFS after EBV infection.
2. **EBV→ME/CFS precedent:** Extends the historical post-infectious ME/CFS literature with a connective-tissue modifier.
3. **Adolescent cohort:** Pediatric/adolescent ME/CFS post-infectious risk.

## Clinical Implications
- Connective tissue phenotype (hypermobility spectrum) may identify patients at higher post-infectious ME/CFS risk
- EBV continues to be a key post-infectious ME/CFS trigger

## Limitations
- Small sample
- Secondary analysis from a larger study
- Joint hyperflexibility assessment variability
