# Joint Flexibility and ME/CFS After Mononucleosis

**Authors:** Poomkudy JT, Torres C, Jason LA, Fishbein J, Katz BZ
**Journal:** Clinical Therapeutics, 2024, vol 46(3), 285-288
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.clinthera.2023.12.011
**PMID:** 38242746
**PMCID:** PMC11009060

## Key Points
- n=?? adolescents, prospective post-infectious mononucleosis (EBV) cohort
- Examined joint hyperflexibility as risk factor for ME/CFS after EBV/mononucleosis
- Connective tissue phenotype may predispose to post-infectious ME/CFS
- Follow-up from Katz 2009 prospective EBV→ME/CFS cohort

## Methodology
Prospective cohort of adolescents with infectious mononucleosis (EBV). Joint flexibility assessed. ME/CFS outcome at follow-up.

## Acronym Definitions
- ME/CFS: Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome
- EBV: Epstein-Barr Virus
- IM: Infectious Mononucleosis
