# Fatigue and symptom-based clusters in post COVID-19 patients

**Authors:** Cornelissen MEB, Bloemsma LD, Vaes AW, Baalbaki N, et al. (P4O2 consortium)
**Journal:** Journal of Translational Medicine, 2024, vol 22(1), 191
**DOI:** 10.1186/s12967-024-04979-1
**PMID:** 38383493
**PMCID:** PMC10880228

## Key Points
- Multicentre, prospective, observational cohort (P4O2 COVID-19 cohort, Netherlands)
- Assessed what proportion of post-COVID-19 patients meet ME/CFS criteria
- Symptom-based clustering to identify ME/CFS-like subgroup
- Compared post-COVID clusters with a reference ME/CFS cohort

## Methodology
Prospective cohort. Post-COVID patients assessed for fatigue and symptom clusters. ME/CFS criteria applied (CCC or IOM). Cluster analysis comparing post-COVID subgroups to ME/CFS cohort.

## Acronym Definitions
- P4O2: Precision Medicine for more Oxygen (Dutch consortium)
- ME/CFS: Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome
- CCC: Canadian Consensus Criteria
- IOM: Institute of Medicine
