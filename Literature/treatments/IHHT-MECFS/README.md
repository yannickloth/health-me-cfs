## Key facts

- **Direct IHHT in ME/CFS = research gap** (zero published studies; only the REenergizeME protocol).
- **No published IHHT long-COVID RCT.** The 3 anchor studies (protocol refs 39-41): Doehner2024 (controlled, non-randomized), Kapel2025 (open, non-controlled), Zha2024 (intermittent-hypoxia, normoxia-control RCT).
- **Cohort/provenance overlap**: Kapel2025 shares investigators (Olsen RJ, Stokholm, Foldager, Nochi Z) and device (SANA/HypoxBreath) with REenergizeME; its SF-36 effect drives the trial's power calculation. Commercial COI (SANA Medical Systems).
- IHHT is **distinct from HBOT** (ambient-pressure alternating hypoxia+hyperoxia vs pressurized pure oxygen). See `experimental-hypoxia-virtual-hypoxia-brainstem` stream.
