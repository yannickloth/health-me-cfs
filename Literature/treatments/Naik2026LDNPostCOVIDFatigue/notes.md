# Notes — Naik 2026 (LDN for Post-COVID Fatigue Syndrome RCT)

**Authors:** Naik H, Boulter T, Cooke E, Cristobal J, Izquierdo AM, Colla Machado BA, Khor E, Flanagan N, Tsai M, Robertson H, Gray G, Fisher C, Song X, Levin A, Bone J, Nacul L
**Journal:** medRxiv (preprint) · 2026.10.22.26363582 · posted 2026-10-26
**DOI:** 10.64898/2026.10.22.26363582 · **Trial:** NCT05430152
**Corresponding author:** Luis Nacul (UBC; senior author; UK ME/CFS Biobank founder)

## Key Points
- **First randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of LDN for post-COVID fatigue syndrome (PCFS).** Prior evidence was observational (Polo 2019, O'Kelly 2022, Tamariz 2024, Isman 2024).
- **NULL result.** LDN (1.0→4.5 mg/day, 16 weeks) did NOT improve fatigue (FSS) vs placebo in n=160.
- **Well-conducted and adequately powered:** n=160 (planned 80/arm to detect a 4.7-point FSS difference, d=0.5, 80% power); decentralized; stratified by sex; block randomization (2/4/6); independent DSMB; SAP finalized before database lock.
- **Well tolerated:** any AE 79.0% (LDN) vs 83.5% (placebo); grade ≥3 AE 6.2% vs 3.8%. No safety signal, but also no efficacy signal.
- **Adherence asymmetry:** 77.9% LDN vs 86.8% placebo reached ≥80% of prescribed dose; 4.5 mg final dose reached by 76.7% (LDN) vs 90.8% (placebo) — a modest between-arm difference to note in per-protocol interpretation.

|---|---|---|---|
| FSS (primary) | 4.2 | 4.4 | −0.76 (−4.18 to 2.67) |
| Pain VAS | — | — | 0.03 (−0.50 to 0.57) |
| PQSymp-12 | — | — | −0.70 (−2.57 to 1.17) |
| Daily step count | — | — | −5 (−720 to 729) |

Sensitivity analyses (unadjusted, additionally adjusted, multiple imputation, per-protocol/adherent subgroup) were all consistent with the null (Supplement 3 Table S7). No prespecified subgroup (sex, comorbidity burden, age, time since COVID, baseline FSS, SARS-CoV-2 ascertainment) showed significant effect modification (Supplement 3 Table S3).

## Methodology
Decentralized double-blind RCT; BC + Ontario, Canada (Jan 2024–Feb 2026). Adults 19–69 with PCFS (new ME/CFS-like symptoms ≥3 months after SARS-CoV-2). 1:1 LDN (1 mg→4.5 mg over 4 weeks, then maintained) vs matching placebo, 16 weeks. Primary = FSS (MMRM, adjusted for baseline score, sex, site). Secondary = pain VAS, PQSymp-12, step count, EQ-5D-5L. Exploratory = inflammatory markers (IL-6, IFNγ, hsCRP, 15-plex cytokine), CK, rT3/T3, cortisol/ACTH, POTS/orthostatic prevalence, handgrip, sit-to-stand, PHQ-9/GAD-7, PEM subscore.

## Acronym Definitions (verified from paper)
- **LDN** = low-dose naltrexone
- **PCFS** = post-COVID fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS-like symptoms ≥3 months post-SARS-CoV-2)
- **FSS** = Fatigue Severity Scale (9-item; abstract reports mean 4.2–4.4, AMD −0.76 — the sample-size section scales FSS as 9–63)
- **PQSymp-12** = Patient Phenotyping Questionnaire Short Form (12-item ME/CFS symptom score)
- **PEM** = post-exertional malaise (a PQSymp-12 subscore, 0–3)
- **EQ-5D-5L** = EuroQol 5-dimension 5-level (health utility, Canadian preference weights)
- **AMD** = adjusted mean difference; **MMRM** = mixed model for repeated measures
- **SAP** = statistical analysis plan; **DSMB** = Data Safety and Monitoring Board

## Certainty Assessment
- **Study Quality:** High design (double-blind, placebo-controlled, n=160, prespecified SAP) but **preprint — not peer-reviewed**
- **Sample Size:** n=160 (81 LDN / 79 placebo)
- **Replication:** First RCT in PCFS; directionally consistent with the fibromyalgia LDN null RCTs (DueBruun 2024 FINAL, Nielsen 2026, Rodriguez-Freire 2026 INNOVA)
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Disclosed; funders (CIHR, ME Association) had no role; HN declared honoraria from Canadian post-COVID guidelines team + fellowship funding; no drug-company support
- **Population-relevance discount:** 0.85 (post-COVID fatigue / Long COVID, not classic ME/CFS — PCFS requires post-SARS-CoV-2 onset)
