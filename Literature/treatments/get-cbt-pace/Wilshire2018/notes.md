## Key findings captured in the document

1. Original pre-change outcome thresholds: recovery dropped from 22% to 7% — not
   significantly different from control groups
2. Step-count actigraphy showed no significant improvement in any group
3. Six-minute walk improvements clinically trivial (~20 meters)
4. Long-term follow-up converged across groups — no durable benefit
5. Effects confined to self-report measures; consistent with reporting bias
