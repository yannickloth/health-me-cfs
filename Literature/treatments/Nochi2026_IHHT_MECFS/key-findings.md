# Nochi 2026 — REenergizeME: IHHT protocol (BMJ Open)

**Citation:** Nochi M, Kristensen SK, Mehrani N, et al. BMJ Open 2026;16:e117729. doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2026-117729
**Type:** RCT protocol (not yet results) — randomised, placebo-controlled, participant- and assessor-blinded
**Registration:** ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07317401
**Ethics:** Danish Scientific Ethical Committees, approval no. 2500959

## Design
- 104 female patients, 20–59 y, International Consensus Criteria ME/CFS, mild–moderate (NICE), PEM documented, disease >6 months.
- 1:1 IHHT vs sham. +20 healthy controls (baseline comparisons only).
- Randomisation: permuted block, stratified by age, disease duration, severity (SF-36 vitality). REDCap.
- Six treatment sessions over 8 weeks (6–8 day spacing).

## Intervention
- HypoxBreath device (Telmed Telemedizin GmbH, CE class IIa), dynamically adjusts FiO2.
- Session: 4–7 alternating hypoxia–hyperoxia cycles, 22–40 min total. SpO2 9–13% hypoxic, 36% hyperoxic restitution.
- Hormesis principle — low-frequency, individualised dosing to avoid PEM.
- Sham: room air 21% FiO2, airflow/acoustic cues mimicked.

## Primary endpoint
- Change baseline→post-treatment in SF-36 vitality domain (ANCOVA, 80% power, α 0.05, n=52/group, 15% dropout).
- Effect assumption from non-controlled long-COVID IHHT cohort: mean +20.5 (SD 22.5); placebo +6.8; between-group 13.7.

## Secondary/exploratory outcomes
- PROMs: SF-36, FSS, FUNCAP-27 (PEM + functional capacity), COMPASS-31, painDETECT.
- Objective: d2-R attention test; NIRS muscle oxygenation during repeated handgrip; handgrip strength/fatigability; NLT orthostatic; Valsalva; Sudoscan; thermal QST; corneal confocal microscopy; skin punch biopsy (IENFD, VIP/DbH autonomic fibres, macrophages, mitochondria, microvasculature); biobank (PBMC flux analysis, oxidative/inflammatory markers, metabolomic/proteomic/transcriptomic).

## Mechanism rationale
- ME/CFS = recurrent mild hypoxic injury to brain/muscle → mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, inflammation → PEM.
- Functional autoantibodies (adrenergic/muscarinic/endothelin) may disrupt microcirculation and O2 delivery.
- IHHT preconditions tissues via HIF-1α stabilization + redox-sensitive stress/repair pathways → hypoxia tolerance, improved mitochondrial + vascular + autonomic function.

## Safety
- Low risk; known transient AEs: headache (day of/after), short-lived fatigue.
- PEM exacerbation >48h = AE, prospectively documented. Continuous SpO2/HR monitoring.
- Exclusions: pregnancy, obstructive lung disease, claustrophobia, pneumothorax history, interfering devices.
- No independent DMC (low risk); sponsor can pause/terminate.

## Limitations (self-declared)
- Dose/regimen not ME/CFS-validated — extrapolated from post-viral fatigue + clinical experience, no ME/CFS pilot.
- Recruitment from specialist settings; bedbound/very-severe underrepresented; female-only limits male generalisability.
- Sample size from observational non-controlled data; type II error risk; sham may not fully mask.
- Not powered for subgroup or small mechanistic effects.

## Timelines
- Recruitment from 1 June 2026; data collection completion anticipated January 2029.
- Patient advisory board incl. Danish ME Association (ME Foreningen) informed design.
