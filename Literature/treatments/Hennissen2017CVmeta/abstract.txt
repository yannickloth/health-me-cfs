Title: Cardiovascular Effects of Stimulant and Non-Stimulant Medication for Children and Adolescents with ADHD: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Trials of Methylphenidate, Amphetamines and Atomoxetine
Authors: Hennissen L, Bakker MJ, Banaschewski T, Carucci S, Coghill D, Danckaerts M, Dittmann RW, Hollis C, Kovshoff H, McCarthy S, Nagy P, Sonuga-Barke E, Wong IC, Zuddas A, Rosenthal E, Buitelaar JK; ADDUCE consortium
Journal: CNS Drugs
Year: 2017
Volume: 31
Issue: 3
Pages: 199-215
DOI: 10.1007/s40263-017-0410-7
PMID: 28236285

Retrieved: 2026-10-20

ABSTRACT
OBJECTIVES: evaluate cardiovascular effects of methylphenidate (MPH), amphetamines (AMP), atomoxetine (ATX) in children/adolescents with ADHD. METHODS: systematic review + meta-analysis of pre-post SBP/DBP/HR. RESULTS: 18 trials, 5837 participants (80.7% boys), average 28.7 weeks. All three increased SBP (MPH SMD 0.25 [0.08-0.42]; AMP 0.09 [0.03-0.15]; ATX 0.16 [0.04-0.27]). AMP increased DBP (0.16 [0.03-0.29]) and HR (0.37 [0.13-0.60]); ATX increased DBP (0.22 [0.10-0.34]) and HR (0.43 [0.26-0.60]); MPH no pre-post DBP/HR effect. 12.6% reported other cardiovascular effects; 2% discontinued for CV effects; most resolved spontaneously. CONCLUSIONS: small significant BP/HR increases; clinically relevant in a minority; monitor BP/HR.
