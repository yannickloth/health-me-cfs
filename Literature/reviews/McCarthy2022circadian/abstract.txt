Title: Circadian rhythm disruption in Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome: Implications for the post-acute sequelae of COVID-19
Authors: Michael J. McCarthy
Journal: Brain, Behavior, & Immunity - Health 2022;20:100412
DOI: 10.1016/j.bbih.2022.100412
PMID: 35465246
PMCID: PMC9019698
URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9019698/
Retrieved: 2026-10-27

Abstract:
Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS) is characterized by persistent fatigue, exercise intolerance, sleep disturbances, autonomic dysfunction, and cognitive problems. Circadian rhythm disruption has been examined as a contributing factor. This review covers older and newer studies, focusing on transforming growth factor beta (TGFB), a cytokine associated with ME/CFS that also has roles in circadian rhythms, especially in peripheral cells. The author proposes that disrupted TGFB signaling in ME/CFS may disrupt physiological rhythms in sleep, activity, and cognition, leading to insomnia, energy disturbances, cognitive problems, depression, and autonomic dysfunction. Chronobiological considerations may have implications for understanding ME/CFS in the context of COVID-19 and possible therapeutic avenues.
