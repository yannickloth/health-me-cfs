# Salih LY et al. 2026 — Pathogenic Role of FGFR3 Autoantibodies in Small Fiber Neuropathy

- Journal: Advanced Science 2026;13(22):e11413. PMID 41700748. PMCID PMC13088302.
- DOI: 10.1002/advs.202511413. Full-text PDF (open access) archived in this folder.
- Position: SUPPORT (mechanistic pathogenicity only — NOT diagnostic utility). Discounted certainty 0.28.
- Population: general / mouse model. NO ME/CFS cohort.
- Key finding: FGFR3-autoantibody sera bind DRG sensory neurons, induce hyperexcitability + mechanical
  hypersensitivity in mice; CRISPR knockout of neuronal FGFR3 blocks sensitisation. Translational only;
  group shares FGFR3-antibody patents/interest with the Saint-Etienne/Lyon discoverers.
- Caveat for Phase 2: mechanism only; does not validate the serological marker for ME/CFS.
