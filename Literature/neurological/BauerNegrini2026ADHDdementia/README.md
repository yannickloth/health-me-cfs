## Why This Paper
Driving paper for integrate-topic `adhd-dementia-energy-deficit`. Establishes, with convergent EHR-diagnosis and ADHD-PRS genetic evidence, that ADHD is associated with a ~4-fold hazard of incident MCI/dementia. Frames the mechanistic question: is a shared cerebral/neuronal ENERGY DEFICIT a plausible common mechanism linking ADHD/autism → dementia, and by analogy → ME/CFS cognitive symptoms?
