# Geerts M et al. 2021 — IVIG RCT in painful idiopathic SFN

- Journal: Neurology 2021;96(20):e2534-e2545. PMID 33766992. PMCID PMC8205474.
- DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000011919. Full-text PDF (open access) archived in this folder.
- Position: CHALLENGE (Class I null for IVIG in unselected idiopathic SFN). Discounted certainty 0.42.
- Population: n=60 painful skin-biopsy-proven idiopathic SFN (Maastricht). NO ME/CFS arm; not
  antibody-stratified.
- Key finding: 40% IVIG vs 30% placebo reached >=1-point pain reduction (p=0.588; OR 1.56, 95% CI
  0.53-4.53). No significant benefit on any prespecified outcome.
- Caveat for Phase 2: does not directly test TS-HDS/FGFR3-positive patients but anchors the
  immunotherapy null in SFN; supports no-routine-IVIG framing.
