# Appeltshauser L et al. 2025 — Inter-laboratory comparison of anti-FGFR3 antibody test

- Journal: Frontiers in Immunology 2025;16:1604456. PMID 41142778. PMCID PMC12549695.
- DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2025.1604456. Full-text PDF (open access) archived in this folder.
- Position: CHALLENGE (assay reproducibility). Discounted certainty 0.33.
- Population: n=42 (18 neuronopathy, 18 SFN, 6 controls); France + Germany. NO ME/CFS cohort.
- Key finding: ELISA concordance 81% (kappa 0.61); discordance near cut-off; optical densities
  systematically LOWER at one centre (0.076 vs 0.293, p<0.0001) — cut-offs lab-specific. DRG
  immunofluorescence improves specificity (68.8% double-positive vs 5% ELISA-negative). No standardised
  anti-FGFR3 protocol exists.
- Caveat for Phase 2: cite as reproducibility weakness against a strong serological diagnostic claim.
