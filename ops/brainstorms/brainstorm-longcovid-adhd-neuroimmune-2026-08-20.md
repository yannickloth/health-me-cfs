# Brainstorm: Long Covid–AD(H)D Neuroimmune Framework — Research Directions

**Date:** 2026-08-20
**Origin:** /integrate-topic longcovid-adhd-neuroimmune (Spanoghe et al. 2026, Brain Behav Immun, viewpoint)
**Phase:** 4 (Creative Brainstorming) — all categories 1–12 allowed under PROCEED
**Anchor mechanisms:** (1) frontal–striatal–hippocampal circuit dysfunction; (2) neuroimmune dysregulation of catecholamine (DA/NA) systems; (3) tryptophan–kynurenine overactivation; (4) mitochondrial bioenergetic defects. Plus precision-immunopsychiatry immune-subtyping, off-label ADHD pharmacotherapy, and the "acquired neuroimmune-mediated dopaminergic/noradrenergic dysfunction" hypothesis.

**Critical constraint (from Phase 2, PROCEED):** The *causal* "infection drives new ADHD" claim MUST stay an `@oq:lc-adhd-acquired-causal` open question — Zemer2024 (null, 0.60) found no independent COVID→ADHD effect; the post-COVID prescribing rise is consistent with catch-up diagnosis. No brainstorm idea may assert infection-driven ADHD as settled. Ideas that presuppose acquisition are flagged speculative and must carry an unmasking-vs-acquired discriminating design.

**Cross-connection anchors used:** `@spec:adhd-mecfs-same-root` (ch14d), `@spec:lc-adhd-neuroimmune-shared-substrate` (registry), `@spec:adhd-dopamine-nrf2-nlrp3-xdisease`, `@spec:dopaminergic-augmentation-mecfs`, `@hyp:selective-dysfunction`, `@hyp:energy-triage` / `@spec:cns-energy-triage` (ch07), `@spec:brain-energy-cross-disease` / `@sec:brain-energy-cross-disease`, `@hyp:cd8-mito-fragmentation-exhaustion`, `@hyp:striatal-symptom-signalling` / `@hyp:vta-sn-dopaminergic` / `@spec:csf-exosome-vmat2-dbh` / `@oq:vmat2-generalizability` (striatal DA corpus), `@hyp:peripheral-serotonin-convergence`, kynurenine-excitotoxicity stream (ch14c / `sec-28-prefrontal-executive-energy-adhd.typ`), `@spec:nrf2-exhaustion-hif2a`.

| 1.2 | **"Kynurenine-mediated DA tone titration" hypothesis:** Chronic (not acute) kynurenic-acid elevation acts as an endogenous low-grade DA/NA tone suppressor, titrating the inverted-U DA dose-response toward the impaired limb and reproducing an ADHD-like *state* that mimics the *trait* | 0.35 | Direct bridge mech 3→2: @Rassoulpour2005KynurenicAcid (kynurenic acid lowers striatal DA) + @Cysique2023KynureninePASC (kynurenine↔cognitive impairment). ## Category 2: Research Directions

| # | Idea | Certainty | Design / cohort / measure |
|---|------|-----------|---------------------------|
| 2.1 | **Acquired-vs-unmasked discriminating cohort (THE key design):** longitudinal prospective cohort of pre-COVID ADHD-screened adults followed post-infection, with (a) ADHD symptom scores, (b) DA/catecholamine CSF or PET markers (VMAT2, D2), (c) kynurenine pathway metabolites, (d) immune subtyping — to separate true acquisition (marker change without prior trait) from unmasking (trait present, symptom threshold crossed) | 0.55 | Directly answers the Zemer2024 null's blind spot (population rates can't distinguish). | 2.2 | **Striatal VMAT2/kynurenine dual-imaging study in ME/CFS+ADHD:** measure VMAT2 binding + brain kynurenine (or PFC MRS) in ME/CFS±ADHD and LC±ADHD, testing whether DA-terminal reduction tracks kynurenine load across both | 0.42 | Directly tests mech 3→2→circuit; extends the LC VMAT2 finding into ME/CFS where it is absent (`@oq:vmat2-generalizability`; registry line 412). | 2.3 | **Catch-up-vs-trend-change pharmacoepidemiology re-analysis:** decompose the post-COVID stimulant rise (Gimbach2024, Koonce2024) by age-at-first-prescription, ADHD-screened history, and symptom-onset timing to distinguish delayed diagnosis from new onset | 0.50 | Resolves the ambiguity the literature summary flags (catch-up vs trend-change); complements Zemer2024 rather than refuting it. | 2.4 | **Twin-discordant ME/CFS+ADHD striatal study:** monozygotic twins discordant for post-infectious illness, measuring VMAT2/DA and kynurenine, to isolate acquired from shared-genetic DA pathology | 0.48 | Uses the established twin-discordant design already in the registry's striatal hypothesis; directly tests "acquired DA dysfunction" under genetic control.
