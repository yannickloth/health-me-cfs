# Brainstorm backlog — neurodivergent-psychological-consequences-mecfs

Date: 2026-09-02 | Phase 4 of integrate-topic cycle

## 1. Diagnostic / recognition
1.1 *Screening tool:* Adapt a brief PEM screen for autistic/ADHD adults in primary care to reduce diagnostic overshadowing.
1.2 *Clinician prompt:* EHR alert when an autistic/ADHD patient presents with new fatigue — require PEM documentation before attribution.
1.3 *Patient-facing guide:* "Is this autism burnout or ME/CFS?" decision aid with PEM as discriminator.

## 2. Mechanism
2.1 *Masking-energy model:* Quantify the independent energy cost of autistic/ADHD camouflaging in ME/CFS using ecological momentary assessment.
2.2 *Prefrontal resource pool:* Test whether social-interaction cost in ND+ME/CFS is additive (ND mask + ME/CFS mask) or synergistic (costs amplify each other).
2.3 *Sensory load:* ND sensory hypersensitivity may compound ME/CFS sensory intolerance; measure joint contribution to PEM.

## 3. Therapeutic / accommodation
3.1 *Reduce masking demand:* Clinical recommendation that ND patients with ME/CFS be allowed to "drop the mask" during appointments to conserve energy.
3.2 *Communication accommodations:* Offer written/async communication, reduced eye-contact expectations, explicit scheduling.
3.3 *Identity-preserving pacing:* Design pacing plans that protect access to special interests even when broader activity is restricted.

## 4. Research design
4.1 *Comorbid cohort:* Prospectively recruit autistic/ADHD adults meeting IOM ME/CFS criteria vs ND-only vs ME/CFS-only; compare psychological outcomes.
4.2 *Mixed-methods:* Qualitative interviews on loss of special interests, identity grief, and diagnostic delay in ND+ME/CFS.
4.3 *Longitudinal:* Track ND+ME/CFS patients over 12–24 months to measure transition rates and masking-adjusted severity.

## 5. Biomarker
5.1 *Camouflaging-correlates:* Correlate self-reported masking with HRV, cortisol, or actigraphy-derived recovery markers in ND+ME/CFS.
5.2 *Cognitive load proxy:* Use pupillometry or reaction-time variability during social-interaction tasks as objective masking-cost readout.

## 6. Risk stratification
6.1 *Misdiagnosis risk:* Identify clinical features that increase risk of ME/CFS being missed in ND patients (e.g., communication differences, alexithymia).
6.2 *Severity risk:* Test whether ND+ME/CFS patients are overrepresented in severe/housebound groups due to delayed diagnosis.

## 7. Public health / clinical practice
7.1 *Training:* Add ME/CFS-ND overlap module to autism/ADHD clinician education.
7.2 *Care pathways:* Separate baseline-ND assessment from new-PEM assessment in specialist services.

## 8. Pharmacology
8.1 *None identified.* Evidence base too indirect for pharmacological speculation.

## 9. Phenotype / subtype
9.1 *ND-ME/CFS subtype:* Define a possible subtype characterized by high masking burden, social-PEM prominence, and diagnostic delay.
9.2 *Autistic-burnout-overlap subtype:* Patients whose presentation is dominated by exhaustion and social withdrawal — distinguish from primary autistic burnout using PEM and two-day CPET.

## 10. Limitations / critiques
10.1 *Convenience-sample bias:* Kentrou 2024 is a self-selected online sample; prevalence of overshadowing in clinical settings may differ.
10.2 *No direct cohort:* All mechanistic claims are extrapolated; a direct ME/CFS×ND study could falsify the double-burden model.
10.3 *Diagnostic criteria:* Most autism/ADHD studies do not verify ME/CFS criteria, and vice versa.

## 11. Cross-disease translation
11.1 *Long COVID:* ND people with Long COVID may show analogous masking/diagnostic-overshadowing patterns — test whether findings generalize.
11.2 *Fibromyalgia / CSS:* Grant 2025 links camouflaging to central-sensitivity symptoms; explore whether CSS+ND shows similar double burden.

## 12. Patient / caregiver
12.1 *Peer support:* ND-specific ME/CFS peer groups may reduce isolation and validate the layered experience.
12.2 *Caregiver guidance:* Explain to family why "just autism" framing is insufficient when new PEM appears.
