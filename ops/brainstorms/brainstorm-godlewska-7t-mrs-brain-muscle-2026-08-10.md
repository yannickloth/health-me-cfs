## Category 1 — Novel Hypotheses

**1.1** *Cerebral lactate as a "resting energy-deficit probe" that discriminates ME/CFS from psychiatric fatigue.* If ventricular/cingulate lactate elevation is reproducible and absent in MDD and anxiety (Murrough; Natelson), then brain lactate is a candidate *objective* differentiator in the ME/CFS-vs-psychiatric-fatigue confusion that has historically fueled GET/BPS misclassification (Instruction E context — this gives an objective, non-BPS-grounded separator). Evidence: @Murrough2010VentricularLactate @Natelson2017Lactate. Prelim cert: 0.50. Falsifiable: a head-to-head ME/CFS vs MDD 7T MRS will reproduce lactate elevation only in ME/CFS. Consequence: a machine-measured brain signal could help end the "it's all in your head" dismissal by distinguishing ME/CFS from depression on objective chemistry. *origin: brainstorm.*

**1.2** *Exercise-provoked, not resting, lactate is the ME/CFS muscle signature (a "load-gated" metabolic failure).* Brain shows resting lactate elevation; muscle shows none at rest (Godlewska), but dynamic 31P-MRS shows delayed recovery in post-viral fatigue (Naegel). Hypothesis: the muscle bioenergetic defect is latent at rest and unmasked by load — meaning resting metabolite panels systematically miss it. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS @Naegel2023MuscleMetabolism. Prelim cert: 0.55. Falsifiable: dynamic 31P-MRS in ME/CFS will show prolonged τPCr/Vmax reduction post-exercise even with normal resting PCr/lactate. Consequence: diagnostic and endpoint choices should use exercise-provoked muscle MRS, not resting panels. *origin: brainstorm.*

**1.3** *The ME/CFS-versus-long-COVID brain divergence is a molecular "subtype fingerprint" for the post-infectious cluster.* ME (lactate↑), LC (choline↓), GWI (choline↓ + lactate↑), FM (lactate↑) — if these regional MRS patterns are stable, they define neurochemical subtypes within the post-infectious fatigue family rather than one uniform disease. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS @Jones2025GulfWar @Natelson2015milnacipran. Prelim cert: 0.35. Falsifiable: unsupervised clustering of MRS metabolite vectors across ME/LC/GWI/FM will produce disease-predictive clusters. Consequence: reframes ME/CFS research away from a single disease toward a family with separable brain chemistries. *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 2 — Research Directions

**2.1** *Combine 7T MRS with arterial spin labeling (perfusion) in the SAME cohort* to resolve which of 4 competing causes (mitochondrial dysfunction / astrocyte-lactate-shuttle export failure / hypoperfusion-driven anaerobic shift / inflammatory-cell glycolysis) underlies the brain lactate rise. This is the single highest-yield experiment. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS. Prelim cert: 0.60 (feasible, high value). Consequence: would convert the ambiguous lactate finding into a mechanism-directed (and therefore treatment-directed) result. *origin: brainstorm.*

**2.2** *Longitudinal 7T MRS following long COVID patients to test whether the choline↓ signature normalizes or "converts" to an ME/CFS lactate↑ profile.* This tests the progression model directly. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS @Bravi2025BrainMetabolism. Prelim cert: 0.45. Consequence: would tell clinicians whether long COVID is a separate stable state or a gateway to ME/CFS — changing monitoring. *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 3 — Drug Ideas (light, mechanistic-probe framing only)

**3.1** *Use brain lactate as a pharmacodynamic endpoint for existing mitochondrial/metabolic interventions* (DCA as PDK inhibitor; ketone esters; coenzyme-bound CoQ10) rather than only symptom scales. Rationale: MRS lactate gives an objective tissue-level readout of whether a metabolic drug is engaging its target. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS @Natelson2015milnacipran. Prelim cert: 0.40. Falsifiable: a metabolic drug that lowers brain lactate in ME/CFS will correlate with cognitive improvement; a drug that raises it will not. Consequence: gives researchers a target-engagement biomarker to shorten and sharpen metabolic drug trials. (Probe use only — NOT a treatment recommendation; DCA has neurotoxicity risk.) *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 4 — Supplement Ideas

**4.1** *Choline/precursor (citicoline) as an experimental probe for the long COVID dACC choline↓/coagulation link (hypothesis-testing only).* Rationale: if low brain choline causally relates to coagulation/brain fog, raising choline status (citicoline is approved for cognitive/fan purposes in some regions) could be tested mechanistic. Caveat: Godlewska explicitly associative; no human data. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS. Prelim cert: 0.20 (low — associative, no trials). Falsifiable: citicoline raising dACC choline (measured by MRS) will lower coagulation markers and improve brain-fog in long COVID; falsified if no MRS choline change or no marker change. Consequence: a cheap, available supplement could become a testable probe — but only after the association is mechanistically anchored; currently too speculative for any recommendation. *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 8 — Cross-Disease Bridges

**8.1** *Gulf War Illness as a parallel chronic-fatigue reservoir for MRS metabolite predictors.* GWI shows choline↓/lactate↑ like severe chronic fatigue; the two share a "metabolic-stress brain signature". Testing the MRS model in the larger/cleaner GWI veteran cohorts could pre-validate ME/CFS diagnostic models. Evidence: @Jones2025GulfWar. Prelim cert: 0.40. Consequence: leverages a different population to speed up biomarker validation. *origin: brainstorm.*

**8.2** *The rested-null / load-gated muscle pattern as a cross-disease principle for post-viral and neuroimmune fatigue.* ME, LC (COVID), MS all show blunted recovery dynamics (load-gated) despite normal/ambiguous resting measures. A unified "load-gated bioenergetic reserve" concept across these conditions. Evidence: @Naegel2023MuscleMetabolism. Prelim cert: 0.35. Consequence: a shared measurement protocol (dynamic MRS) could compare and stratify across the whole post-viral/neuroimmune fatigue family. *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 9 — Diagnostic / Biomarker Ideas

**9.1** *Multi-region 7T MRS "neurochemical fingerprint" as a stratification biomarker for the post-infectious fatigue family* (ME = lactate-predominant; LC = choline-predominant; GWI = combined; FM = lactate). A standardized regional metabolite vector used to subtype rather than diagnose. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS @Jones2025GulfWar @Natelson2015milnacipran. Prelim cert: 0.35. Consequence: turns MRS from a one-off research curiosity into a subtype-defining tool for patient stratification in trials. *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 10 — Reasons This Mechanism May NOT Be Relevant

**10.1** *Brain lactate/choline changes may be epiphenomenal — downstream of deconditioning, mood, sleep, or attention during scanning, not the core disease process.* Bedbound/deconditioned patients have reduced cerebral perfusion and altered metabolic state; low mood and poor sleep both shift CNS metabolites. If so, MRS measures correlate with severity but are not mechanistic. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS (no perfusion, no severity/activity control). Prelim cert: 0.40 (this is a serious competing explanation). Consequence: if true, MRS lactate is a severity marker, not a mechanism — and metabolic-targeted treatments could fail. *origin: brainstorm.*

**10.2** *The ME-CF vs LC choline difference may reflect medication or diet, not disease* (e.g., different drug exposure, dietary choline intake differing between cohorts) rather than divergent pathophysiology. Evidence: no medication/diet control in the Godlewska cohort. Prelim cert: 0.25. Consequence: a spurious divergence could mislead the field into over-differentiating two conditions that are actually the same. *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 11 — Null-Hypothesis Assessment

**11.1** *If brain lactate has no role in ME/CFS symptoms:* the "mitochondrial/energy-gate" brain-end of the bioenergetic theory weakens (a central pillar). Existing claims (selective energy dysfunction, astrocyte-energy-gate, brain-energy-conservation) would need substantial revision toward vascular or immune-primary models. Conversely, a null for lactate-as-differentiator would undo the MRS-as-diagnostic line. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS @Murrough2010VentricularLactate. Consequence: clarifying whether brain lactate is causal or marker decides between entire treatment families (mitochondrial support vs perfusion/anti-inflammatory vs mobilization). *origin: brainstorm.*

## Category 12 — Evidence-Quality Concerns

**12.1** *7T MRS metabolite quantification uncertainties:* small n (24/25/24), single site, no severity stratification, no perfusion co-measure, cross-sectional (no causation), voxel-placement sensitivity, T2/water-referencing error, and the choline direction heterogeneity across regions/ages/conditions (Mueller cingulate↑; Pajuelo corpus-callosum↑ older; Godlewska dACC↓). The divergence is a single-study, un-replicated-at-7T observation. Evidence: @Godlewska2025MRS @Mueller2020MRS @Pajuelo2024CorpusCallosum. Consequence: the high-profile "ME≠LC" claim needs replication before it changes trial design. *origin: brainstorm.*

**12.2** *Lactate as a biomarker is confounded by arousal/headache/scanning anxiety in patients.* Ventricular/cingulate lactate can rise with hyperventilation, acute stress, or the scanner experience itself — patient emotional state during scanning may drive part of the signal. Evidence: MRS methodology literature (general). Prelim cert: 0.30. Consequence: if unaccounted, lactate differences could partly reflect scan-related arousal rather than resting brain chemistry. *origin: brainstorm.*
