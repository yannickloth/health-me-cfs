# Brainstorm: ADHD-Nrf2 Measurement & Adult-Onset Consensus — Research Directions

**Date:** 2026-08-20
**Origin:** evidence-update integration (adhd-nrf2-adultonset-update)
**Context:** Gurbuzer 2026 first measured serum Nrf2/HO-1 in adult ADHD (both reduced); adult-onset-ADHD consensus moved to a mix (mostly adolescent/missed-childhood). Brainstorm limited to research directions + critical categories (no treatment/intervention ideas — this is a mechanism/biomarker evidence update, not a treatment topic).

## Ideas

| # | Category | Idea | Certainty | Rationale / evidence anchor |
|---|----------|------|-----------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Hypothesis | ME/CFS patients with ADHD comorbidity show reduced PBMC NRF2 nuclear translocation and NQO1 expression relative to ME/CFS without ADHD | 0.35 | Directly extends Gurbuzer's serum finding to the relevant ME/CFS+ADHD population; NQO1/HO-1 targets suppressed is the axis's core prediction; would confirm the mechanism is nuclear-transcriptional, not just circulating protein |
| 2 | Research direction | Prospective birth-cohort re-analysis of adult-onset ADHD with repeated childhood assessments + ME/CFS outcome, independent of the MTA family, to settle whether a distinct adult-onset ADHD subset exists | 0.45 | Addresses the adult-onset consensus's main weakness (single US comparison cohort + self-referential cluster); would resolve whether the acquired-catecholamine residual is real |
| 3 | Research direction | Measure NRF2 target-gene panel (NQO1, HO-1, GCLC) by qPCR in ADHD PBMCs to test whether the serum-protein deficit reflects transcriptional suppression | 0.40 | Distinguishes circulating-protein artifact from true antioxidant-defense deficit; the decisive test for whether Gurbuzer's finding reflects functional NRF2 deficiency |
| 4 | Critical (caveat) | The adult-onset "mix" conclusion rests on a small MTA comparison cohort (n=239) and a self-referential research cluster; population-grade independent replication is missing | n/a | Adversarial-review finding; prevents treating the mix as settled consensus |
| 5 | Critical (method) | The Gurbuzer serum-NRF2 finding may reflect systemic health/medication confounds (adult ADHD often on stimulants, with comorbidity), not ADHD pathophysiology per se | n/a | Serum protein is an imperfect proxy; medication-naive + comorbidity-controlled replication needed |
