## Category 1 — Novel hypotheses (mechanistic, not yet in literature)

### Idea 1.1: Spike dose–response threshold — vaccination is a lower-dose instance of one shared pathway, not a distinct mechanism (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 1
**Preliminary certainty:** 0.30
**Mechanistic rationale:** mRNA vaccination delivers a fixed intramuscular dose of
spike antigen; natural infection delivers a higher, replicating, multi-tissue antigen
load. If the final common pathway — spike persistence → immune dysregulation →
GPCR/anti-nerve autoantibodies → SFN/POTS — is antigen-dose-dependent, then
vaccination is simply a *lower-dose instance* of the same pathway. This would explain,
mechanistically rather than anecdotally, why PVS is rarer and weaker than
post-infectious disease (POTS ~5× lower, SFN 94% vs 79%). It reframes PVS as
dose-graded, not trigger-specific, and unifies F1 (real phenomenon) with F2 (weak
population signal) under one model.
**Evidence link:** @Bhattacharjee2025PVSsignatures (spike persistence in PVS),
@Proal2025Reservoir and @Peluso2024ViralPersistence (spike/viral persistence in Long
COVID), @Kwan2022POTSrisk and @Donadio2025SFN (infection > vaccination),
@Magnus2015influenza (infection HR 2.04 vs vaccine HR 0.97).
**Falsifiable prediction:** PVS spike-persistence magnitude should be lower than
post-infectious spike persistence, and should correlate with symptom severity/incidence
(a dose–response). Falsified if PVS cases show equal-or-greater spike load than
post-infectious cases without correspondingly more disease.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — basic observation; it explains why
vaccination causes the syndrome far less often than infection does.

### Idea 1.2: The PACVS GPCR-autoantibody "signature" is a downstream immune-activation readout, not a PVS-specific cause (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 1
**Preliminary certainty:** 0.35
**Mechanistic rationale:** The Düsseldorf PACVS panel (GPCR receptor antibodies,
AT1-R/alpha-2B) is presented as disease-defining, but GPCR autoantibodies are already
documented in post-infectious ME/CFS (@Sotzny2021, @Loebel2016) and, in one large
ME/CFS antigen-profiling effort, were **not** found elevated (@Germain2025autoantibody,
@Germain2025comprehensiveNull). If GPCR-antibody production is a non-specific readout
of B-cell/immunoglobulin activation common to any immune stressor, then the PACVS
"signature" is a shared biomarker of immune activation, not a PVS-specific mechanism.
This predicts PVS and PASC are serologically indistinguishable — consistent with
@Hanc2026PVScognitive finding PVS ≈ PASC neuropsychologically.
**Evidence link:** @Semmler2023PACVSmarkers, @Mundorf2024PACVS (the panel),
@Sotzny2021, @Loebel2016 (GPCR-AAb in ME/CFS), @Germain2025autoantibody (ME/CFS null),
@Hanc2026PVScognitive (PVS ≈ PASC).
**Falsifiable prediction:** Blinded classification of PVS vs PASC vs ME/CFS by
GPCR-antibody profile should perform no better than chance if these are shared
readouts. Falsified if the panel separates PVS from PASC in an independent cohort at
the claimed ROC-AUC (0.824–0.850).
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — basic observation about what the blood
test actually measures.

### Idea 1.3: EBV reactivation is the shared proximal effector downstream of any trigger (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 1
**Preliminary certainty:** 0.28
**Mechanistic rationale:** @Bhattacharjee2025PVSsignatures reports EBV reactivation
alongside spike persistence in PVS. EBV reactivation is a well-documented feature of
ME/CFS onset and of Long COVID. A novel synthesis: whatever the trigger (vaccine
spike or infection), EBV reactivation — not the trigger itself — may be the proximal
driver of the ME/CFS-like phenotype. Under this model "post-vaccination" and
"post-infectious" syndromes are two routes to the same EBV-driven endpoint, unifying
F3 under a single proximal mechanism rather than a trigger-specific one.
**Evidence link:** @Bhattacharjee2025PVSsignatures (EBV reactivation in PVS),
@Hanc2026PVScognitive (PVS phenotype), existing EBV-ME/CFS reactivation stream in the
corpus (not re-derived here).
**Falsifiable prediction:** EBV reactivation kinetics (serology/RNA) should track
symptom onset and trajectory equally in PVS and post-infectious ME/CFS, independent of
trigger. Falsified if a prospective cohort shows the PVS phenotype without EBV
reactivation.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — basic observation; suggests any trigger
could produce the same illness through a common viral-reactivation step.

## Category 2 — Research directions (resolve key uncertainties)

### Idea 2.1: The decisive prospective nested case–cohort with anti-N serology and a nocebo/information control (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 2
**Preliminary certainty:** n/a (the definitive missing experiment)
**Mechanistic rationale:** @YangWang2026roadmap names the gap: no prospective
controlled PVS cohort with objective outcomes exists. The decisive design must do four
things at once: (1) recruit a large pre-vaccination cohort with baseline symptoms +
biospecimens; (2) serially measure SARS-CoV-2 **anti-N** (nucleocapsid) antibody —
mRNA vaccines elicit only anti-S, so anti-N flags coincident (including silent)
infection, separating infection from vaccine; (3) active symptom surveillance with
objective outcomes (2-day CPET for PEM, tilt-table for POTS, skin biopsy for SFN,
actigraphy); (4) randomize adverse-event-expectation information to quantify the
nocebo component, building on @Jensen2025DanishPVS's unconcerned-vs-concerned finding.
**Evidence link:** @YangWang2026roadmap (the gap), @Jensen2025DanishPVS (nocebo),
@Kwan2022POTSrisk, @Donadio2025SFN (objective outcomes to adopt), @Magnus2015influenza
(prior registry design).
**Falsifiable prediction (discriminating test):** A vaccine-specific PVS signal that
survives when both coincident infection (anti-N) and nocebo (information
randomization) are measured and controlled — or its absence.
**Non-specialist consequence:** This is the study that would settle whether
vaccination itself can cause the syndrome, versus infection or expectation.

### Idea 2.2: Independent blinded replication of the PACVS GPCR-antibody panel with pre-specified thresholds (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 2
**Preliminary certainty:** n/a
**Mechanistic rationale:** The PACVS signature (@Semmler2023PACVSmarkers,
@Mundorf2024PACVS) is single-lab (CellTrend/Düsseldorf), discovery-only, ROC-AUC
0.824–0.850, never externally validated. The decisive experiment is an independent
lab, pre-registered thresholds, samples split across ≥2 labs, with PVS +
post-infectious ME/CFS + healthy controls and blinded classification. This tests both
replicability of the signature and its discrimination of PVS from PASC (Idea 1.2's
question).
**Evidence link:** @Semmler2023PACVSmarkers, @Mundorf2024PACVS (panel to replicate),
@Germain2025autoantibody (independent ME/CFS assay precedent).
**Falsifiable prediction (discriminating test):** Does the panel replicate at the
claimed performance, and does it separate PVS from PASC at all, in an independent
cohort?
**Non-specialist consequence:** Verifies whether the PVS "biomarker" is real before
patients or clinicians rely on it.

### Idea 2.3: Entity-vs-phenocopy test — blinded multi-omics classification of PVS against post-infectious ME/CFS (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 2
**Preliminary certainty:** n/a
**Mechanistic rationale:** The central question — entity (F1) vs phenocopy (F3) — is
answerable only by asking whether PVS cases are distinguishable from matched
post-infectious ME/CFS/Long COVID cases by any independent measurement. Design: PVS +
post-infectious ME/CFS + healthy controls, matched on age/sex/illness-duration/severity,
run through a blinded multi-omics battery (autoantibodies, cytokines, proteomics,
neurophysiology, actigraphy) with a pre-registered classifier. If indistinguishable,
PVS is a phenocopy (F3) and the F1 "distinct entity" claim fails its decisive test.
**Evidence link:** @Krumholz2023PVS, @Hanc2026PVScognitive (PVS phenotype to compare),
@Purpura2026PVSoverlap, @Arlt2024PVSnerveIgG (candidate discriminating analytes).
**Falsifiable prediction (discriminating test):** Above-chance classification accuracy
separating PVS from post-infectious ME/CFS.
**Non-specialist consequence:** Tells patients whether "post-vaccination syndrome" is a
separate disease or the same illness with a different trigger.

## Category 10 — Reasons PVS may NOT be relevant to ME/CFS (MANDATORY)

### Idea 10.1: PVS is substantially a misattribution artifact — coincident (often silent) SARS-CoV-2 infection is the true trigger (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 10
**Preliminary certainty:** 0.42
**Mechanistic rationale:** Infection is the stronger trigger for every PVS feature —
POTS ~5× (@Kwan2022POTSrisk), SFN 94% vs 79% (@Donadio2025SFN), influenza HR 2.04 vs
vaccine HR 0.97 (@Magnus2015influenza). Vaccination occurs amid high circulating
virus, and no PVS cohort serologically excluded SARS-CoV-2 infection. A large share of
"PVS" may therefore be post-infectious ME/CFS in people whose infection was
asymptomatic, with the salient, dated vaccination event attracting the causal
attribution by temporal proximity.
**Evidence link:** @Kwan2022POTSrisk, @Donadio2025SFN, @Magnus2015influenza,
@Feiring2017HPVCFS, @Hviid2020HPVautonomic (the registry nulls).
**Falsifiable prediction (observation that would refute the concern):** Anti-N-
serology-confirmed infection-free PVS cases still develop the syndrome in a
prospective cohort.
**Non-specialist consequence:** Many people labelled "post-vaccination syndrome" may
actually be post-infection, which changes the causal story.

### Idea 10.2: PVS adds no new mechanism to ME/CFS — every reported finding already exists in post-infectious disease (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 10
**Preliminary certainty:** 0.40
**Mechanistic rationale:** Spike persistence, EBV reactivation, GPCR/anti-nerve
autoantibodies, SFN, and POTS are all already documented in post-infectious ME/CFS and
Long COVID. If PVS contributes nothing mechanistically novel, its relevance to ME/CFS
is pedagogical — one more trigger of a known pathway — rather than mechanistic. The
paper would gain an illustrative example, not a new disease mechanism.
**Evidence link:** @Bhattacharjee2025PVSsignatures (spike/EBV), @Arlt2024PVSnerveIgG
(anti-nerve IgG), @Donadio2025SFN (SFN), @Leys2025POTS (POTS), cross-referenced to
existing post-infectious ME/CFS/LC entries (@Proal2025Reservoir, @Sotzny2021).
**Falsifiable prediction (observation that would refute the concern):** A
vaccine-specific mechanism — e.g. an anti-lipid-nanoparticle or adjuvant-specific
signature — found only in PVS and absent from post-infectious disease.
**Non-specialist consequence:** The illness is likely the same whatever triggered it;
the vaccine is one more trigger, not a new disease.

## Category 11 — Null-hypothesis assessment (MANDATORY)

### Idea 11.1: Null — PVS has no distinct role; "PVS" cases are post-infectious ME/CFS with coincidental vaccination history (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 11
**Preliminary certainty:** 0.38 (that the null is true)
**Mechanistic rationale:** Under the null, the F1 phenotype papers
(@Mundorf2024PACVS, @Semmler2023PACVSmarkers, @Krumholz2023PVS, @Hanc2026PVScognitive)
describe post-infectious ME/CFS patients whose vaccination history is coincidental and
prominently recalled. The registry nulls (@Feiring2017HPVCFS, @Hviid2020HPVautonomic,
@Jensen2025DanishPVS, @Magnus2015influenza) become the decisive evidence, not an
anomaly. Papers needing revision: the PACVS cohorts would be re-labelled
"post-infectious ME/CFS (vaccination-coincident)"; the GPCR signature
(@Semmler2023PACVSmarkers/@Mundorf2024PACVS) reinterpreted as a generic
immune-activation marker (Idea 1.2); and the ~0.02% "PVS prevalence" re-expressed as
background ME/CFS incidence.
**Evidence link:** @Feiring2017HPVCFS, @Hviid2020HPVautonomic, @Jensen2025DanishPVS,
@Magnus2015influenza (nulls), @YangWang2026roadmap (correlation-vs-causation critique).
**Falsifiable prediction (evidence to reject the null):** The prospective cohort
(Idea 2.1) or blinded classification (Idea 2.3) demonstrates a vaccine-specific signal
independent of infection and nocebo.
**Non-specialist consequence:** The default view would be that vaccination does not
cause ME/CFS, and "PVS" is a label for coincidental timing.

### Idea 11.2: "Long Vax" as a cautionary example of correlation-with-temporality generating a disease label in the absence of a causal signal (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 11
**Preliminary certainty:** 0.35
**Mechanistic rationale:** If PVS has no distinct role, the "Long Vax" literature
(@Yong2025LongVax, @YangWang2026roadmap) documents how a salient, dated,
socially-contested exposure plus temporal proximity generates a disease label without
a causal signal. Its relevance to ME/CFS extends beyond PVS: it is a cautionary case
for trigger attribution generally, mirroring the psychologization/stigmatization
literature (@Buchner2025Psychologization) and the nocebo finding
(@Jensen2025DanishPVS). It warns that "started after X" is not "caused by X."
**Evidence link:** @Yong2025LongVax, @YangWang2026roadmap, @Jensen2025DanishPVS,
@Buchner2025Psychologization.
**Falsifiable prediction (evidence to reject the concern):** A prospective,
infection-controlled vaccine signal (Idea 2.1) establishes genuine vaccine causation.
**Non-specialist consequence:** It warns against assuming "started after X" means
"caused by X" — relevant to how ME/CFS triggers are interpreted.

## Category 12 — Evidence-quality concerns (MANDATORY)

### Idea 12.1: Selection, referral, and recall bias — PVS cohorts are self-referred registries, not population samples (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 12
**Preliminary certainty:** n/a (validity concern, not a claim to weight)
**Mechanistic rationale:** Yale LISTEN, Düsseldorf PACVS, and similar cohorts recruit
people who self-identify as vaccine-injured — extreme selection and referral bias. No
population-based PVS cohort exists, so the ~0.02% prevalence and the F1 phenotype are
unrepresentative and likely inflated: the phenotype is that of people who already
believe vaccination harmed them. The exposure is also salient, dated, and contested,
making recall and retrospective attribution highly probable (@Jensen2025DanishPVS
shows symptom reporting differs sharply by vaccine concern). These biases are not
captured by the per-paper certainty scores, which discount for tier/population but not
for self-referral into a grievance-defined cohort.
**Evidence link:** @Krumholz2023PVS, @Mundorf2024PACVS (self-referred cohorts),
@Jensen2025DanishPVS (concern-dependent reporting).
**Falsifiable prediction (observation that would refute the concern):** A
population-based, non-self-referred PVS cohort reproduces the phenotype and prevalence.

### Idea 12.2: Residual confounding by coincident infection, unvalidated single-lab measurement, and non-severity-stratified generalizability (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 12
**Preliminary certainty:** n/a (validity concern, not a claim to weight)
**Mechanistic rationale:** Three internal/external-validity threats are not reflected
in the certainty table: (1) **confounding** — no PVS cohort serologically excluded
coincident SARS-CoV-2 infection, the strongest known trigger (Idea 10.1); (2)
**measurement validity** — the PACVS GPCR panel is single-lab, discovery-only, no
pre-specified external thresholds, so its ROC-AUC is optimistically biased, and the
core outcomes (fatigue, brain fog) are self-reported with objective measures only in
subsamples; (3) **generalizability** — PVS cohorts are ambulatory and not
severity-stratified (mild/moderate/severe/very-severe), so applicability to the full
ME/CFS spectrum is unknown. These jointly cap any integration at
speculation/open-question/limitation.
**Evidence link:** @Semmler2023PACVSmarkers, @Mundorf2024PACVS (single-lab panel),
@Donadio2025SFN, @Kwan2022POTSrisk (infection confound), @Hanc2026PVScognitive
(self-report-dominant outcomes).
**Falsifiable prediction (observation that would refute the concern):** An
independently replicated, infection-excluded, severity-stratified PVS cohort with
objective outcomes.
