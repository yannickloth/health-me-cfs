# Brainstorm: Interoceptive Dose-Finding — Deliberate Intermittent Dosing as Rational N-of-1 Experimentation

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Origin:** Kevin Lee clinical observation — patients deliberately intermittent-dosing as interoceptive testing of the hormesis window
**Phase 2 decision:** PARTIAL — limited to categories 1–2 + 10–12

---

## Category 1 — Novel Hypotheses

### 1.1 Interoceptive Accuracy Predicts Pulsing Behaviour (cert 0.25)
Patients with higher interoceptive accuracy (measured by cardiac interoception tasks) will show more deliberate dose-variation behaviour. Reasoning: the allostatic-interoceptive network requires reliable afferent signals to compute dose-response; patients with poor interoceptive accuracy can't distinguish signal from noise and won't pulse deliberately. Falsifiable prediction: interoceptive accuracy scores correlate with deliberate pulsing frequency (r ≥ 0.3, n ≥ 50). Falsified if no correlation.

### 1.2 Interoceptive Hypervigilance Enables Subconscious Dose-Finding (cert 0.15)
ME/CFS patients who are interoceptively hypervigilant (Section @sec:ch15-interoceptive-dissociation) may be running dose-finding algorithms *without conscious awareness* — they skip a dose because they "feel like it," not because they've reasoned about the dose-response window. The interoceptive network is computing the optimal schedule; conscious awareness is epiphenomenal. Falsifiable: administer a cardiac interoception task + survey on dosing strategy. Prediction: patients with high interoceptive accuracy but low self-reported deliberate pulsing → subconscious dose-finding hypothesis supported.

## Category 2 — Research Directions

### 2.1 Qualitative Interview Study — Dosing Decision Narratives (cert n/a, methodological proposal)
Conduct semi-structured interviews with 30 ME/CFS patients who pulse medications. Ask: "Walk me through how you decided to take the drug that way." Probe for: deliberate experimentation, side-effect avoidance, forgetfulness, cost, external advice. Code responses for interoceptive dose-finding vs other narratives. This is the direct falsification test for spec:interoceptive-dose-finding.

### 2.2 Ecological Momentary Assessment of Dosing Decisions (cert n/a)
Deploy a smartphone app that pings patients 4×/day for 4 weeks: "Did you take [medication]?" "Why/why not?" "How do you feel right now (0–10)?" Analyze temporal coupling between symptom reports and dosing decisions to detect dose-response probing patterns without requiring patient to articulate them.

## Category 10 — Reasons This Mechanism May NOT Be Relevant

### 10.1 Side-Effect Avoidance Explains All Observed Pulsing (cert 0.40)
The simplest explanation is that patients skip doses to avoid side effects (sedation, agitation, GI distress), not to probe the hormetic window. This is fully sufficient to explain the behavior — no interoceptive dose-finding needed. The interoceptive hypothesis adds explanatory overhead with no predictive gain.

### 10.2 Forgetfulness, Cost, and Practical Constraints (cert 0.50)
ME/CFS patients have cognitive impairment — forgetting doses is a common and well-documented phenomenon. Cost of medications, pharmacy access, and caregiver availability constrain dosing schedules far more than interoceptive deliberation. The "deliberate pulsing" narrative may romanticize what is actually practical necessity.

## Category 11 — Null Hypothesis Assessment

### 11.1 Null Hypothesis: No Deliberate Interoceptive Component (cert n/a)
If deliberate intermittent dosing is driven entirely by side-effect avoidance, forgetfulness, cost, and external advice — with zero interoceptive experimentation component — then spec:interoceptive-dose-finding is false. The qualitative interview study (2.1) would return <10% of patients reporting interoceptive dose-finding as a reason for pulsing.

### 11.2 What Would Be Lost If This Hypothesis Is False? (cert n/a)
Nothing. The paper's existing framework (paradoxical reactor management, micro-dosing protocols, time-dependent hormesis) covers the pharmacological rationale for intermittent dosing. The interoceptive framing adds a patient-experience interpretation that, if false, removes no existing content.

---

## Category 12 — Evidence Quality Concerns

### 12.1 Conceptual Claim with No Empirical Anchor (cert n/a)
spec:interoceptive-dose-finding (cert 0.20) is constructed entirely from conceptual synthesis of established components — none of which were studied in the context of deliberate intermittent dosing. The certainty reflects the components' evidence, not the claim's. This is a speculation by construction; it could be entirely wrong.

### 12.2 Risk of Romanticizing Patient Behavior (cert n/a)
Framing non-adherence as "rational interoceptive experimentation" risks validating behavior that is genuinely harmful (patients skipping doses of drugs that require steady-state levels, or pulsing in dangerous patterns). The interoceptive framework should not be used to justify skipping doses without clinical supervision.
