## Category 2 — Research directions

| ID | Idea | Certainty | Rationale (one line) | Falsifiable prediction |
|----|------|-----------|----------------------|------------------------|
| R1 | Two-day CPET **plus continuous orthostatic BP/HR and cerebral-CBF monitoring across the post-exertional window** (day 2 onwards) | 0.35 | Tests whether exertion, not tilt, triggers collapse: submaximal exertion reduces CBF `@Rayhan2021SubmaximalPEM`; CBF can dissociate from BP `@Razumovsky2003CerebralHemodynamics` | A defined fraction of patients show a *delayed* near-syncopal BP/CBF dip in the post-exertional window that does NOT occur at baseline tilt |
| R2 | **Crash-diary near-syncope-symptom elicitation** with standardized descriptors (grey-out, pre-syncopal warning, position-dependence, latency after exertion) | 0.40 | No instrument captures the candidate feature; direct 0-hit gap means first step is descriptive measurement | Diary-derived near-syncope episodes map onto OI severity `@Jason2024TiltTable` and onto orthostatic CBF burden `@Novak2026AutonomicPhenotype` if shared-mechanism; they fail to map if distinct |
| R3 | **Within-subject crash-vs-upright comparison** (same patient: symptom + hemodynamic readout in a crash vs on tilt) | 0.35 | Isolates whether the crash phenomenology is position-contingent or exertion-contingent — the crux of H1 vs H3 | Crash episode reproduces the tilt near-syncope signature (same-mechanism); crash episodes occur in supine/postural-neutral subjects with no tilt signature (distinct) |
| R4 | **Longitudinal capture** of near-syncopal crash frequency and severity across illness duration and symptom flares | 0.30 | Whether the feature is stable, waxing/waning, or onset-phase-specific is unmeasured | Crash near-syncope burden tracks illness trajectory (flares) rather than static trait — refutable by flat trajectory |
| R5 | **Qualitative elicitation / phenomenology study** (structured interviews) of the collapse descriptor to establish whether patients reliably distinguish "shutdown" from "dizzy on standing" | 0.40 | The candidate feature has never been systematically named or queried — face validity and linguistic reliability are unmeasured | If descriptors are not reliably separable, the near-syncope category collapses into OI and the named feature is unfounded |
