## Category 1 — Novel hypotheses
1.1 **Dismissal→hopelessness→suicidality pathway (stigma-mediated)** — Medical dismissal and psychiatricization operate as the proximate modifiable driver between severe symptom burden and suicidality/assisted-death requests; the CFS-label stigma effect (Johnson2022) and Denis's own testimony ("Honte à ceux qui psychiatrisent") are its observable face. Cert: 0.30. Falsifiable prediction: cohorts measuring perceived medical dismissal longitudinally will show dose-dependent suicide-ideation gradients independent of symptom severity; if severity alone predicts suicidality with dismissal contributing nothing, H dies. Consequence: if true, clinician-communication interventions become suicide-prevention tools, not etiquette.
