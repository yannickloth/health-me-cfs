## Evidence base (verified)

**Anchor + supportive papers (in bib):**
- Nochi2026REenergizeME (ME/CFS, discounted 0.95, informational) — RCT **protocol only**, 104 female ICC mild–moderate ME/CFS, 1:1 IHHT vs sham, 6 sessions/8 wks, primary SF-36 vitality; objective endpoints: NIRS muscle oxygenation, autonomic (NLT/Valsalva/Sudoscan), CCM, skin biopsy (IENFD/mitochondria/microvasculature), PBMC bioenergetics, oxidative/inflammatory biomarkers. Results due 2029.
- Doehner2024IHHTLongCovid (0.48) — long-COVID open non-randomized pilot, n=145, 6MWT improved 2.8×.
- Zha2024IntermittentHypoxia (0.44) — PASC RCT, n=95, normoxia comparator; **hypoxia-only (IHE), NOT IHHT** (no hyperoxic phase).
- Kapel2025IHHC (0.28) — PCC non-controlled cohort, **overlaps REenergizeME team+device (SANA/HypoxBreath); its +20.5 SF-36 drives the trial power calc; commercial COI.**
- Zhang2023HypoxiaConditioning (0.49) — hypoxia-conditioning hormesis mechanism review; mild hormetic IH = adaptive, excess = harmful (dose-dependent).
- Erdem2026HIF1aOSA (0.33) — pathological chronic IH (OSA) → sustained HIF-1α elevation + harm (harm contrast).
- She2026IHParadigms (0.25) — animal; IH cycle structure/pattern → distinct outcomes even at equal cumulative burden.
- Serebrovska2019IHHTvsIHT (0.33), Bestavashvili2022IHHEmetabolic (0.33), Afina2021IHHTLipidInflammation (0.30, same cohort as Bestavashvili) — other-population safety/physiology.

**Existing corpus links (cross-check, not duplicate):**
- IHHT = controlled ambulatory instantiation of the existing `hypoxia-altitude-hif1a` corpus hypothesis (`spec:hif-pathway-inertia`).
- Distinct from HBOT (ambient-pressure alternating hypoxia+hyperoxia vs pressurized pure oxygen). Existing HBOT entries do not cover IHHT.
- Phase 3 already added `spec:ihht-reenergizeme` (ch31, cert 0.30) + registry row — this brainstorm feeds that speculation environment.

**Gaps (documented, not invented):** zero direct ME/CFS IHHT efficacy studies; no published IHHT long-COVID RCT; no blinded sham-controlled IHHT RCT in any population; no direct HIF-1α×ME/CFS study (0 PubMed hits).

## Category 1 — Testable hypotheses

| # | Idea | Certainty | Rationale / evidence link | Falsifiable prediction | Non-specialist consequence | Category fit |
|---|------|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------|--------------|
| H1 | **IHHT's benefit, if any, is contingent on the presence of the hypoxia-pathology subtype — patients with objective microvascular/oxygen-handling deficit (low NIRS oxygenation, small-fibre neuropathy on Sudoscan/IENFD, reduced CBF) respond; those without it do not (and may worsen).** | 0.35 | The mechanism presumes recurrent mild hypoxic injury from microvascular dysfunction + functional GPCR autoantibodies disrupting O2 delivery (Nochi2026 protocol rationale); REenergizeME's objective endpoints (NIRS, Sudoscan, CCM, IENFD) will capture this subtype (Nochi2026). Zhang2023 frames IHHT as hormetic adaptation — a mechanism that only helps if there is a hypoxia deficit to correct. If no deficit exists, the hormetic stimulus has no substrate. | Baseline NIRS muscle oxygenation / Sudoscan conductance / CBF deficits predict SF-36 vitality response to IHHT but not sham; response is absent in the normal-oxygen-handling subgroup. Falsified if IHHT improves vitality equally regardless of baseline oxygen-handling phenotype. | Only some patients may get a real benefit, and the trial's objective scans may finally identify who — rather than a one-size-fits-all treatment. | Category 1 ✓ |
| H2 | **The operative variable is not IHHT per se but the hypoxic phase — the added hyperoxic restitution confers no incremental benefit over hypoxia-only (IHE) in ME/CFS.** | 0.25 | Zha2024 (hypoxia-only, IHE) already showed 6MWD/fatigue improvement in PASC; the hyperoxic phase is the untested distinguishing feature of IHHT (Zhang2023 hormesis via alternating redox). REenergizeME uses alternating hypoxia+hyperoxia (Nochi2026) but has no hypoxia-only comparator arm — so the hyperoxic component's contribution is unmeasurable in the trial design. | A three-arm design (IHHT vs hypoxia-only vs sham) shows no between-group difference on vitality between IHHT and hypoxia-only, both exceeding sham. Falsified if hyperoxic restitution produces a vitality or NIRS effect hypoxia alone cannot. | The expensive/device-specific hyperoxic phase might be unnecessary — the cheap hypoxia part may carry the whole effect, or none. | Category 1 ✓ |
| H3 | **Hormetic dosing is severity-critical: the adaptive window (Zhang2023) lies within a narrow FiO2/cycle regime, and the mild–moderate REenergizeME cohort sits inside it while severe patients sit outside (too sensitive → PEM), making IHHT inherently non-generalizable.** | 0.30 | Zhang2023: low-dose hormetic IH = adaptive, excess = harmful; She2026: cycle structure determines outcome at equal cumulative burden. REenergizeME excludes bedbound/very-severe (Nochi2026) — so the dose is validated only for the mild–moderate window; PEM>48h is the protocol AE. If severe patients are outside the hormetic window, any dose pushes them into the pathological pole (Erdem2026-style harm). | In a severe/very-severe cohort, IHHT at any tolerable dose either fails to improve or exacerbates PEM (prolonged symptom worsening), while the same protocol helps mild–moderate patients — a step-function, not a graded dose-response. Falsified if a tolerated dose improves severe patients without PEM. | A treatment that is safe for moderately ill people could be harmful for the most severely ill, so it can never simply be rolled out to everyone. | Category 1 ✓ |
| H4 | **IHHT's effect is mediated not by hypoxia tolerance but by placebo/demand — the sham fails to fully mask (airflow/acoustic/SpO2 cues), so the between-group effect is inflated.** | 0.25 | Nochi2026 self-declares "sham may not fully mask"; Kapel2025 (the power-calc source) is non-controlled open-label with a +20.5 SF-36 swing far above placebo (+6.8 assumed). An unbundled placebo component would explain the protocol's optimistic effect assumption without invoking mechanism. | Fully masked IHHT (nasal catheter with imperceptible FiO2, no SpO2 display) produces a between-group vitality difference smaller than the 13.7 assumed, approaching sham. Falsified if the blinded design reproduces the open-label effect. | A big part of the reported benefit could be "feeling it must work," not the oxygen itself — and the trial as designed may not be able to tell. | Category 1 ✓ |
| H5 | **IHHT may paradoxically convert subclinical autonomic stress into PEM by activating sympathetic drive in dysautonomic ME/CFS — the hormetic stimulus is itself a small exertion, and PEM risk scales with autonomic reactivity, not with hypoxic load.** | 0.20 | The protocol documents PEM>48h as the AE (Nochi2026); She2026 shows IH cycle structure drives autonomic outcomes; Zhang2023 warns excess → harm. In dysautonomic ME/CFS (NLT/Valsalva/Sudoscan endpoints in the trial), repeated sympathetic stimulation from alternating O2 could be a metabolic stressor rather than a preconditioner. | In the REenergizeME biomarker subset, patients with the most abnormal baseline autonomic reactivity (NLT/Valsalva) show the most PEM/AE, and transient sympathetic activation post-session predicts sustained symptom worsening. Falsified if AE rate is independent of autonomic reactivity. | For patients with a fragile nervous/autonomic system, the "mild" oxygen training could itself act like exercise and trigger the very crash it is meant to treat. | Category 1 ✓ |

## Category 2 — Research directions

| # | Direction | Certainty | Rationale / evidence link | Falsifiable prediction | Non-specialist consequence | Category fit |
|---|-----------|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------|--------------|
| R1 | **Stratified post-hoc analysis of REenergizeME's objective endpoints to test the hypoxia-pathology-subtype hypothesis (H1): correlate baseline NIRS/Sudoscan/CCM/IENFD with vitality response, not just the group mean.** | n/a (direction) | The trial collects the exact objective markers needed (Nochi2026) but is not powered for subgroups — so the mechanistic subtype question can only be answered as an exploratory post-hoc, which the trial explicitly acknowledges is underpowered. | If baseline oxygen-handling markers do NOT correlate with response, the hypoxia-pathology-subtype model (H1) is falsified at the mechanistic level. | The trial's scans may tell us not just "does it work" but "for which type of patient" — but only if analyzed the right way. | Category 2 ✓ |
| R2 | **A three-arm IHHT vs hypoxia-only vs sham trial to isolate the hyperoxic-phase contribution (H2) — resolving whether IHHT is a distinct intervention or hypoxia-only in disguise.** | n/a (direction) | No published IHHT RCT has a hypoxia-only arm; Zha2024 (IHE, hypoxia-only) and the IHHT cohort (Kapel2025) are different interventions that have never been compared. The hyperoxic restitution is IHHT's only claimed distinguishing mechanism (Zhang2023 alternating redox). | If IHHT and hypoxia-only produce statistically indistinguishable vitality/NIRS effects, the hyperoxic phase is inert for ME/CFS. | Would tell us whether patients need a special dual-gas machine or just a simple low-oxygen protocol — a large cost and access difference. | Category 2 ✓ |
| R3 | **A dedicated blinded IHHT RCT in severe/very-severe ME/CFS with PEM as a primary safety endpoint — the only way to map the hormetic window (H3).** | n/a (direction) | REenergizeME excludes severe patients (Nochi2026), leaving the whole upper-severity range unstudied; Zhang2023's dose-dependence and Erdem2026's harm pole mean severity could flip benefit to harm. No severe-cohort IHHT data exist. | If a severe-cohort trial shows no tolerated dose (all produce PEM>48h or no benefit), the hormetic window does not extend to severe ME/CFS. | Determines whether the treatment can ever reach the most disabled patients or is confined to the mild–moderate. | Category 2 ✓ |
| R4 | **A sham-masking integrity substudy: measure whether participants can guess their allocation and whether guess accuracy predicts response — quantifying the unmasking risk (H4).** | n/a (direction) | Nochi2026 self-declares the sham may not fully mask; Kapel2025 (open-label) drives the optimistic effect assumption. If patients can feel the hypoxic sessions, the blind breaks and the between-group effect is confounded by expectation. | If allocation-guessing accuracy is high and guess-believing-patients show disproportionate improvement, expectation explains a meaningful share of the effect. | Tells us how much of any reported win is genuine physiology versus the power of expecting improvement. | Category 2 ✓ |
| R5 | **Direct HIF-1α pathway interrogation in ME/CFS tissue (the 0-hit gap): measure HIF-1α/EPO/hypoxic-gene induction under a standardized hypoxic challenge, pre/post an IHHT course.** | n/a (direction) | Zero direct HIF-1α×ME/CFS studies exist (0 PubMed hits); the entire mechanism rests on Zhang2023 general-mechanism + the protocol rationale. REenergizeME's PBMC bioenergetics/biomarkers will give indirect readouts but no direct HIF-1α induction measure. A hypoxic-challenge + EPO/hypoxic-gene assay (analogous to the existing `spec:hif-pathway-inertia` EPO-stimulation concept) would test whether the pathway is even inducible in ME/CFS. | If ME/CFS shows blunted EPO/hypoxic-gene induction to a controlled hypoxic challenge vs controls, and IHHT does not restore it, the preconditioning mechanism is not operating as hypothesized. | Tests the core scientific assumption that ME/CFS patients' oxygen-sensing machinery responds to training at all — the foundation of the whole treatment idea. | Category 2 ✓ |

## Category 10 — Reasons the mechanism may NOT be relevant to ME/CFS (critical)

| # | Idea | Rationale / evidence link | What would change the verdict |
|---|------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| C10.1 | **Zero direct ME/CFS efficacy data — the entire positive signal comes from long-COVID/other populations, and the strongest of them (Kapel2025) overlaps the trial team+device with a commercial interest.** | No direct ME/CFS IHHT study exists; the only non-controlled long-COVID cohort (Kapel2025, COI-discounted 0.28) is the power-calc source; the two independent studies (Doehner2024, Zha2024) are non-randomized or hypoxia-only (Zha2024 is IHE, not IHHT). | An independent, blinded, placebo-controlled IHHT RCT in ME/CFS reproduces a vitality/mechanistic effect (REenergizeME 2029, or an independent replication). |
| C10.2 | **The microvascular/O2-delivery mechanism may be wrong for most patients — the hypoxia-deficit substrate may be present only in a minority.** | The protocol's rationale assumes recurrent mild hypoxic injury (Nochi2026), but the objective endpoints (NIRS, Sudoscan, IENFD) are exploratory and not powered; ~1/3 ME/CFS show reduced IENFD, implying most do not. If most patients lack an oxygen-handling deficit, hormetic hypoxia has no substrate (H1). | Baseline oxygen-handling markers predict response (R1), demonstrating the substrate exists in responders. |
| C10.3 | **Placebo/unmasking could explain the entire anticipated effect — sham masking is self-declared uncertain (H4), and the open-label power-calc source inflates the assumed effect.** | Nochi2026 self-declared "sham may not fully mask"; Kapel2025 (non-controlled) swing is far above placebo. No blinded sham-controlled IHHT RCT exists in any population, so the true effect is entirely unknown. | A masked-verified design (R4) reproduces the between-group effect. |
| C10.4 | **The pathological-chronic-IH harm pole (Erdem2026, She2026) could dominate in ME/CFS rather than the hormetic pole — sustained HIF-1α elevation and autonomic harm rather than adaptation.** | Erdem2026 documents sustained HIF-1α elevation + oxidative/inflammatory/cognitive harm in OSA-like chronic IH; She2026 shows cycle structure drives autonomic outcomes. ME/CFS has documented dysautonomia; the hormetic-vs-harmful boundary (Zhang2023) is dose-sensitive and ME/CFS-untested. | Objective AE/biomarker data show IHHT does not push ME/CFS toward the pathological pole (no sustained HIF-1α/oxidative-stress elevation, no sympathetic activation with PEM). |
| C10.5 | **IHHT may be a redundant re-description of the existing hypoxia-altitude/HIF-1α corpus hypothesis — not a distinct mechanism, just an ambulatory delivery of an already-claimed idea (spec:hif-pathway-inertia).** | The protocol itself frames IHHT as the controlled ambulatory instantiation of the altitude/HIF-1α preconditioning hypothesis (Nochi2026, Zhang2023) already in the paper; IHHT-specific biology beyond that corpus is unproven. | A blinded IHHT RCT shows a mechanism (NIRS/autonomic/biomarker change) that hypoxia-altitude preconditioning alone does not predict, i.e., IHHT is not merely re-delivering an existing hypothesis. |

## Category 11 — Null hypothesis assessment

| # | Idea | What would it mean if the mechanism has no role? | Existing ME/CFS claims requiring revision |
|---|------|------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| N11.1 | **Null: IHHT shows no between-group vitality or mechanistic benefit in the REenergizeME RCT.** | The hormetic-adaptation mechanism (spec:ihht-reenergizeme, cert 0.30) is falsified at the efficacy level; IHHT becomes an unproven/unhelpful intervention for ME/CFS. | Would require downgrading or removing the `spec:ihht-reenergizeme` mechanistic-adaptation claim and registry row; the hypoxia-altitude/HIF-1α corpus (spec:hif-pathway-inertia) would need re-examination for whether it predicts a null treatment too. |
| N11.2 | **Null: baseline hypoxia-pathology markers do not predict response — there is no oxygen-handling substrate to correct.** | The microvascular-dysfunction/GPCR-autoantibody→hypoxic-injury→PEM chain that IHHT targets (Nochi2026 rationale) is not the operative mechanism, or applies to too few patients to matter. | Would weaken the hypoxic-injury PEM pathway as a causal driver; the microvascular/autoantibody O2-delivery claims would need to be reframed as minority-subtype or non-causal. |
| N11.3 | **Null: the long-COVID IHHT effect was placebo/allocation-bias — the true effect is nil, and the power-calc basis was inflated by a non-controlled COI-linked cohort.** | The entire antecedent positive signal (Kapel2025, and by extension the trial design) rests on an artifact; IHHT has no real efficacy in post-viral fatigue either. | Would require flagging the Kapel2025-derived power calculation as biased and re-examining whether any long-COVID IHHT claim survives independent blind testing (Doehner2024 is open, Zha2024 is hypoxia-only). |
| N11.4 | **Null: IHHT is effective but the mechanism is generic stress/placebo, not HIF-1α hormesis — no mechanistic biomarker changes despite clinical improvement.** | The treatment might "work" for non-specific reasons (expectation, routine, attention), invalidating the hypoxia-hormesis mechanism specifically while leaving a vague beneficial effect. | Would require re-framing spec:ihht-reenergizeme away from the HIF-1α/hormetic mechanism toward an unexplained non-specific effect; the hypoxia-altitude corpus's mechanistic claim would lose its IHHT validation. |
| N11.5 | **Null: IHHT harms (net negative) — PEM/autonomic exacerbation outweighs any benefit, especially outside the mild–moderate window.** | The hormetic window does not exist for ME/CFS, or sits so narrow that routine dosing lands on the pathological pole (Erdem2026 harm). | Would require a stronger safety caveat on spec:ihht-reenergizeme and any future oxygen-based treatment; would caution against rolling IHHT out to severe/autonomic patients (H3/H5). |

## Category 12 — Evidence quality concerns (not captured by certainty scores)

| # | Concern | Detail / evidence link |
|---|---------|------------------------|
| E12.1 | **Cohort/provenance overlap + commercial COI in the power-calc source — not captured by the anchor's 0.95.** | Kapel2025 shares investigators (Olsen RJ, Stokholm, Foldager, Nochi Z) and device (SANA/HypoxBreath) with REenergizeME; SANA is co-founded/held by the team; its non-controlled +20.5 SF-36 drives the trial's power calculation. The 0.95 certainty scores the protocol design, not this independence/COI issue. A commercial positive-outcome interest is present. |
| E12.2 | **Effect-size assumption derived from a single non-controlled open-label cohort — the 13.7 between-group target is not independently evidenced.** | Power calc uses Kapel2025 (0.28, COI-discounted) with placebo +6.8 assumed; no independent blinded control supports the assumed effect. Type II error risk is self-declared (Nochi2026). A trial powered on an inflated assumption can fail to detect a real smaller effect. |
| E12.3 | **Female-only sample — generalizability to male ME/CFS is untested.** | Nochi2026 enrolls 104 females only; male generalizability is a self-declared limitation. Sex differences in HIF-1α/hypoxic responses are documented in other populations, so the result may not transfer. |
| E12.4 | **Severity selection bias — mild–moderate only, bedbound/very-severe excluded, recruitment from specialist settings.** | Nochi2026 self-declared; the hormetic-window hypothesis (H3) means the excluded severity range may be exactly where the treatment flips from benefit to harm, and it is entirely unstudied. |
| E12.5 | **Sham-masking validity — airflow/acoustic mimicked but O2 saturation cues may break the blind.** | Nochi2026 self-declared "sham may not fully mask." Hypoxic SpO2 drop is perceptible to many; if the blind breaks, the primary between-group endpoint is confounded by expectation (H4). No masking-integrity measure is planned (no R4-style substudy in the protocol). |
| E12.6 | **No measure of the operative mechanism (HIF-1α/hypoxic-gene induction) — the mechanistic claim is untested by the trial's own endpoints.** | The protocol's PBMC bioenergetics/biomarkers are indirect; no direct HIF-1α/EPO/hypoxic-gene induction assay is listed. The R5-style direct interrogation is absent, so a null or positive clinical result cannot cleanly confirm or refute the HIF-1α hormesis mechanism. |
| E12.7 | **Measurement-validity gap for the primary outcome in severe/poor-memory patients and for objective endpoints in a fatigue population.** | SF-36 vitality is self-report (subject to expectation/social-desirability, heightened by the open unmasking risk); d2-R attention and handgrip fatigability are the only performance endpoints; NIRS/Valsalva/Sudoscan are technically demanding and their reliability in ME/CFS is not established as trial-grade. No independent DMC (low-risk self-declared) reduces oversight of these. |

## Tier assignment

- **H1** (hypoxia-pathology-subtype contingency) → Tier 1 — the most defensible, testable framing; it explains why a group-mean may be null while a mechanism exists, and the trial already collects the endpoints (R1). Directly feeds the existing spec:ihht-reenergizeme.
- **H4 / R4** (unmasking/placebo) → Tier 1 — the single largest non-mechanistic threat; self-declared; cheap to test; undermines the primary endpoint if confirmed.
- **H3 / R3** (severity hormetic window) → Tier 2 — safety-critical, bounds generalizability; severe cohort unstudied.
- **H2 / R2** (hypoxic-phase vs hyperoxic-phase) → Tier 2 — determines whether IHHT is a distinct intervention.
- **R5** (direct HIF-1α interrogation) → Tier 2 — fills the 0-hit mechanism gap; tests the foundational assumption.
- **C10–C12** → critical-review entries; NOT integration candidates but MUST gate the current spec:ihht-reenergizeme framing (its 0.30 mechanism claim) and any future IHHT/oxygen-therapy integration.

## Integrate now vs defer

- **Do NOT integrate any efficacy claim.** Anchor is protocol-only; zero ME/CFS results until 2029. Existing spec:ihht-reenergizeme (cert 0.30) already correctly frames as research-stage speculation; nothing here changes that.
- **Do surface the mechanistic-refinement framing (H1, H2, H3, H5 + C10–C12)** as open questions/limitations around the existing spec — subject to the 0.45 certainty cap and speculation-only environment (no hypothesis-box).
- **Defer** all category 3–9 ideas (drug/supplement/intervention/combination/model/cross-disease/biomarker) — PARTIAL decision explicitly defers these on weak evidence; no new category-3–9 idea is generated here.
- **R1–R5 and the critical entries** are research-direction backlog for future cycles once REenergizeME 2029 (or replication) results exist.
