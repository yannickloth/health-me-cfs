## Category 1 — Novel hypotheses (mechanistic, not yet in literature)

### Idea 1.1: VZV age-at-primary-infection as a ME/CFS subtype discriminator (childhood-varicella signature) (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 1
**Certainty:** 0.20
**Rationale:** The pediatric signal is the single most concrete asymmetry in the
VZV evidence: asymptomatic/rash-less VZV reactivation drives arterial ischemic
stroke in children (Fullerton 0.56), and up to 1/3 of pediatric AIS is VZV-related
(Bubak 0.64). VZV differs from the other panel members in that primary infection
(chickenpox) is nearly universal and childhood-timed. The paper already holds an
adolescent-EBV-window hypothesis (`hyp:ebv-adolescence`); a parallel age-at-primary-
VZV-infection partition is not present. If age-at-primary-VZV or a childhood AIS /
severe-varicella history interacts with the adolescent-EBV-trigger subtype, it
would define a small VZV-neurovascular ME/CFS subset with a distinctive symptom
pattern (one-sided pain, cranial-nerve or vascular complaints). Certainty low: no
ME/CFS cohort records chickenpox age or VZV-complication history; the pediatric AIS
signal is general-population and the ME/CFS application is entirely speculative.
Evidence link: Fullerton 0.56, Bubak 0.64; mirrors structure of hyp:ebv-adolescence
but is untested.
**Falsifiable prediction:** In a ME/CFS cohort with documented childhood varicella
age and VZV-related childhood complications (e.g. history of childhood chickenpox
with neurologic complication), patients with early/severe primary VZV or a
VZV-related childhood event show higher frequency of one-sided neuropathic,
cranial, or vascular-type symptoms and of the anti-VZV-dUTPase serologic member of
the poly-herpesvirus pattern. Falsified if VZV-history partitions show no clinical
or serologic distinction.
**Non-specialist consequence:** If confirmed, a routine childhood-chickenpox
history question would flag a small VZV-related ME/CFS subset — but the effect is
currently unproven and likely minor, so the practical change today is nil.

### Idea 1.2: "Rash-less zoster sine herpete as a captured amplifier, not a first driver" (cascade-position hypothesis) (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 1
**Certainty:** 0.15
**Rationale:** The poly-herpesvirus ALR cascade (`spec:poly-herpesvirus-alr`)
proposes sequential reactivation following an index herpesvirus. VZV's distinct
feature is ganglionic neurotropism plus a neurovascular effector arm (arterial
inflammation → vasculopathy → stroke; Livieratos 0.38). A novel mechanistic claim:
when VZV is the *later* member to reactivate in the cascade, its neurovascular arm
is what converts diffuse immune activation into *focal* neurovascular damage — a
qualitative severity step the non-vascular panel members (EBV, HHV-6) cannot
produce at the same anatomical site. This reframes VZV as a cascade-positioned
amplifier of cerebrovascular/neuroinflammatory disease rather than an initiator,
which is consistent with the cause-vs-consequence caveat (Li 0.38). Certainty low:
the neurovascular arm is general-population, and no ME/CFS study demonstrates VZV-
specific arterial or cerebral-vascular involvement (the intersection is mechanistic
only, per Phase-1 mapping).
**Falsifiable prediction:** If VZV adds a neurovascular amplifier step, then ME/CFS
patients with the VZV serologic member plus a documented neurovascular finding
(e.g. cerebral hypoperfusion, endothelial dysfunction, or focal arteriopathy on
imaging) should show those findings lateralized or anatomically correlated with a
documented prior VZV complication, and such patients should cluster at higher
disease severity. Falsified if VZV-marker-positive patients show no excess of
focal/neurovascular over diffuse findings relative to VZV-marker-negative patients.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — the claim is untested and would only
matter if it separated a focal-vascular ME/CFS subgroup, which is not yet
demonstrated.

### Idea 1.3: VZV ORF8 dUTPase confers a *distinct* neuromodulatory signal separable from EBV BLLF3 / HHV-6 U45 (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 1
**Certainty:** 0.20
**Rationale:** The dUTPase section already documents that all three viruses encode
dUTPase homologs with immunomodulatory/neuromodulatory functions (BLLF3, U45,
ORF8). What is NOT established is whether VZV's ORF8 contributes a signal
*distinct* from the EBV/HHV-6 members (the three are currently treated as
contributing to one shared co-reactivation pattern, 72.5%). Because VZV is
ganglion-resident (dorsal root + autonomic ganglia; Shapiro 0.30) whereas EBV is
B-cell-resident and HHV-6 is T-cell/neuronal-resident, an ORF8-specific antibody
signal localized to VZV (rather than co-expressed) could carry distinct anatomical
(neuro/autonomic) meaning. Certainty moderate-low: the VZV-specific versus shared
contribution has not been isolated; Halpin 0.55 shows VZV participates but does not
parse ORF8-specificity against the other two.
**Falsifiable prediction:** Antibody-panel deconvolution (anti-ORF8 vs anti-BLLF3 vs
anti-U45) in ME/CFS will show that isolated VZV-ORF8 elevation (without the other
two) predicts sensory/autonomic-predominant symptoms, whereas isolated EBV/HHV-6
elevation does not. Falsified if VZV-ORF8 elevations never occur independently and
always track the shared poly-herpesvirus pattern, or if ORF8-specific signal shows
no autonomic/sensory correlation.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — it is a fine-grained serologic
hypothesis with no current patient impact unless it isolates a VZV-autonomic
subgroup, which is unproven.

### Idea 1.4: One-sided neuropathic pain in ME/CFS as an undiagnosed-zoster-sine-herpete diagnostic-signal hypothesis (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 1
**Certainty:** 0.22
**Rationale:** The Phase-3 spec (`spec:rashless-vzv-mecfs`, cert 0.30) already
names rash-less VZV generally. The genuinely novel, narrower, and more actionable
claim is a *diagnostic-signal* hypothesis: that some ME/CFS patients whose pain is
currently attributed to "unexplained" or small-fiber neuropathy actually have
undiagnosed, rash-less zoster sine herpete (dermatomal/one-sided neuropathic pain
with normal skin and imaging). This is clinically distinct from the systemic
reactivation claim because it is a *mimicry* hypothesis — VZV disease masquerading
as an ME/CFS symptom. Rationale: zoster sine herpete presents as one-sided burning
pain with normal imaging and no rash (Bubak 0.64); ME/CFS pain is commonly
neuropathic and bilateral-attributed (small-fiber, ch09 sec-03). A subset-level
mimicry is testable and, unlike the causal claim, does not require VZV to drive
ME/CFS — only to co-occur and be missed. Certainty moderate-low because direct ME/CFS
data are absent; anchored to Bubak 0.64 (clinical entity real) but the ME/CFS
co-occurrence is unmeasured.
**Falsifiable prediction:** In ME/CFS patients with unilateral/dermatomal neuropathic
pain, VZV PCR (skin, blood) or paired anti-VZV-dUTPase timing to pain onset will
confirm zoster sine herpete in a non-trivial fraction (above the general zoster
sine herpete background rate); and those confirmed cases will respond to a
VZV-targeted antiviral course with pain improvement. Falsified if PCR is
universally negative and no case shows temporal/pain response consistent with VZV.
**Non-specialist consequence:** If validated, a small number of ME/CFS patients with
one-sided neuropathic pain would get a treatable (antiviral-responsive) explanation
instead of being told the pain is "unexplained" — but the fraction is unknown and
may be negligible.

## Category 2 — Research directions

### Idea 2.1: VZV saliva/blood PCR probe timed to PEM — but designed around the once-in-a-lifetime constraint (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 2
**Certainty:** n/a
**Rationale:** The digest asks for a longitudinal VZV probe parallel to the HSV
probe. The ch08 HSV section already states VZV is once-in-a-lifetime, which is why
HSV (not VZV) is the panel's longitudinal probe. A VZV probe therefore cannot copy
the HSV design (frequent visible events). Instead the informative VZV question is
*subclinical viral shedding* (saliva/blood VZV DNA) rather than clinical events,
since rash-less reactivation is by definition not clinically obvious. Design:
paired to the existing HSV PEM-probe cohort, sample saliva/blood VZV DNA at
PEM-peak and PEM-free windows, not continuous diaries. The added value over the HSV
probe is the poly-herpesvirus *comparison*: does VZV shed subclinically in the same
subset as HSV/EBV/HHV-6, or is VZV the odd one out (supporting the cascade-position
idea 1.2)? Do NOT duplicate the HSV visible-lesion diary.
**Falsifiable prediction:** VZV DNA shows no subclinical shedding at PEM-peak beyond
the poly-herpesvirus co-reactivation baseline (already the falsifier of
spec:rashless-vzv-mecfs); OR VZV shedding is present only in a subset that is also
HSV/EBV-positive (i.e., VZV adds no independent temporal signal).
**Non-specialist consequence:** None directly — a research measurement to resolve
whether VZV is an independent reactivation signal or only a co-passenger, with no
immediate patient action.

### Idea 2.2: VZV neurovascular imaging probe — testable only if a focal ME/CFS prediction exists (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 2
**Certainty:** n/a
**Rationale:** Cross-disease VZV-vasculopathy content is mechanistic only (per
Phase-1 mapping — no ME/CFS intersection paper). A research direction is only
warranted if a *focal* ME/CFS prediction exists. Idea 1.2 supplies one: a
VZV-marker-positive ME/CFS subset should show focal arterial/cerebral-vascular
findings anatomically consistent with VZV, not merely diffuse hypoperfusion. If the
paper's existing cerebral-vasculature findings (CBF, endothelial dysfunction) are
diffuse and non-focal across the whole ME/CFS cohort, then there is no a priori
VZV-focal signal to chase and this direction should NOT proceed until 1.2's
serologic partition has been shown. Design hinges on a prior serologic screen; do
not run imaging before the subset is identified.
**Falsifiable prediction:** No imaging design is justified until the 1.2 serologic
partition is demonstrated; the research direction is itself contingent and should
be gated on that result.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None — this is a gated, contingent research plan,
not a patient-facing finding.

### Idea 2.3: Retrospective natural-experiment analysis of the shingles-vaccine era (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 2
**Certainty:** n/a
**Rationale:** The digest proposes the zoster-vaccination natural experiment (did
zoster-vaccination cohorts change post-herpetic-fatigue / ME/CFS incidence?). This
is a legitimate research direction but must be scoped honestly: (a) shingles
vaccine protects against VZV reactivation in the *older adult* population where it
is administered, which is NOT the typical younger post-infectious-onset ME/CFS
population (framing note: relevance is mechanistic, not epidemiological); (b) if VZV
reactivation in ME/CFS is a *consequence* of immune disturbance (Li 0.38), then
vaccine-induced prevention of reactivation would not remove the underlying immune
dysfunction and would NOT be expected to reduce ME/CFS incidence — so the natural
experiment is, on the consequence framing, predicted to be *null*. This makes it a
useful discriminating test only if a causal-VZV subset exists. It is also
confounded by vaccination-timing relative to infection and by indication bias.
**Falsifiable prediction:** In vaccinated older-adult cohorts, if VZV were an
independent causal ME/CFS trigger, post-herpetic-fatigue and incident ME/CFS should
decline post-vaccination-rollout; the consequence framing predicts NO decline.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — retrospective, confounded, and likely
null under the dominant consequence interpretation.

## Category 3 — Drug / medication ideas (antiviral territory already covered; novel angles only)

### Idea 3.1: Immune-reconstitution *timing* relative to VZV reactivation as a study design, not a new drug (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 3
**Certainty:** n/a
**Rationale:** No new antiviral is warranted (valacyclovir/valganciclovir already in
ch33/ch28; vaccine already covered). The novel, non-duplicative angle is
immune-reconstitution *timing*: because VZV reactivation is best read as a
consequence of a lowered immune threshold (Li 0.38), the intervention question is
not "which antiviral" but "does restoring immune competence *before* a reactivation
event prevent it" — i.e., prophylaxis-timed-to-risk rather than reaction-to-symptom.
This reframes antiviral/vaccine content as a *mechanistic probe of threshold* rather
than a therapy. Any patient-facing proposal is off-label/speculative and must carry
a safety flag; this is a study-design idea only.
**Falsifiable prediction:** A threshold model predicts that immune-reconstitution
interventions given when a VZV (or panel) reactivation marker is rising would reduce
the transition to clinical/serologic reactivation, whereas the same intervention
given reactively after symptoms would not. Falsified if timing is irrelevant to
outcome.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — purely a research-design framing; no
medication is being recommended to patients.

### Idea 3.2: Shingles-vaccine-as-therapeutic is speculative and off-label — flag, do not recommend (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 3
**Certainty:** 0.05 (inverse — flagging why NOT to pursue)
**Rationale:** The digest explicitly flags the shingles-vaccine-as-therapeutic idea
in ME/CFS as speculative and off-label. In ME/CFS the underlying immune disturbance
persists (per the consequence framing), so vaccinating to prevent reactivation does
not address the driver and is not a mechanism-matched therapy; and giving a live or
recombinant vaccine to an immunologically dysregulated ME/CFS population carries
safety uncertainty. The honest position is that this idea should be recorded as a
reason AGAINST pursuing vaccine-as-treatment, and the only defensible vaccine role
is the population-level natural experiment (2.3). Safety flag: any vaccine-based
trial in ME/CFS requires an explicit safety gate.
**Falsifiable prediction:** (negative) vaccine-as-therapy shows no benefit because
reactivation is downstream; the idea is structurally unlikely to rescue a causal
role.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None — this flags an off-label idea as not to be
pursued, protecting patients from unproven treatment.

## Category 5 — Non-pharmacological

### Idea 5.1: Clinical recognition that one-sided neuropathic pain in ME/CFS warrants a VZV-directed work-up (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 5
**Certainty:** 0.20
**Rationale:** Distinct from the systemic causal claim, the highest practical-value
non-pharmacological output is a *diagnostic-awareness* change: when an ME/CFS
patient presents with unilateral/dermatomal neuropathic pain, normal skin, and
puzzling imaging, the differential should include rash-less zoster sine herpete
(Bubak 0.64). This is non-pharmacological in the sense that it changes evaluation,
not treatment — it does not assert VZV causes ME/CFS, only that VZV disease can be
missed and should be ruled in/out. Anchored to Bubak 0.64 (entity real) and the
ch08 diagnostic-awareness note.
**Falsifiable prediction:** Educational/recognition intervention increases
appropriate VZV testing in ME/CFS patients with unilateral neuropathic pain; the
yield of positive cases determines value. Falsified if testing in this symptom
subgroup yields no VZV cases above background.
**Non-specialist consequence:** A small number of patients with one-sided nerve pain
would be tested for a treatable viral cause they might otherwise miss — though the
test yield in ME/CFS is currently unknown.

## Category 7 — Mathematical model extensions

### Idea 7.1: Immune-threshold reactivation model with virus-occupancy (rate) rather than virus-identity (cause) (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 7
**Certainty:** n/a
**Rationale:** The paper's causal-hierarchy / reactivation framework (consequence vs
cause vs bidirectional; `hyp:viral-reactivation-consequence/cause/bidirectional`)
treats each virus as a potential driver. VZV's lesson (Li 0.38: reactivation as
threshold-lowering consequence) motivates a model where reactivation probability is
a continuous function of immune-competence state shared across the whole panel, not
a per-virus causal term. Extend the causal model with a latent "immune-threshold"
variable that gates all three members (EBV/HHV-6/VZV), and let VZV be the member
with the highest *neurovascular conversion rate* conditional on reactivation
(ganglion + arterial arm). This makes a quantitative prediction: individual
reactivation order and frequency are explained by shared threshold + per-virus
occupancy, not by any single virus being causal.
**Falsifiable prediction:** A threshold-plus-occupancy model fits longitudinal
poly-herpesvirus reactivation data better than independent per-virus driver models;
and the fitted VZV neurovascular conversion parameter predicts which reactivation
events carry focal/neurovascular features.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None — a modeling abstraction that sharpens the
cause-vs-consequence debate; no patient impact.

## Category 8 — Cross-disease bridges

### Idea 8.1: VZV childhood-AIS focal-cerebral-arteriopathy as a bridge to ME/CFS cerebral-vasculature — only via a focal prediction (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 8
**Certainty:** 0.15
**Rationale:** The only defensible cross-disease bridge (per framing note 5) is
mechanistic and requires a testable focal prediction (idea 1.2). Childhood AIS
shows that asymptomatic VZV reactivation produces *focal cerebral arteriopathy*
(Fullerton 0.56). The ME/CFS cerebral-vasculature literature (CBF, endothelial
dysfunction) is largely diffuse. The bridge is valid only as: does a VZV-marker-
positive ME/CFS subset show *focal* arteriopathy-like findings rather than diffuse
hypoperfusion? Without that prior serologic partition the bridge is epidemiological
mismatch (pediatric-stroke population ≠ younger post-infectious ME/CFS) and should
not be asserted.
**Falsifiable prediction:** VZV-marker-positive ME/CFS patients show focal
arterial/cerebral-vascular imaging findings; VZV-marker-negative and
poly-herpesvirus-negative patients do not. Falsified if findings are uniformly
diffuse across all subgroups.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None today — a mechanistic bridge contingent on a
focal subset being found, not a current clinical fact.

## Category 9 — Diagnostic / biomarker ideas

### Idea 9.1: Paired anti-VZV-dUTPase ORF8 IgG as a *subset-defining* biomarker independent of static VZV titre (origin: brainstorm)
**Category:** 9
**Certainty:** 0.25
**Rationale:** Static VZV serology does not discriminate ME/CFS (Koelle2002,
Buchwald1996, Domingues2023 — the paper's established null). The informative signal
per the existing ALR/dUTPase framework is the *temporal/reactivation-pattern* not
the titre. A novel diagnostic angle consistent with that framework: track
anti-VZV-ORF8 IgG *longitudinally* (paired rise), specifically to time against PEM,
rather than cross-sectionally. This is the antibody-marker complement to the PCR
probe (2.1) and directly extends the existing dUTPase content without asserting
diagnostic value for static titre. Certainty moderate-low because it reuses the
dUTPase antibody platform but in a longitudinal, subset-defining role not yet tested.
**Falsifiable prediction:** Longitudinal anti-ORF8 IgG rises (not absolute titre)
cluster with PEM and with one-sided neuropathic/autonomic symptom flares; a static
single-timepoint ORF8 titre does not discriminate. Falsified if paired ORF8 rises
carry no more signal than static titre.
**Non-specialist consequence:** If validated it would give a blood marker for a
reactivation-related subset, but today it is an untested assay refinement with no
routine use.

## Category 10 — Reasons this mechanism may NOT be relevant to ME/CFS (MANDATORY)

### Idea 10.1: Static and longitudinal VZV signals may be entirely explained by the poly-herpesvirus co-reactivation pattern (no VZV-specific role) (origin: brainstorm)
**Certainty:** 0.40 (that VZV adds no independent signal beyond the shared pattern)
**Rationale:** The 72.5% co-expression of EBV+HHV-6+VZV (Palomo 2026) and the
lab-lineage overlap of Halpin 2017 with already-integrated Palomo/Ariza mean most
of the VZV serologic "signal" may simply be one member of a single surveillance
failure. If VZV never rises independently of the other two, there is no
VZV-specific mechanism in ME/CFS — only a shared poly-herpesvirus phenomenon in
which VZV is an interchangeable member. This is the strongest reason to doubt VZV
specificity and directly limits ideas 1.2/1.3/8.1 which depend on a separable VZV
signal.

### Idea 10.2: VZV is a consequence, not a cause — the threshold model removes any VZV-specific driver (origin: brainstorm)
**Certainty:** 0.38 (Li 2026, that reactivation is consequence of lowered immune threshold)
**Rationale:** If VZV reactivation in post-infectious states is downstream of a
lowered immune threshold (Li 0.38), then treating or explaining ME/CFS via VZV
misattributes the direction. The immune dysfunction precedes and permits VZV
reactivation; VZV is a readout, not a driver. This undermines causal and therapeutic
VZV ideas and aligns with the paper's existing cause-vs-consequence debate.

### Idea 10.3: The neurovascular evidence comes from a non-comparable population (children and older adults) (origin: brainstorm)
**Certainty:** 0.56 (Fullerton) for the pediatric finding; relevance to ME/CFS low
**Rationale:** The strongest VZV mechanisms (stroke in children; stroke/dementia in
older adults) arise in populations with immature or senescent immune systems and
structural vascular vulnerability. ME/CFS is typically younger with a post-infectious
trigger. Framing note 5 states the relevance is mechanistic, not epidemiological.
The pediatric-AIS frequency (9.8% vs 3.1%, Fullerton 0.56) is a *vaccinated,
neurologically-injured* population, not an unselected ME/CFS population — the VZV
fraction may not generalize at all. This caps the whole neurovascular arm.

### Idea 10.4: No direct empirical link exists between rash-less VZV and ME/CFS (zero-cohort gap) (origin: brainstorm)
**Certainty:** (structural) — 0 direct ME/CFS cohort links rash-less VZV to ME/CFS
**Rationale:** The only ME/CFS-direct VZV proposal is a 2009 hypothesis essay
(Shapiro 0.30), and the only ME/CFS-primary serology (Halpin 0.55) is
cohort-overlapped with existing corpus. E1/R3 searches returned zero hits linking
rash-less VZV / zoster sine herpete to ME/CFS or post-viral fatigue. Absent any
empirical ME/CFS cohort, the entire mechanism may simply not operate in this
population; the paper must hold it at hypothesis/speculation level (already done in
Phase 3).

### Idea 10.5: If VZV were a major treatable cause, static anti-VZV measures and antiviral trials would likely have shown it (origin: brainstorm)
**Certainty:** 0.30
**Rationale:** VZV has been studied serologically in ME/CFS for decades with
repeated nulls (Koelle2002, Buchwald1996, Domingues2023). Antiviral trials already
in the paper show limited/heterogeneous benefit. If a large VZV-treatable subset
existed, decades of serologic study and broad-spectrum antiviral use (valacyclovir
covers VZV) would plausibly have surfaced a signal. The absence of one is weak
negative evidence that VZV is not a major independent ME/CFS driver.

## Category 11 — Null hypothesis assessment (MANDATORY)

### Idea 11.1: Null hypothesis — VZV contributes nothing specific beyond the shared poly-herpesvirus and consequence framings (origin: brainstorm)
**Certainty:** 0.35 (that the null is true)
**Rationale:** The most parsimonious null is: (a) VZV's presence in ME/CFS is a
member of the shared poly-herpesvirus co-reactivation (Palomo 72.5%), (b) its
reactivation is a consequence of immune disturbance (Li 0.38), and (c) no VZV-
specific mechanism, subset, or treatment response exists in ME/CFS. Under this null:
static VZV serology is non-discriminative (already shown), longitudinal VZV signal
tracks the shared panel (never independent), the neurovascular findings do not
generalize from pediatric/older populations, and no VZV-separable clinical subset
exists.
**Assessment:** The null is well-supported and is the *default* position. It is not
falsified by any current evidence. To reject it requires exactly the designs this
backlog proposes (subset-defining longitudinal ORF8/ORF8 PCR probes 2.1/9.1, focal-
vascular partition 1.2/8.1, and the vaccine natural experiment 2.3). Per Phase-2,
the ME/CFS-causal claim stays at #speculation (0.30) precisely because the null is
not yet rejected. The brainstorm ideas must all be read as attempts to *reject*
this null, not as affirmations of a VZV role.

### Idea 11.2: Minimum design required to reject the null (origin: brainstorm)
**Certainty:** n/a (methodological)
**Rationale:** Because cross-sectional serology is already null, only a
*longitudinal, subset-defining, multi-compartment* design can test the null: paired
saliva/blood VZV DNA + anti-ORF8 IgG timed to PEM, in a cohort already carrying the
HSV probe, comparing VZV-independent versus shared-panel signals, and correlating
any independent VZV signal with one-sided neuropathic/autonomic or focal-vascular
features. Rejecting the null requires (a) VZV signal independent of the shared
panel AND (b) a clinical correlate absent in VZV-negative patients. Absent both, the
null stands and VZV content stays as a documented-but-speculative member.
**Non-specialist consequence:** None — defines what evidence would be needed before
VZV could be treated as more than speculative in ME/CFS.

## Category 12 — Evidence quality concerns (MANDATORY)

### Idea 12.1: Direct ME/CFS evidence is near-absent; only Shapiro (hypothesis essay) is ME/CFS-direct and causal (origin: brainstorm)
**Rationale:** Discounted certainties mask the population gap: Bubak 0.64 and
Fullerton 0.56 are general-population; Karachaliou 0.55 and Li 0.38 are long-COVID;
Livieratos 0.38 is general mechanism. The only ME/CFS-direct causal source is
Shapiro 0.30 (a single-author 2009 hypothesis essay, non-empirical). Halpin 0.55 is
ME/CFS-primary but (a) cohort-overlapped with already-integrated Palomo/Ariza
(same lab lineage, count once) and (b) shows VZV participation, not VZV-specific
causality. The Phase-2 "PROCEED" gate was met on *general/long-COVID weight*, not
on ME/CFS-direct evidence — a fact that must govern how strongly any idea here is
written into the paper. This is the single most important quality caveat.

### Idea 12.2: General-population → ME/CFS and long-COVID → ME/CFS translation gaps are unbridged (origin: brainstorm)
**Rationale:** The neurovascular and serologic findings come from stroke cohorts,
older adults, and post-COVID populations. Framing note 5 states ME/CFS relevance is
mechanistic, not epidemiological. No study bridges pediatric-stroke/long-COVID
findings to a younger post-infectious ME/CFS population. Any idea that imports these
general findings into ME/CFS must carry an explicit translation-gap annotation
(Phase 3 spec already does). Ideas 1.2, 2.2, 8.1 depend on this gap being bridged,
which it is not.

### Idea 12.3: Cohort-overlap and single-lineage risk inflate the dUTPase/VZV serology (origin: brainstorm)
**Rationale:** Halpin 2017 shares the Ariza/Williams + Klimas/Fletcher lineage with
Palomo 2026 / Ariza 2025 (already integrated). The anti-dUTPase findings across
"multiple cohorts" are substantially one research group (Ariza lab) — the paper's
own `lim:dUTPase-evidence-base` flag already notes independent replication is
pending. Ideas built on anti-ORF8 / anti-dUTPase serology (1.3, 9.1) inherit this
single-lineage risk and cannot be trusted until an independent group replicates the
dUTPase platform.

### Idea 12.4: Publication/recency bias — Bubak 2026 (the driving source) is not yet PubMed-indexed (origin: brainstorm)
**Rationale:** Bubak 2026 (NRDP, the authoritative scaffold at 0.64) was published
2026-09-03 and not yet PubMed-indexed (verified via Crossref at Phase 1). The
highest-certainty source is the newest and least independently vetted. Its
up-to-one-third pediatric-stroke figure and zoster-sine-herpete framing should be
treated as a strong review claim pending broader indexing and citation.

### Idea 12.5: Cause-vs-consequence circularity risk in "reactivation as consequence of low threshold" (origin: brainstorm)
**Rationale:** Li 0.38 (consequence framing) and the poly-herpesvirus surveillance-
failure model both risk circular reasoning: immune dysfunction "causes" reactivation
and reactivation "causes" immune dysfunction. Without longitudinal intervention
data separating them, VZV reactivation can be cited to support either direction.
This ambiguity must prevent any idea from asserting a directional VZV claim as
established (ideas 1.2, 3.1, 7.1 are direction-sensitive and must stay tentative).
