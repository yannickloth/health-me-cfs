## Category 1 — Novel hypotheses

**1.1 Attention is an energy-budget read-out, not a module.** *Mechanistic rationale:* if ADHD performance tracks central-executive load and arousal recruitment (movement, stimulant) rather than a fixed attention capacity @Sarver2015HyperactivityCompensatory @Orban2018InattentiveBehaviorWM, then "attention" in ME/CFS is best modelled as the *observable* of executive energy state. *Evidence link:* Orban's WM mediation; Kofler's load-locked activity. *Certainty: 0.35.* *Falsifiable prediction:* objective attention measures in ME/CFS should covary with an independent energetic index (MRS PCr:ATP, creatine) more than with any attention-specific manipulation. *Non-specialist consequence:* if true, "pay attention harder" is the wrong instruction in ME/CFS — managing the energy budget is the lever. `origin: brainstorm`

**1.2 Movement shows a recruitment threshold, not a dose-response.** *Rationale:* activity steps up to a WM condition and then stays flat rather than scaling with load @Dekkers2021CognitiveDemandActivity — a threshold/switch, not a graded dial. *Evidence link:* Dekkers 2021 "increase … similar for all experimental conditions". *Certainty: 0.30.* *Falsifiable prediction:* a finer-grained load series should show a step, not a slope, in activity. *Consequence:* a switch-like recruitment would mean small cognitive overshoots can trigger disproportionate movement/effort — relevant to pacing thresholds. `origin: brainstorm`

**1.3 The supply-side mirror is directly testable.** *Rationale:* the ADHD evidence predicts a *movement-linked WM gain*; the supply-side reading predicts its *absence* in ME/CFS. *Evidence link:* @Wiegers2024StimulantActivityWM (medication×activity interaction). *Certainty: n/a (research direction).* *Falsifiable prediction:* an ME/CFS WM task with a movement-opportunity arm shows no activity-linked WM gain. *Consequence:* one small experiment could falsify the whole bridge — cheap and decisive. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 2 — Research directions

**2.1 Arousal-response phenotype stratification.** *Rationale:* if a stimulant response indexes arousal/effort-reward rather than capacity @Kay2025stimulantarousalreward, then activity/arousal response should cluster patients into arousal-limited vs capacity-limited subgroups. *Certainty: 0.30.* *Falsifiable prediction:* a baseline classifier independent of drug response predicts who benefits. *Consequence:* drug selection could follow phenotype instead of trial-and-error. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 3 — Drug/medication ideas

**3.1 Stimulant-with-activity-monitoring pilot (mechanistic, not efficacy).** *Rationale:* measure actigraphy + WM on/off stimulant in ME/CFS to test whether the ADHD medication×activity interaction reproduces @Wiegers2024StimulantActivityWM. *Certainty: 0.30 (mechanistic probe only).* *Falsifiable prediction:* the interaction coefficient is ~0 in ME/CFS. *Consequence:* would distinguish "drug acts on arousal recruitment" from "drug restores capacity" in fatigue. **Safety note:** this is a probe proposal — stimulant masking/overexertion hazard and autonomic cautions are already documented (@spec:stimulant-arousal-reward-not-attention; ch24/ch33 sec-16); not a treatment recommendation. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 4 — Supplement/nutraceutical ideas

**4.1 CNS-penetrant creatine as a buffer test.** *Rationale:* if PFC WM depends on the PCr shuttle @Godlewska2024creatineMRS, then recruiting arousal cannot substitute for a depleted buffer — predicts creatine (not movement) restores the *buffered* component. *Certainty: 0.30.* *Falsifiable prediction:* creatine improves sustained-WM tasks (buffer-limited) more than it improves arousal-limited lapses. *Consequence:* separates a buffer fix from an arousal fix. *Dedup flag:* creatine content already exists in ch07 — check before integrating. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 5 — Non-pharmacological interventions

**5.1 Movement/fidgeting as a cognitive-load self-monitor.** *Rationale:* in ADHD, spontaneous movement indexes executive load; in ME/CFS the patient's *urge to move/fidget* (or its absence) could be an early load signal for pacing. *Certainty: 0.30.* *Falsifiable prediction:* self-reported restlessness tracks objective cognitive-load-induced deterioration better than subjective fatigue. *Consequence:* a zero-cost pacing cue that lets patients stop *before* cognitive PEM rather than after. **Patient-safety note:** framing must not imply movement is therapeutic for ME/CFS; the claim is about *reading* a load signal, and must not encourage activity in PEM-prone patients. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 6 — Combinations + access

**6.1 Severity-stratified cognitive-work adaptations.** *Rationale:* the recruitment model implies mild patients might benefit from allowing movement during cognitive work (as ADHD children do), while severe/bedbound patients have no spare budget for movement — so the intervention inverts across severity. *Certainty: 0.25.* *Falsifiable prediction:* movement-permitted cognitive tasks help mild but harm severe patients (PEM cost > arousal benefit). *Consequence:* prevents exporting a paediatric-ADHD classroom adaptation into a bedbound population where it would cause harm. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 7 — Mathematical model extensions

**7.1 Add an arousal/recruitment term to the energy-triage model.** *Rationale:* the current hierarchy makes executive function a pure ATP-threshold function @fig:energy-triage-hierarchy; the ADHD data imply a second, recruitable input (arousal) that gates whether ATP is *allocated* to persistent firing. *Certainty: 0.30.* *Falsifiable prediction:* a two-input model (ATP budget × arousal recruitment) predicts the observed step-up activity and the medication×activity interaction better than a single ATP threshold. *Consequence:* makes a quantitative prediction that distinguishes energy-supply from energy-allocation failure. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 8 — Cross-disease bridges

**8.1 A three-point arousal spectrum: ADHD → Long COVID brain fog → ME/CFS.** *Rationale:* ADHD (recruitment-limited), post-viral brain fog (partial), ME/CFS (supply-limited) may sit on one arousal/energy axis, with the existing LC-ADHD framework @Spanoghe2026LongCovidADHD supplying the middle point. *Certainty: 0.30.* *Falsifiable prediction:* a common arousal/energetic index orders the three groups monotonically. *Consequence:* reframes three separate "attention" complaints as one measurable axis. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 9 — Diagnostic/biomarker ideas

**9.1 Actigraphy/arousal-response as a stratification marker.** *Rationale:* if arousal recruitment is measurable (actigraphy during cognitive load, pupillometry), it could stratify patients @Drescher2021PupillometryStateRegulation. *Certainty: 0.25.* *Falsifiable prediction:* actigraphy-during-cognition separates stimulant responders from non-responders. *Consequence:* a wearable-based triage that needs no blood test. **Caveat:** Drescher's null tonic-arousal finding means any arousal marker must be validated first. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 10 — Reasons this may NOT be relevant to ME/CFS (CRITICAL)

**10.1 The ADHD movement phenomenon may be developmentally specific.** *Rationale:* all core evidence is in 8–12-year-old boys, where cortical maturation and motor-control maturation are incomplete; the compensation may not exist in adults, and ME/CFS is adult-predominant. *Evidence link:* the paediatric-only sample base @Rapport2009HyperactivityWMDeficits @Sarver2015HyperactivityCompensatory @Kofler2015ActivityWM @Dekkers2021CognitiveDemandActivity. *Certainty: 0.40 (for the caution).* *Falsifiable prediction:* adult ADHD cohorts should show the same movement-linked WM gain if the phenomenon is not developmental. *Consequence:* if developmental, the bridge to adult ME/CFS loses its behavioural arm — a major limitation that must be stated. `origin: brainstorm`

**10.2 ME/CFS may not be hypoarousal.** *Rationale:* the direct arousal proxy was *null* in a state-regulation study @Drescher2021PupillometryStateRegulation, and ME/CFS shows *central* NE deficiency with *peripheral* sympathetic elevation @Aregawi2026Noradrenergic — so "low arousal" is not a clean description. *Certainty: 0.40 (caution).* *Falsifiable prediction:* if ME/CFS is not hypoaroused, arousal-raising interventions should fail to improve cognition despite improving subjective wakefulness. *Consequence:* prevents building the bridge on a mischaracterised arousal state. `origin: brainstorm`

**10.3 Delay aversion / motivational accounts may fully explain the movement.** *Rationale:* movement-as-delay-escape @SonugaBarke2003DualPathway and activity-in-waiting data @Antrop2005WaitingActivity fit without any arousal/energy construct. *Certainty: 0.35 (caution).* *Falsifiable prediction:* movement should track delay/reward structure regardless of WM demand size if delay aversion dominates. *Consequence:* if delay aversion explains it, the energetic bridge to ME/CFS is unfounded. `origin: brainstorm`

## Category 11 — Null-hypothesis assessment (CRITICAL)

**11.1 What if cognitive-energetics coupling has no role in ME/CFS?** *Rationale:* brain fog could be an entirely separate (e.g., neuroinflammatory, glymphatic, or perfusion) mechanism with no arousal-recruitment component. *Impact if null:* the behavioural arm of @syn:prefrontal-energy-adhd-unification would need revision; the new @spec:adhd-movement-arousal-energy would be deleted; and stimulant benefit would need re-explaining without an arousal-recruitment story. *Certainty: n/a (null framing).* *Falsifiable prediction:* ME/CFS executive impairment that is fully explained by a non-energetic mechanism. *Consequence:* identifies exactly which existing claims would need revision, making the null testable rather than rhetorical. `origin: brainstorm`
