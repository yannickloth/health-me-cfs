# Female-Specific Reproductive Lifespan Chapter — Integration Plan

**Purpose:** Integrate a dedicated treatment of the female reproductive axis in ME/CFS — covering menstrual cyclicity of symptoms, pregnancy outcomes and obstetric management, perimenopause/menopause and HRT, and endometriosis/PCOS co-occurrence. ME/CFS is ~4:1 female; MCAS/POTS/hEDS cluster around estrogen. Coverage is currently scattered (~89 hits) with no unified chapter.
