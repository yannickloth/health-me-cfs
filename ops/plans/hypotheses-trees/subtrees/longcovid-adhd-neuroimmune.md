## Integration Context

**Priority:** 🟠 medium — viewpoint-framework synthesis; shared mechanism well-corroborated, causal claim contested (Zemer2024 null)
**ME/CFS links:**
- Four convergent neuroimmune mechanisms linking Long Covid and AD(H)D: frontal-striatal-hippocampal circuits, catecholamine neuroimmune dysregulation, tryptophan-kynurenine overactivation, mitochondrial bioenergetic defects (Spanoghe 2026)
- Kynurenine→dopamine/cognition arm best-supported (Cysique2023 direct human PASC evidence; Rassoulpour2005 kynurenic-acid-lowers-striatal-DA; Tanaka2022 kynurenine↔mitochondrial)
- Converges with existing `@spec:adhd-mecfs-same-root` and kynurenine/mitochondrial ME/CFS corpus
**Target chapters:** ch14d-cross-disease.typ; hypothesis-registry.typ
**Pre-identified hypotheses:** shared LC–AD(H)D–ME/CFS neuroimmune substrate (kynurenine-to-dopamine link); "acquired neuroimmune-mediated dopaminergic/noradrenergic dysfunction" — MUST remain open-question (Zemer2024 null vs positive epidemiology, cert diff ≤0.15)
**Key null result:** Zemer2024 (20-yr national cohort, raw 0.70) found no independent COVID→ADHD diagnosis/treatment effect; post-COVID prescribing rise consistent with catch-up diagnosis (Gimbach2024). The causal "infection→ADHD" claim is unresolved and framed as `#open-question`.
