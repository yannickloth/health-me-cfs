## Reassessment notes (Phase 5)

- **1.2** 0.35 → `#open-question`: the suicide excess is established, the *impulsivity-specificity* (short ideation-to-attempt latency, unplanned attempts) is unmeasured in ME/CFS and unsupported by any Phase 1 paper. An open question is honest; a certainty would be fabricated.
- **6.1** 0.40 → 0.20: prevalence (20.9% persistent adult ADHD in a CFS cohort) is documented; the mortality benefit of screening is inferred, not shown. Usefulness dx 3 retained (a validated screen exists; its mortality-predictive value does not).
- **3.1** 0.15 → `#open-question` per Instruction H: guanfacine and atomoxetine both interact with common ME/CFS co-prescriptions (beta-blockers, midodrine, fludrocortisone) through additive hypotension/bradycardia and noradrenergic effects — practical deployment is blocked, mechanism remains interesting.
- **1.1** 0.25 → `#open-question`: no study separates behavioural from pharmacological mediation; it is a question about study design, not a claim.
- **5.2** merged into 1.2: same impulsivity mechanism; the safety-planning consequence travels with the open question.
- **9.1** merged into 6.1: both are the same screening/stratification claim at different specificity.
- **8.1 / 4.1** Tier 3: the supporting literature (narcolepsy stimulant mortality; iron/omega-3 trials in ADHD) is outside this cycle's bib and was not searched — writing either without sources would be fabrication.
