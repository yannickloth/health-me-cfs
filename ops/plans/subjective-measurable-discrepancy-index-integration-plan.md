## Purpose

Formalizes the recurring pattern across ME/CFS clinical observations: patients report symptoms (orthostatic intolerance, allergy, fatigue) that standard objective measurements don't capture or contradict (normal blood tests, normal IgE, normal pupillometry resting but abnormal dynamic). The discrepancy itself may be a diagnostic biomarker — high discrepancy → dysautonomia-dominant subtype (perception intact, objective deficit only during challenge/orthostasis); low discrepancy → immune-inflammatory subtype (measurable biomarkers track symptom severity). Derived from material in ch30, pupillometry, ocular, and GI symptom integrations.
