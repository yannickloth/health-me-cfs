# Integration Plan: Pulsed Therapy / Tachyphylaxis Framework

**Purpose:** Develop a formal analysis of tachyphylaxis mechanisms across the 17-drug hormesis framework — why time-dependent dosing (pulsed vs continuous) matters, which drugs benefit from pulsing, and how the Calabrese corpus's time-dependent hormesis (Sun 2020, yin-yang hormesis) informs dosing schedules for drugs with fluctuating receptor occupancy (LDN's 4–6 hour blockade window, intermittent rapamycin).

**Target chapters:** ch15 (integrative models — hormesis framework), ch27 (medications-systems — dosing strategies), ch30 (mechanistic cascade tracing)

**Pre-identified hypotheses:**
- Time-dependent hormesis (pulsed dosing) preserves therapeutic windows that continuous dosing extinguishes
- Tachyphylaxis subtype taxonomy: Nrf2 desensitization, receptor internalization, compensatory pathway exhaustion
- Drugs with fluctuating receptor occupancy (LDN, rapamycin) are most sensitive to pulsing schedules
- The 17-drug hormesis framework can be extended to categorize each drug by tachyphylaxis risk and optimal pulsing strategy
