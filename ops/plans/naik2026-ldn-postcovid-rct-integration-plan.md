# Integration Plan: Naik 2026 — Low-Dose Naltrexone for Post-COVID Fatigue Syndrome (RCT)

**Purpose:** Integrate the randomized controlled trial by Naik et al. (2026) on low-dose naltrexone (LDN) for post-COVID fatigue syndrome, including its supplementary material, to assess efficacy, safety, and mechanistic relevance to ME/CFS and Long COVID.

**Target chapters:**
- Part 3 (treatments/pharmacological) — primary integration of LDN evidence
- ch14d (cross-disease comparisons) — post-COVID fatigue / Long COVID parallels
- ch30 sec-12 (pharmacodiagnostic compendium) — LDN entry update

**Pre-identified hypotheses:**
- LDN improves fatigue severity in post-COVID fatigue syndrome versus placebo
- LDN's TLR4 antagonism and microglial modulation are plausible mechanisms in post-viral fatigue
- Safety and tolerability of LDN in post-COVID fatigue are favorable
- Findings may generalize to ME/CFS, but population relevance discount applies
