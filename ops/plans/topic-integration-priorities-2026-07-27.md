## New Ideas (not in any brainstorm file)

### N1. Methylation Erosion → Tissue Remodeling Axis

Methylation loss at tissue-specific enhancers → fibroblasts lose joint-tissue
identity (acquired CT weakness), microglia lose surveillance phenotype (sustained
activation), brainstem neurons lose pacemaker identity (autonomic instability).
Single mechanism → three disconnected findings. Testable: same-cell methylation
arrays across skin fibroblasts, PBMC microglia-like cells, brainstem nuclei →
convergent hypomethylation at shared tissue-identity loci.

Reframes ME/CFS as methylation-driven de-differentiation disease.

### N2. Endogenous Pharmacodiagnostic Battery

The body runs its own drug trials: luteal phase = endogenous progesterone/GABA-A
PAM trial; pregnancy = endogenous corticosteroid/neurosteroid trial; viral
infection = endogenous interferon/IDO trial; sleep deprivation = endogenous
orexin-receptor-challenge trial. Each produces a response pattern structurally
identical to a medication trial. Integrates into ch30 sec-07 (endogenous probes)
+ ch15 (hormesis).

If symptoms *improve* during first trimester (progesterone/allopregnanolone rise
100-fold) → positive endogenous neurosteroid trial — diagnostic information no
drug can replicate.

### N3. Chronological ICE Triphasic Model

Every major mechanism follows: Phase 1 = infection/substrate damage (DRP1
phosphorylation → mitochondrial fragmentation). Phase 2 = compensation becomes
self-sustaining (PDK4→PDH inhibition→lactate shunt; IDO→kynurenine drain;
HIF-1α→WASF3→Complex I decoupling). Phase 3 = epigenetic consolidation → the
compensation IS the disease, trigger no longer needed.

Unifies mitochondrial, immune, neurological, and CT chapters. Therapeutic
implication: Phase 2 interventions (CoQ10, LDN, antivirals) are compensatory;
Phase 3 interventions (epigenetic reprogramming, ISR reset) would be curative.
Belongs as `#synthesis` in ch15 + framing paragraph in ch16 + one sentence in
abstract.

**Highest-impact-for-least-effort. Start here.**

### N4. Severity-as-Compartment-Spread Model

Severity maps to which compartments the disease has colonized. Mild = metabolic
only. Moderate = + autonomic (brainstem). Severe = + CNS full-spectrum
(microglial, white matter, glymphatic). Very severe = + bone marrow
(pancytopenia) + systemic endothelial.

Testable: SFN density ↔ orthostatic intolerance, FDG-PET ↔ cognitive severity,
bone marrow biopsy ↔ pancytopenia. Cross-reference from every Part II chapter's
severity-applicability statements. Target: ch16 causal hierarchy.

### N5. Drug-Identity Theorem

Every drug has a different identity in ME/CFS than in its labeled indication. A
stimulant → dopamine-depletion probe. An antihistamine → CNS H1-receptor density
assay. A benzodiazepine → chloride-gradient polarity test. Paper already contains
this logic in ch30/ch33 (Ligand-Receptor Duality). Elevate to Part II framing
principle.
