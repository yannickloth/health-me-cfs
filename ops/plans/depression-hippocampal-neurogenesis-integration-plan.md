**Key finding & why it matters:** Adult hippocampal neurogenesis stalls in major depressive disorder (Peng et al. 2026, Nature Medicine), raising the possibility that molecular depression is distinguishable from the reactive low mood that most ME/CFS patients develop after onset. This offers a potential future marker for the depression–ME/CFS differential and connects to the post-infectious suppression of hippocampal neurogenesis seen after mild COVID. Direct ME/CFS relevance is unproven (zero direct studies) and the field disputes whether adult human neurogenesis is measureable at all — hence the cross-disease, speculation-capped framing.
