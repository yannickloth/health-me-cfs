# Integration Plan: Common Biology of Fatigue (Cross-Disease Neuroimmune / Immunometabolic)

**Purpose:** Integrate the hypothesis that pathological fatigue across immune-inflammatory diseases (Long COVID, ME/CFS, SLE, Sjögren's, RA, PsA, axSpA, SSc, AAV, sarcoidosis, IIM, MG, MS, IBD, coeliac, PBC, cancer, IM/EBV, ICI-therapy) reflects a shared neuroimmune programme and an immune-metabolic energetic conflict (selfish brain vs selfish immune system), and that biologically defined fatigue phenotypes (not a single universal biomarker) should replace the 0–10 severity-only framing. Distinct from prior fatigue cycles.

**Pre-identified hypotheses (provisional, to be validated by Phase 1):**
- Pathological fatigue across immune-inflammatory diseases shares a neuroimmune programme (sickness-behaviour / cytokine-to-brain signalling) — provisional
- Immune activation imposes a metabolic cost that redistributes energy away from brain/muscle (selfish-brain vs selfish-immune-system conflict) — provisional, likely speculative
- Fatigue is mechanistically heterogeneous → several objective subtypes (immune-inflammatory, neuroimmune, autonomic, vascular, bioenergetic, endocrine, haematological, neuromuscular), not one universal biomarker — provisional
