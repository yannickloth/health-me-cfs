- **Key finding + why it matters:** A n=21 ADHD crossover shows L-theanine-caffeine and methylphenidate both reduce selective-attention false alarms and improve P3b, but only methylphenidate improves behavioral reaction time — a pattern NOT a clean selection-vs-speed dissociation (P3b latency is itself a speed index).
