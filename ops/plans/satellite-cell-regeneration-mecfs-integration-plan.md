## Purpose

Evaluate evidence that ME/CFS and Long COVID patients have reduced satellite cell (Pax7⁺) and fibroadipogenic progenitor (FAP, PDGFRα⁺) cell abundance in skeletal muscle, indicating impaired muscle regeneration capacity. Integrate findings on oxidative-to-glycolytic metabolic shift and reduced antioxidative metabolites. Assess implications for PEM, muscle damage persistence, and mitochondrial-muscle cross-talk.
