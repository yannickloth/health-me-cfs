# Integration Plan: Osteoporosis & Bone Health in ME/CFS

**Purpose:** Evaluate and, if warranted, integrate the ME Association Q&A on osteoporosis risk, prevention, and treatment in ME/CFS — covering decreased activity, vitamin D deficiency, calcium deficiency, weight-bearing exercise limitations, and treatment options (calcium/vitamin D, antiresorptives, anabolic therapies).

**Target chapters:**
- Part 1 — ch06 disease course (severe reality: reduced mobility)
- Part 2 — ch10 endocrine (vitamin D / calcium axis); ch20 biomarkers (vitamin D)
- Part 3 — ch30 lifestyle (weight-bearing exercise, nutrition); ch24 urgent action (nutritional failure); ch33/ch34 medication reference (bisphosphonates)
- Appendices — appendix-f supplement guide (vitamin D, calcium); appendix-c diagnostic tools

**Pre-identified hypotheses:**
- H1: Reduced mobility/mechanical unloading in moderate–severe ME/CFS raises osteoporosis risk (bedrest precedent — ch26 ped osteoporosis-risk).
- H2: Housebound ME/CFS patients have high vitamin D deficiency prevalence.
- H3: Restricted diets (avoidance patterns) risk calcium deficiency.
- H4: Weight-bearing exercise advice is not achievable for moderate–severe; needs non-weight-bearing alternative framing.
