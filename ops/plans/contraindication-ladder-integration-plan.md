# Contraindication Ladder — Integration Plan

**Purpose:** Formalize a "contraindication ladder" — 4-level classification of drug combinations that MUST NOT be combined in ME/CFS patients (receptor antagonism, pharmacokinetic catastrophe, pharmacodynamic opposition, metabolic PEM risk), plus a patient-experience-driven "what NOT to use if" table.

**Target chapters:** ch33 sec-10 subsec-05 (drug-drug interactions as probes) — extension of `subsubsec-05-prerequisite-and-contraindicated-combinations.typ`

**Pre-identified hypotheses:** (from brainstorm sec 13b)
- Level 1: Receptor antagonism (certain — direct pharmacological opposition)
- Level 2: Pharmacokinetic catastrophe (certain if CYP genotype known)
- Level 3: Pharmacodynamic opposition (probable — opposing physiological goals)
- Level 4: Metabolic PEM risk (probable in severe patients)
- Contraindication table: patient-experience → permanent avoidance rules
- Sequential ordering principle: which drug first (sec 13c)
