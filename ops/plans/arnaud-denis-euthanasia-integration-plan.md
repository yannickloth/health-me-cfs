## Purpose

Research online the euthanasia death of French actor/director Arnaud Denis (died 2026-09-22 in Belgium, age 43) and decide whether/how it belongs in the ME/CFS paper. Media report he suffered 3 years after a 2023 hernia-mesh implant and was ultimately diagnosed with severe myalgic encephalomyelitis; his final statement denounced psychiatricization of ME patients ("Honte à ceux qui psychiatrisent les malades"). The paper already narrates two end-of-life cases in ch06 sec-06 (Samuel, Austria 2024, reddit-sourced; Pauline Hongnat, France 2026-03-17, news-sourced) — none peer-reviewed.
