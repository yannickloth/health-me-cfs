# Integration Plan: Lifetime Energy-Level Problems → ME/CFS (Mild / Constitutional Cases)

**Purpose:** Evaluate whether patients who have had energy-level problems for a lifetime — constitutional tiredness, "wired-tired" ADHD/autism energetic profiles, or "just more tired than expected" at baseline — constitute a distinct mild/constitutional predisposing trajectory that precedes (or is continuous with) a later more moderate/severe ME/CFS development. Distinguish lifelong mild energy constraint from acute-onset moderate/severe ME/CFS, and assess whether a low-baseline-energy constitution is a risk factor, a prodromal form, or a qualitatively separate presentation.

**Topic slug:** `lifetime-energy-mild-cases`

**Target chapters:**
- ch02–ch05 (Part 1: clinical features / symptom onset / severity stratification)
- ch14d (cross-disease comparisons — ADHD/ASD energetic profiles)
- ch06/ch07 (energy metabolism / mitochondrial reserve — low baseline reserve)
- ch17 (lifestyle / pacing for mild cases)
- ch20 (biomarkers of low-baseline energy)
- ch25b / ch30 sec-09 (research directions / cross-disease convergence)

**Overlapping prior work (MUST cross-reference, not duplicate):**
- `adhd-energy-predisposition` subtree (20 nodes, completed) — ADHD as brain energy failure + ME/CFS predisposition
- `adhd-asd-comorbidity-mecfs` plan (completed) — shared metabolic reserve, BH4 bottleneck
- `adhd-same-causes-mecfs` plan (brainstorm exists) — same-cause framing
- `brain-energy-asd-mecfs` plan (Phase 4: 50 brainstorm ideas pending) — ASD brain energy adaptation
- `energy-cartography`, `energy-failure-differential`, `energy-cartography` subtrees
- Registry entries for constitutional/predisp position (grep `energy level|constitutionally|predispos`)

**Pre-identified hypotheses:**
- A low-baseline energy reserve (constitutional/neurodivergent) lowers the threshold for post-infectious or stressor-triggered ME/CFS decompensation → "mild before, moderate/severe after" trajectory
- Lifetime "just more tired than expected" (without PEM) is distinct from ME/CFS PEM; the PEM criterion discriminates constitution from disease
- ADHD/ASD energetic phenotypes (dopaminergic demand, mitochondrial reserve deficit) represent a continuous spectrum from constitutional low-energy to ME/CFS, not a categorical boundary
- Severity stratification: mild cases may be under-diagnosed because constitutional tiredness is normalized
