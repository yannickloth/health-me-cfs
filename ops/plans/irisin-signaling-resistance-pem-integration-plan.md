## Purpose

Evaluate and, if evidence warrants, integrate the Souma 2026 mechanistic framework proposing irisin signaling resistance in ME/CFS as a contributor to post-exertional malaise (PEM), via the TSP-1–HSP90α–αvβ5 axis. The paper proposes that irisin (a myokine released by exercised muscle) engages an HSP90α/αvβ5 integrin signaling pathway whose anti-inflammatory and homeostatic functions are blunted in ME/CFS, contributing to PEM pathophysiology.
