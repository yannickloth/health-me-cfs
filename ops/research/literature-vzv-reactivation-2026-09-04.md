# Literature Summary: VZV Reactivation & Rash-Less VZV Disease (vzv-reactivation)

Date: 2026-09-04
Phase: 1 (Literature Research) of /integrate-topic
Scope: Rash-less VZV (zoster sine herpete) + VZV neurovascular disease as they
relate to ME/CFS, post-viral syndromes, and the poly-herpesvirus reactivation
model. Does NOT re-establish that VZV causes shingles (already in ch08).

## Landscape at a glance

- **Existing corpus already covers** ME/CFS herpesvirus serology and nulls
  (Palomo2026herpesvirus, Ariza2025polyherpesvirus, Buchwald1996ViralSerologies,
  Koelle2002TwinsHSV, Domingues2023HerpesIgG, Apostolou2022Saliva, Domingues2021).
  The novel gap is the **rash-less VZV** concept and VZV **neurovascular/vascular**
  disease, plus the **cause-vs-consequence** framing for VZV reactivation.
- **Direct ME/CFS evidence is sparse and largely hypothesis-level** (Shapiro 2009).
  No empirical cohort directly links rash-less VZV or zoster sine herpete to
  ME/CFS (E1/R3 = 0 PubMed hits for the post-viral-fatigue phrasing).
- **Strongest new data** come from long-COVID/post-viral serology (Karachaliou
  2024) and from the asymptomatic-reactivation → vascular mechanism
  (Fullerton 2025 VIPS II), plus the driving NRDP review (Bubak 2026).

## Papers included (NEW bib entries — verified present in viral-infection.bib)

### 1. Bubak2026VZVInfection (DRIVING SOURCE)
- Nature Reviews Disease Primers, 2026;12(1). DOI 10.1038/s41572-026-00735-5.
- Raw certainty: **0.85** (top-tier journal, established authors: Nagel, Breuer,
  Mahalingam). Population weight 0.75 (general/mechanism review).
- Discounted certainty: **0.64**.
- Role: authoritative scaffold establishing rash-less VZV (zoster sine herpete),
  VZV neurovascular disease, childhood-stroke fraction, and diagnostic gap.
- Not yet PubMed-indexed (published 2026-09-03); metadata verified via Crossref.

### 2. Shapiro2009VZVgangliaCFS
- Medical Hypotheses, 2009;73(5):728-734. PMID 19520522.
- Raw certainty: **0.30** (hypothesis essay, single author, non-empirical).
  Population weight 1.00 (conceptual ME/CFS relevance).
- Discounted certainty: **0.30**.
- Role: THE direct proposal that rash-less VZV (zoster sine herpete) reactivation
  in peripheral/autonomic ganglia produces ME/CFS-like symptoms. Supports the
  existing ch08 note on VZV sensory/autonomic ganglion neurotropism.

### 3. Halpin2017VZVdUTPase
- J Med Virol, 2017;89(9):1636-1645. PMID 28303641.
- Raw certainty: **0.55** (peer-reviewed primary serology, single lab series).
  Population weight 1.00 (ME/CFS cohort).
- Discounted certainty: **0.55**.
- Role: foundational primary study that VZV (anti-VZV dUTPase) participates in
  the poly-herpesvirus co-reactivation serologic pattern in ME/CFS.
- **Cohort-overlap flag:** Ariza/Williams (Ohio State) + Klimas/Fletcher (Nova
  Southeastern) lineage overlaps Palomo2026/Ariza2025 — count once.

### 4. Karachaliou2024VZV_PASC
- J Med Virol, 2024;96(9):e29862. PMID 39247972.
- Raw certainty: **0.65** (population cohort n=1083). Population weight 0.85
  (long-COVID/post-viral).
- Discounted certainty: **0.55**.
- Role: VZV IgG elevated in PASC (incl. neuropsychiatric subtype), even without
  prior severe COVID → serologic VZV reactivation in post-viral sequelae.

### 5. Li2026PostCOVIDVZVthreshold
- Front Cell Infect Microbiol, 2026;16:1904565. PMID 42621909.
- Raw certainty: **0.45** (peer-reviewed review/opinion). Population weight 0.85
  (long-COVID).
- Discounted certainty: **0.38**.
- Role: cause-vs-consequence framing — VZV reactivation post-COVID best read as
  immune-threshold-lowering (consequence), not established causal driver. Directly
  informs the ch08 "reactivation as consequence vs cause" debate.

### 6. Fullerton2025VIPSII
- J Am Heart Assoc, 2025;14(6):e039184. PMID 40099546.
- Raw certainty: **0.75** (22-center prospective cohort, n=205). Population weight
  0.75 (general/mechanism — childhood stroke).
- Discounted certainty: **0.56**.
- Role: asymptomatic (rash-less) VZV reactivation in 9.8% of childhood AIS vs 3.1%
  controls, concentrated in focal cerebral arteriopathy → primary evidence that
  VZV vasculopathy can proceed without clinical rash.

### 7. Livieratos2025VZVstroke
- Viruses, 2025;17(12):1591. PMID 41472261.
- Raw certainty: **0.50** (peer-reviewed narrative review). Population weight 0.75
  (general/mechanism).
- Discounted certainty: **0.38**.
- Role: mechanism synthesis of VZV ganglion-reactivation → cerebral arterial
  inflammation (vasculopathy) → stroke, bridging VZV mechanism to ME/CFS
  cerebral-vasculature/neuroinflammation interest.

## Existing-corpus reuse (relevant, NOT re-added)
- Koelle2002TwinsHSV — NULL: VZV PCR did not differ in 22 MZ twin pairs discordant
  for CFS (co-twin design).
- Buchwald1996ViralSerologies — NULL: 13 viruses, no consistent CF/CFS differences.
- Domingues2023HerpesIgG — UKMEB; VZV IgG in panel; poor subgroup discrimination.
- Apostolou2022Saliva — ME/CFS saliva panel incl. VZV; EBV/HHV-6/HERV-K standout.
- Palomo2026herpesvirus, Ariza2025polyherpesvirus — poly-herpesvirus co-reactivation.

## Indirect biochemical / systemic links (mandatory mapping)
VZV reactivation biology (established in Bubak2026 + Livieratos2025 + Fullerton2025):
sensory/autonomic ganglion reactivation → (a) local ganglionic inflammation,
(b) virus travel along nerves to cerebral arteries → VZV vasculopathy/arterial
inflammation → ischemic stroke; (c) endothelial/vascular involvement.

Intersection with documented ME/CFS pathophysiology:
- Cerebral vasculature / endothelial dysfunction — VZV vasculopathy pathway
  (Livieratos2025) parallels ME/CFS cerebral vascular findings; intersection is
  mechanistic but NOT ME/CFS-validated.
- Neuroinflammation — VZV ganglionic + arterial inflammation is a neuroinflammatory
  stimulus; ME/CFS has documented neuroinflammation (TSPO-PET etc., already in
  corpus) but no VZV-specific demonstration.
- Autonomic + sensory ganglia — Shapiro2009 maps VZV autonomic/sensory ganglion
  involvement to ME/CFS autonomic + sensory symptoms; supported only as hypothesis.
- Searches: "VZV vasculopathy stroke", "varicella arterial ischemic stroke
  children", "VZV dorsal root ganglia inflammation", "varicella zoster
  neuroinflammation" — screened; direct ME/CFS intersection papers absent, so the
  indirect link is recorded at hypothesis/mechanism level.

## Null / negative findings
1. No direct cohort/epidemiological study links rash-less VZV (zoster sine
   herpete) to ME/CFS or post-viral fatigue (E1, R3 = 0 hits).
2. Existing corpus nulls (Koelle2002TwinsHSV VZV PCR; Buchwald1996 serologies)
   show no consistent VZV (or herpesvirus) elevation distinguishing ME/CFS.
3. Li2026 cautions the COVID→HZ association is modest and not causal; VZV
   reactivation likely a consequence of immune dysregulation.
4. Fullerton2025: VZV stroke role "modest" in a vaccinated population (9.8%).

## Competing mechanism
The evidence weighs VZV reactivation as a **consequence** of immune disturbance
(Li2026) and as one member of a poly-herpesvirus pattern (Halpin2017,
Palomo2026), rather than a proven independent causal trigger. This mirrors the
existing ch08 "reactivation as consequence vs cause" framing.

## Cohort-overlap verdict (Augment B)
- **Ariza/Williams + Klimas/Fletcher site lineage:** Halpin2017 overlaps
  Palomo2026herpesvirus and Ariza2025polyherpesvirus (same labs). Flag; count as
  one contributing lineage. Recruitment years differ (Halpin ~2017 sera vs
  Palomo 2026 longitudinal), so not identical patients, but same lab pool.
- Karachaliou2024 (ISGlobal Barcelona, GCAT/COVICAT) — independent cohort.
- Fullerton2025 (VIPS II, 22 North American/Australian centers) — independent.
- No other overlap among the 7 new papers.
- Verdict: **two papers share a lab lineage (Halpin↔Palomo/Ariza); all others
  independent.** No double-counted patient cohort across the new additions
  themselves.

## Overall verdict for Phase 2
Direct ME/CFS evidence for VZV (rash-less) is **thin and hypothesis-level**
(Shapiro2009, discounted 0.30). Strong support exists at the general/long-COVID
serology level (Karachaliou2024, 0.55) and the asymptomatic-reactivation→vascular
mechanism level (Fullerton2025, 0.56), plus the authoritative Bubak scaffold
(0.64). Evidence is not uniformly null but is indirect for ME/CFS. Expect Phase 2
to weigh PARTIAL (speculation/open-question framing for the rash-less VZV →
ME/CFS claim; mechanism documented, direct causal role unproven).
