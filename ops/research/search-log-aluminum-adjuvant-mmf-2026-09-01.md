## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed journal (all; reviews acceptable for framework/mechanism claims).
- Direct relevance to aluminum oxyhydroxide adjuvants, MMF, ASIA, or ME/CFS (or a mechanistically downstream pathway of aluminum adjuvants).
- Human clinical (ME/CFS-like or MMF) preferred; animal/in vitro accepted only for mechanism (flagged lower weight).
- Null/negative/counter evidence and regulatory safety assessments given equal search priority.
- Diagnostic criteria era recorded for any ME/CFS clinical claim.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Non-peer-reviewed, predatory-journal, or preprint without later peer-review.
- Purely allergic/inflammatory conditions not connected to adjuvants/MMF/CFS (e.g. allergic conjunctivitis, allergic rhinitis pyroptosis).
- Unrelated aluminium water/occupational toxicity without immune/adjuvant/CFS link.
- Duplicate coverage already in corpus (Magnus2015influenza — the Norwegian vaccine/CFS registry counter-evidence; Ruiz2024hpa; Skare2024 — retained as existing, NOT re-added).
- Case reports of single MMF patients with non-diagnostic extra findings (Simpson 2002) — excluded as low-value.

## Cohort / Overlap Detection Notes
- All clinical MMF papers (Rigolet2014, VanDerGucht2015, VanDerGucht2017, AounSebaiti2018, Passeri2011, Ragunathan-Thangarajah2013) draw from the SAME single site: the Garches–Necker–Mondor–Hendaye Reference Center for rare neuromuscular diseases, Henri-Mondor Hospital, Créteil (INSERM U955-Team 10), PIs Gherardi + Authier. **These are overlapping sub-analyses of one referral cohort, not independent replications.** The MMF→ME/CFS link therefore rests on a single-site, single-group observation with no independent replication found.
- Masson2024 (in vitro, same group) and Gherardi2019 review (same group) are not independent.
- Counter-evidence (Magnus2015, Norwegian nationwide registry) IS independent and large-n.
