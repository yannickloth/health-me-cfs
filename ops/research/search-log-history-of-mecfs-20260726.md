## Inclusion Criteria

- Primary source outbreak reports (Los Angeles 1934, Royal Free 1955, Incline Village 1984-85, Akureyri 1948-49)
- Historical review papers covering ME/CFS timeline
- Papers documenting diagnostic criteria evolution (Holmes 1988, Fukuda 1994, CCC 2003, ICC 2011, IOM 2015)
- PACE trial primary report + re-analysis papers
- Papers on nomenclature evolution and psychosomatic framing
- WHO ICD classification history
- NIH deep phenotyping study (2024)
- DecodeME GWAS (2025)
- Papers documenting shift from psychosomatic to biological recognition

## Exclusion Criteria

- Papers on ME/CFS pathophysiology without historical content
- Papers predating 1934 (irrelevant to ME/CFS timeline)
- News articles, blogs, patient advocacy websites (non-peer-reviewed)
- Papers on Long COVID only (no ME/CFS historical connection)
- Duplicate entries across bib files
