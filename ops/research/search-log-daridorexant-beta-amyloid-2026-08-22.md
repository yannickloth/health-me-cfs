## Inclusion Criteria
- Primary human, animal, or cell study reporting daridorexant (or a DORA) effect on β-amyloid (Aβ) production, clearance, or pathology.
- Peer-reviewed or indexed preprint.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Reviews/editorials without primary Aβ data (noted but not counted as primary).
- Papers on tau (p-tau) only — different protein, not β-amyloid.
- Papers on other DORAs (suvorexant, lemborexant) unless explicitly generalizable.
- Papers on daridorexant for insomnia without any amyloid/neurodegeneration endpoint.

## Null result
**No primary study reporting daridorexant effect on β-amyloid exists.** The daridorexant+Alzheimer's hits are all reviews (orexin→AD rationale) or drug profiles. The real neuropathology data for the DORA class is on TAU (not Aβ): Lucey2023SuvorexantTau (CSF p-tau181 −10–15%), Parhizkar2025LemborexantTau (tau phosphorylation reduction). These are for suvorexant and lemborexant, not daridorexant, and measure tau, not β-amyloid.

## Consequence for integration
The direct claim (daridorexant reduces Aβ) is UNSUPPORTED — no primary evidence. The pathway is documentable only as speculation (PARTIAL tier), grounded on (a) the established orexin→Aβ-production link, (b) DORA→sleep→glymphatic Aβ-clearance, and (c) the real (but tau, other-drug) DORA neuroprotection data. No new bib entries for a daridorexant+Aβ primary study — none exists.
