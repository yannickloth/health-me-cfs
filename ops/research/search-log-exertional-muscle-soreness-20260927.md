## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed (preprints accepted only if recent + directly relevant — none used).
- Addresses (A) the mechanism distinguishing ME/CFS post-exertional muscle pain from healthy DOMS — DOMS physiology, proton/acidosis nociception (ASIC/TRPV1), muscle ischemia/hypoperfusion, small-fiber neuropathy, central sensitization, muscle membrane/ion-channel dysfunction, oxidative stress, and the lactate-does-not-cause-DOMS correction — OR (B) management of muscle soreness (DOMS levers: massage, heat/cold, magnesium, tart cherry/antioxidant, compression; ME/CFS caution: pacing, NSAID harm, overexertion).
- Provides sample size, design, journal, and replication status.
- Direct or indirect mechanistic link to ME/CFS, OR foundational DOMS/nociceptor mechanism needed to argue that link.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Papers already in the corpus (reused by PMID/DOI, not duplicated): `Clarkson2002DOMS`, `Lien2019lactate`, `Lien2019LactateAbnormal`, `Jones2012lactate`, `Brooks2018lactate`, `Gerwyn2017muscleFatigue`, `Vecchiet1996muscleCFS`, `Light2009` (ASIC3/P2X), `Sarvaiya2016TRPV1CFS`, `Wirth2020unifying`, `Wirth2021muscle`/`Wirth2021SkeletalMuscle`, `Wirth2024keyRole`, `Wirth2024MicrovascularPostCOVIDMECFS`, `Nijs2021sensitization` + Nijs central-sensitization set, `Hejbol2022myopathy`, `Agergaard2023LongCOVIDmyopathy`, `Appelman2024MusclePEM`, `Fluge2016PDH`, `Novak2026AutonomicPhenotype`, `Germain2025PlasmaProteomePEM`, the autoimmune-SFN-subtype stream.
- `Schmitt2024` (neuropathic myalgia) rejected: population is myotonic dystrophy (DM1/DM2), not ME/CFS/SFN.
