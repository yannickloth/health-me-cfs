## Inclusion Criteria

- Human studies preferred; animal studies included only when they are the primary mechanistic source for the bioenergetic claim (Kealy 2020).
- Directly addresses ≥1 of the three target claims: (a) common fatigue biology / neuroimmune programme; (b) immunometabolic energy redistribution; (c) subtype/biomarker heterogeneity.
- Provides a quantitative prevalence, a mechanistic signal, or a prospective/longitudinal design.
- Peer-reviewed journal.
- Seed PMIDs from the driving thread, verified against PubMed metadata.
- Preprints only if recent and uniquely relevant (none required).

## Exclusion Criteria

- Papers already present in the corpus under any research stream (dedup by PMID) — they are reused, not re-added.
- Narrative opinion without either prevalence data or mechanistic framing (none met both, so kept two bridging reviews: Morris2016, Arnett2012).
- Cohort re-analyses of an already-included cohort treated as independent evidence (Sandler2022 = Dubbo DIOS re-analysis of Hickie2006 cohort — noted, not independently weighted).
- Papers whose population/diagnosis is not a systemic immune-inflammatory or immune-activating condition.
- Retracted GET/CBT trial literature (out of scope; GET hygiene handled elsewhere).
