# Literature Summary — Naik 2026 LDN Post-COVID Fatigue RCT (naik2026-ldn-postcovid-rct)

**Date:** 2026-10-27
**Driving paper:** Naik et al. (2026) — Low Dose Naltrexone for Post-COVID Fatigue Syndrome: A Randomized Controlled Trial (medRxiv preprint, NCT05430152).

## Headline

The **first double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT of LDN in a post-viral-fatigue population is NULL.** LDN (1.0→4.5 mg/day × 16 weeks) did not improve fatigue (FSS) vs placebo in n=160 people with post-COVID fatigue syndrome, with a consistent null across all secondary outcomes (pain, symptom score, step count, quality of life). This is a high-quality, adequately powered negative result that directly undercuts the observational optimism that has driven off-label LDN prescribing in Long COVID / ME/CFS.

## The evidence landscape (rival framings)

### F1 — Supportive (LDN effective): observational only
The entire "LDN works" case in post-viral fatigue rests on **uncontrolled** studies:
- **Polo 2019** (in corpus): retrospective n=218 ME/CFS, 73.9% self-reported response. No control.
- **Tamariz 2024** (NEW): retrospective n=108, LDN HR 5.04 (1.22–20.77) vs physical therapy. Observational, wide CI.
- **O'Kelly 2022** (NEW): pre-post n=52, 6/7 parameters improved. No control.
- **Isman 2024** (NEW): open-label n=36 LDN+NAD+, SF-36 improved, 52% responders. Commercial (AgelessRx), no control, combined drug.
- **Byambasuren 2026** (in corpus): meta-analysis of LDN for long COVID found **zero RCTs**, only 4 observational pre-post (n=155), low certainty.

### F2 — Null/negative (LDN no better than placebo): now the strongest evidence
- **Naik 2026 (NEW, driving):** well-powered RCT, null. The decisive datapoint.
- **Fibromyalgia nulls (in corpus):** DueBruun 2024 (FINAL, n=99, null), Nielsen 2026 (FINAL re-analysis, null), Rodriguez-Freire 2026 (INNOVA 12-month, null). LDN has now failed placebo-controlled testing in fibromyalgia AND post-COVID fatigue.
- **Gouda 2026 (in corpus):** narrative review — "early positive uncontrolled results rarely survive placebo-controlled testing."
- **Bolton 2019 SAE (in corpus):** safety anchor.

### F3 — Responder subtype: unproven but not excluded
- Younger 2013 (in corpus): baseline ESR predicted response (higher inflammation → better response). Hypothesis only.
- Cabanas TRPM3 series (in corpus): LDN restores impaired TRPM3 in NK cells from ME/CFS/Long COVID in vitro — a plausible mechanism but never shown to predict clinical response.
- Naik 2026 prespecified subgroups found **no** significant effect modifier, though the trial was not enriched for a TRPM3/inflammatory subtype.

### F4 — Mechanistic: plausible but clinically unproven
TLR4 antagonism, microglial modulation, opioid-receptor blockade (Osman 2024, Kucic 2021, Toljan 2018 — all in corpus). Mechanism is real; clinical efficacy in post-viral fatigue has now failed its first RCT test.

## Per-paper notes

### 1. Naik2026LDNPostCOVIDRCT (DRIVING) — certainty 0.65
medRxiv preprint. Decentralized double-blind RCT, BC + Ontario (Jan 2024–Feb 2026). n=160 (81/79), 70% female, mean age 45.1. LDN 1→4.5 mg vs placebo ×16 weeks. Primary FSS null (AMD −0.76, 95% CI −4.18 to 2.67); all secondary null. Grade ≥3 AE 6.2% vs 3.8%. PREPRINT (not peer-reviewed). Population weight 0.85. Senior author Luis Nacul (UK ME/CFS Biobank founder).

### 2. Naik2024LDNprotocol — certainty 0.65
BMJ Open 14(5):e085272. SPIRIT protocol for the RCT. Defines PCFS (ME/CFS-like ≥3 months post-SARS-CoV-2), endpoints (FSS primary; pain VAS, PQSymp-12, EQ-5D-5L, step count secondary; IL-6/IFNγ/hsCRP/cortisol/rT3 exploratory). Peer-reviewed, no efficacy data. Population 0.85.

### 3. Tamariz2024LDNpostcovid — certainty 0.45
Clin Ther 46(3):e101–e106. Retrospective n=108 post-COVID clinic. LDN HR 5.04 (1.22–20.77, p=0.02) vs PT alone; fatigue + pain improved. Observational, no randomization, wide CI, single clinic (Klimas group). Paywalled (abstract-only archived). Population 0.85.

### 4. OKelly2022LDNlongcovid — certainty 0.35
Brain Behav Immun Health 24:100485. Pre-post n=52 (Dublin), LDN 1→2 mg ×2 months. 6/7 parameters improved (p≤0.001); 2/38 stopped for diarrhoea/fatigue. Uncontrolled, small, subjective. Population 0.85.

### 5. Isman2024LDNNAD — certainty 0.30
Brain Behav Immun Health 36:100733. Open-label n=36, LDN 4.5 mg + NAD+ patches ×12 weeks. SF-36 36.5→52.1 (p<0.0001); Chalder 25.9→17.4; 52% responders. Commercial sponsor (AgelessRx), no control, combined drug. Population 0.85.

## Synthesis for integration

1. **The LDN evidence base for post-viral fatigue has inverted.** Prior to this RCT the corpus rated LDN "MODERATE (large observational; no RCT)" (appendix-g). The Naik 2026 null RCT, combined with the fibromyalgia null RCTs already integrated, shifts the weight of evidence toward "negative under placebo control."

2. **Safety is settled; efficacy is not.** LDN is consistently well tolerated (Naik 2026, O'Kelly 2022, Isman 2024, Bolton 2019 SAE). The open question is purely whether any efficacy signal survives placebo control.

3. **Population-relevance discount applies.** Naik 2026 is post-COVID fatigue (0.85), not classic ME/CFS (1.00). The null RCT does NOT directly falsify LDN for classic ME/CFS — no ME/CFS-specific LDN RCT has reported (LIFT trial is factorial LDN+pyridostigmine, still recruiting). But it removes the strongest near-population proxy, and the honest inference is that the ME/CFS LDN case is now weaker, not stronger.

4. **Data-integrity observation (not in scope to fix):** appendix-g attributes the "n=42 Long COVID, LDN 78% improved" claim to `@Gottschalk2023`, but the corpus `@Gottschalk2023` entry is a tetrahydrobiopterin pilot study, not an LDN cohort. The actual source for that observational claim is Tamariz 2024 (now added as `@Tamariz2024LDNpostcovid`). Flag for a future citation-fidelity pass.
