## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed (or high-quality preprint) studies relevant to female reproductive lifespan and ME/CFS or directly analogous post-viral/POTS/fibromyalgia conditions
- Target gap areas: perimenopause/menopause transition, HRT (benefit + harm), estrogen-progesterone-mast cell mechanism, menstrual cyclicity of orthostatic/dysautonomia symptoms, obstetric/Long-COVID-in-pregnancy outcomes
- Indirect biochemical search included (estrogen-mast cell mechanism without ME/CFS named) per skill rules
- Null/negative results and contradicting evidence actively sought and included
- Sample size, design, journal tier, replication status recorded for each included paper

## Exclusion Criteria

- Papers already in the bib (Schacterle2004pregnancy, Slack2023pregnancyReview, Thomas2022sexDifferences, Allen2008childbearing, Compton2025endometriosis, Pollack2023reproductiveHealth, boneva2011gynecological, boneva2015menopause, pipper2024steroid, Blitshteyn2026POTSmenopause, Thomas2026neuroendocrinologyFramework)
- Papers already in OTHER bib files with same PMID (heng2025sexspecific PMID 41205594 in immune.bib)
- Irrelevant menopause hits (OSA, breast implant illness, migraine, sleep, dental)
- Papers with no ME/CFS/pots/fibromyalgia/post-viral relevance or no reproductive-lifespan relevance
- Low-quality primary studies failing inclusion bar (e.g., Cureus PCOS-fatigue n=37, no control, null fatigue correlation)
- Non-female-reproductive topics (e.g., male-focused, non-hormone chronic fatigue)

## Certainty Scale Applied

Raw certainty (ordinal heuristic): sample size, publication tier, replication, methodology. Discounted certainty = raw × population weight (ME/CFS 1.00, Long COVID/post-viral 0.85, fibro/POTS/comorbid 0.80, general population 0.75, animal 0.50, in vitro 0.40). See literature summary for per-paper values.
