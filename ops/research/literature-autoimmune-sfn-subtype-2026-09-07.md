# Literature Summary: Autoimmune SFN Subtype — anti-TS-HDS & anti-FGFR3 (autoimmune-sfn-subtype)

Date: 2026-09-07
Phase: 1 (Literature Research) of /integrate-topic
Scope: The contested autoimmune **serological** subtype of SFN — anti-TS-HDS (IgM, trisulfated heparin disaccharide) and anti-FGFR3 (IgG) antibodies — and its treatment (IVIG/immunomodulation). Gap in chapter: ch09 SFN narrative covers IENFD, DRG-IgG passive transfer, ISR-DRG, post-COVID SFN+IVIG, visceral-vs-somatic, but NOT the TS-HDS/FGFR3 serological subtype.

## Landscape at a glance

- **Existing corpus already covers** SFN structural/mechanistic/DRG axes and general autoantibody SFN (SFNAutoantibodies2023, Oaklander2016autoimmuneSFN, Liu2020IVIG, Oaklander2022SFN, McAlpine2024sfnCOVID). The novel gap is specifically the **TS-HDS/FGFR3 serological biomarker subtype** and its CONTESTED evidence base.
- **The biomarker is genuinely contested.** The literature does NOT support a strong diagnostic claim. Six supportive (association/phenotype/mechanism) papers are outweighed in methodological weight by ten validity-challenge papers that include negative RCTs, independent real-world data, and an inter-laboratory reproducibility study.
- **No direct ME/CFS-cohort study exists** for these antibodies. Relevance to ME/CFS is inferential. The two fibromyalgia proxies (closest non-ME/CFS overlapping population) are both NEGATIVE for anti-TS-HDS utility.

## Supportive / mechanism papers (SUPPORT)

### 1. Antoine2015FGFR3 (discovery)
- JNNP 2015;86(12):1347-55. PMID 25628376. Certainty 0.30.
- First description of anti-FGFR3 (intracellular domain) in sensory neuropathies; proposed DRG subgroup. Descriptive discovery, no control-sensitivity analysis. General sensory neuropathy, not ME/CFS.

### 2. Levine2020TSHDSFGFR3
- Muscle Nerve 2020;61(4):512-15. PMID 31650561. Certainty 0.33.
- n=155 cryptogenic SFN, blinded: 48% seropositive; TS-HDS > controls (p=0.0012); 92% acute-onset anti-TS-HDS+. Association in general SFN; no specificity arm. General cryptogenic SFN, not ME/CFS.

### 3. Tholance2020FGFR3
- JNNP 2020;91(1):49-57. PMID 31690697. Certainty 0.33.
- n=426 sensory neuropathies: 15% anti-FGFR3+, DRG-predominant, non-length-dependent 89%. Strong epidemiology but descriptive; big geographic (Brazilian 36% vs European 13%) variability. General sensory neuropathy, not ME/CFS.

### 4. Murin2024SFNsero
- J Clin Neuromuscul Dis 2024;25(4):171-77. PMID 38771226. Certainty 0.25.
- n=172: TS-HDS vs FGFR-3 distinct phenotypes, but skin biopsy normal in many seropositive. Antibody alone does not establish SFN. General SFN.

### 5. Salih2026FGFR3Pathogenic
- Advanced Science 2026;13(22):e11413. PMID 41700748 (PMC13088302, OA PDF archived). Certainty 0.28.
- Preclinical: FGFR3-Ab sera bind DRG neurons, induce hyperexcitability + mechanical hypersensitivity in mice; CRISPR FGFR3 knockout blocks it. Mechanism only; translational; shares patents/interest with the FGFR3 discoverers. Not ME/CFS.

### 6. Zeidman2022IVIG
- J Clin Neuromuscul Dis 2022;24(1):26-37. PMID 36005471. Certainty 0.19.
- n=54 cryptogenic SFN, 44.4% seropositive; IVIG completers IENFD +297%. Uncontrolled, responder-selective, Grifols ties. Directly conflicts with negative controlled trials below. General cryptogenic SFN.

## Validity-challenge / null papers (CHALLENGE) — the core of this review

### 7. Malik2019TSHDSfibromyalgia
- J Clin Neuromuscul Dis 2019;20(3):103-10. PMID 30801480. Certainty 0.38.
- **Fibromyalgia proxy (closest to ME/CFS): 5/22 (23%) anti-TS-HDS+, NOT significantly different from controls (p=0.41).** Null — undercuts discrimination in the overlapping fibromyalgia population.

### 8. Nissan2023TSHDSfibromyalgia
- IMAJ 2023;25(3):187-90. PMID 36946662. Certainty 0.38.
- **Fibromyalgia proxy: anti-TS-HDS IgM significantly LOWER in FMS than healthy controls (7.7 vs 13.2 U/ml, p=0.013)** — reverse of the expected direction; raises healthy-control-reactivity specificity concern. CellTrend assay.

### 9. Chompoopong2023TSHDS
- J Neurol 2023;270(9):4523-28. PMID 37294321. Certainty 0.39.
- **Real-world Mayo, n=77: 34% had NO neuropathy, majority normal IENFD/autonomic tests; immunotherapy benefit in only 31%, NOT better than seronegative (p=0.30).** Directly undercuts diagnostic and predictive value of TS-HDS.

### 10. Trevino2021TSHDSFGFR3
- Muscle Nerve 2021;64(1):70-76. PMID 33792960. Certainty 0.29.
- n=322: antibodies present irrespective of normal/abnormal skin biopsy; do not track objective small-fiber damage. Specificity concern. Includes sudomotor/sweat-nerve-fiber data.

### 11. Appeltshauser2025FGFR3assay
- Front Immunol 2025;16:1604456. PMID 41142778 (PMC12549695, OA PDF archived). Certainty 0.33.
- **Inter-laboratory: 81% concordance (kappa 0.61); lab-specific cut-offs (OD median 0.076 vs 0.293); DRG-IHC confirmatory improves specificity.** No standardized anti-FGFR3 protocol exists — a fundamental reproducibility weakness.

### 12. Morelli2024antibodyReview
- Rev Neurosci 2024;35(8):877-93. PMID 38865989. Certainty 0.35.
- Independent Bologna review: for TS-HDS/FGFR3/Plexin-D1 pathogenic role demonstrated only for some; clinical phenotype and immunotherapy-response association unclarified.

### 13. Gibbons2023IVIGpilot
- Muscle Nerve 2023;67(5):363-70. PMID 36367813. Certainty 0.39.
- **Double-blind placebo RCT pilot, n=17, antibody-positive SFN: IVIG NOT better than placebo on IENFD, pain, or UENS (all NS).** Pivotal negative against antibody-guided IVIG. Small pilot, underpowered.

### 14. Geerts2021IVIG
- Neurology 2021;96(20):e2534-45. PMID 33766992 (PMC8205474, OA PDF archived). Certainty 0.42.
- **Class I RCT, n=60 painful idiopathic SFN: IVIG no benefit vs placebo (pain 40% vs 30%, p=0.588).** Not antibody-stratified, but anchors the immunotherapy null in SFN.

### 15. Ng2026IVIGsystrev
- J Peripher Nerv Syst 2026;31(3):e70138. PMID 42419753. Certainty 0.42.
- Systematic review (2 RCTs + 5 observational): RCTs negative; observational benefits only in uncontrolled autoimmune subtypes; GRADE low-to-very-low. No routine-IVIG basis.

### 16. Katzberg2023IVIGsignal
- Muscle Nerve 2023;67(5):339-40. PMID 36762497. Certainty 0.32.
- Independent editorial: IVIG efficacy signal for TS-HDS/FGFR-3 SFN "fading"; cautions against premature antibody-guided IVIG adoption.

## Existing-corpus reuse (NOT re-added)
- SFNAutoantibodies2023 (Murinello), Oaklander2016autoimmuneSFN, Liu2020IVIG (Oaklander 2018 IVIG retrospective), Oaklander2022SFN, McAlpine2024sfnCOVID, josephoaklander2021mecfssfn, Grayston2019sfn, Oaklander2013sfn, Azcue2023sfn.

## Null / negative findings (explicit)
1. Fibromyalgia proxy nulls: Malik 2019 (no TS-HDS elevation vs controls) and Nissan 2023 (TS-HDS LOWER than controls).
2. TS-HDS real-world (Chompoopong 2023): limited disease specificity; no immunotherapy-response prediction.
3. FGFR3 assay reproducibility (Appeltshauser 2025): lab-specific cut-offs, 19% discordance.
4. RCT nulls: Gibbons 2023 (antibody-positive), Geerts 2021 (idiopathic); systematic review GRADE low-to-very-low (Ng 2026).
5. No ME/CFS-cohort study exists — a direct evidence gap, not a positive finding.

## Competing mechanism / context
The passive-transfer + DRG-IgG axis documented elsewhere in ch09 (Goebel 2021, Mignolet 2026, Chen 2026) provides a plausible *general* IgG-DRG mechanism for SFN in ME/CFS, but the specific TS-HDS/FGFR3 serological markers are NOT validated in that axis and have a contested, low-certainty evidence base of their own. The narrative should present TS-HDS/FGFR3 seropositivity as a **proposed but unvalidated subtype hypothesis**, explicitly citing the negative real-world and reproducibility data — NOT as an established diagnostic entity.

## Cohort-overlap verdict
- Levine/Zeidman/Kafaie (Phoenix, Saint-Louis) and Zeidman (Loyola/Henry Ford) and Malik/Pestronk (WashU/Saint-Louis) share a Washington University / Saint-Louis antibody-testing lineage (the WashU sensory-neuropathy panel). These supportive papers draw on overlapping methodology and referral pools — count as one supporting lineage.
- Salih/Moutal/Kafaie (Saint-Louis) overlaps Kafaie above AND the Saint-Etienne FGFR3 group (Camdessanché/Antoine/Moritz/Tholance), who co-author both the discovery paper and the reproducibility study — a single Franco-US FGFR3 lineage.
- Independent of the supportive lineage: Chompoopong/Dubey (Mayo), Trevino/Novak (Brigham), Gibbons (BIDMC/Harvard), Geerts (Maastricht), Ng (Singapore), Morelli/Giannoccaro (Bologna), Nissan (Sheba Israel), Katzberg (Toronto).
- Verdict: the supportive papers are **concentrated in one laboratory/testing lineage**; the challenge papers come from **multiple independent centres**. This asymmetry strengthens the challenge side.

## Overall verdict for Phase 2
Treat the anti-TS-HDS / anti-FGFR3 "autoimmune SFN subtype" as a **LOW-CERTAINTY, PROPOSED-but-unvalidated hypothesis** — honest certainty ~0.25–0.35 at best, and only as a *serological association / subgroup descriptor*, NOT a diagnostic biomarker or a validated IVIG-selection tool. Expect Phase 2 to frame this in ch09 as open-question/speculation territory with an explicit limitation note naming the negative RCTs and the reproducibility/specificity studies. Any clinical-implication claim (e.g. IVIG candidacy by serostatus) is not supported by the current evidence.
