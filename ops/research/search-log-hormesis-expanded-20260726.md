## Biochemical Connections to ME/CFS-Relevant Mechanisms

1. **Catecholamine inverted-U** → modafinil, methylphenidate, duloxetine, atomoxetine dosing
   - D1 receptor inverted-U explains why 200mg modafinil works but 400mg worsens (Randall 2005)
   - Alpha-2A/D1 balance determines optimal PFC function

2. **Yerkes-Dodson / stress biology** → beta-blocker dosing for POTS
   - Low-dose beta-blockers may optimize autonomic function while higher dose impairs

3. **mTORC1/mTORC2 dose selectivity** → rapamycin/sirolimus dosing
   - Low-dose intermittent rapamycin: mTORC1 inhibition (beneficial) > mTORC2 inhibition (harmful)
   - High-dose chronic: mTORC2 inhibition → insulin resistance, impaired immune function

4. **GABA-A biphasic** → allopregnanolone/brexanolone dosing
   - Low concentrations paradoxically worsen mood/anxiety
   - Higher concentrations produce sedation
   - Directly relevant to allopregnanolone trials for ME/CFS/PASC

5. **General hormesis framework** → all medications
   - NF2 activation as generalized mediator
   - 30-60% stimulation above baseline, ~10-20 fold dose window
   - Argues that biphasic dose-response is the default, not the exception
