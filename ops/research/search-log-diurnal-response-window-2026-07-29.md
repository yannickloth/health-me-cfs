## Search Limitations

1. **Database coverage:** PubMed only. Google Scholar not systematically searched (rate-limited API).
2. **Date range:** No date restriction; found papers 2013–2026.
3. **Language:** English only (PubMed default). One Russian paper excluded.
4. **Grey literature:** Not searched (conference abstracts, preprints unless indexed in PubMed).
5. **MeSH indexing:** Newer papers (2024–2026) may not be fully indexed — keyword searches may miss some.
6. **Core gap:** No MeSH term exists for "diurnal response window" or "pharmacodiagnostic probe" — all searches keyword-based. The concept itself is novel.
7. **LDN timing:** Zero PubMed results for LDN timing studies in ME/CFS — this is a genuine literature gap, not a search failure.
8. **Circadian × drug response × ME/CFS intersection:** Essentially zero directly relevant papers — the topic is novel. All evidence is from adjacent fields (general chronopharmacology, autoimmune chronotherapy, ME/CFS HPA axis studies).
