## Primary Paper

### Fleury ST et al. (2026) — Encapsulated Leptin-Producing Cells Facilitate Entrainment of Circadian Rhythms in Rodents and Nonhuman Primates
- **Journal:** Advanced Science, article e77191 (2026 Aug 13)
- **DOI:** 10.1002/advs.77191 · **PMID:** 42593855
- **Authors:** Fleury ST, Lin X, Rios PD, Olker C, Song EJ, Perez AC, Fell C, Lopez D, Joshi I, Nasir H, Curtis C, Muringi D, Wang K, Oberholzer J, Turek FW, Hilton IB, Rivnay J, Vitaterna MH, Veiseh O
- **Design:** Preclinical. Encapsulated human RPE cells engineered to constitutively produce leptin ("leptin factories"); subcutaneous injection in mice and diurnal cynomolgus macaques.
- **Key findings:**
  - Subcutaneous leptin-factory injection produces sustained blood-leptin elevation >1 day.
  - Accelerates re-entrainment to BOTH phase delays and phase advances (the clinically relevant jet-lag/shift-work scenario).
  - In diurnal cynomolgus macaques: safe, reduces entrainment time by ~1 day.
  - No adverse sleep effect: increases NREM slow-wave energy.
- **Conclusion:** Metabolic (leptin) regulation is a novel lever for accelerating circadian adaptation to schedule change.
- **Limitations:** Preclinical (mouse + NHP); no human cohort; no ME/CFS data; entrainment-speed endpoint, not clinical symptom endpoint.

## Null / negative papers

1. **Cleare 2001** — Baseline plasma leptin NOT significantly different between CFS (n=32) and matched controls. Hydrocortisone (10 mg) raised leptin, and the rise was greater in treatment responders — but this reflects glucocorticoid sensitivity, not a leptin-CFS causal signal. **The only completed, controlled human measurement of leptin in CFS is null at baseline.**
2. **Musker 2021** — A systematic review *protocol* (PROSPERO CRD42020169903). No results published yet. Counts as null/gap: there is NO completed meta-analysis of circulating leptin in ME/CFS.

## Competing evidence

- **Cambras 2026** — Light exposure patterns (not metabolic intervention) are the dominant environmental circadian modulator in ME/CFS; nocturnal/rhythm-instability light pattern (PC2) associated with higher VCAM-1 + triglycerides and lower serotonin. Positions *light* (zeitgeber) as the primary circadian lever in ME/CFS, not metabolic/leptin signalling.
- **Williams 2002** (already in bib) — melatonin and phototherapy produced NO symptomatic improvement in CFS circadian disorders — cautions that even direct circadian interventions may not translate to ME/CFS symptom relief.

## Indirect biochemical links (topic mechanism ↔ ME/CFS)

1. **Leptin→SCN entrainment circuit (Tang 2023; Faber 2021):** DMH leptin-receptor (LepR) neurons project to the SCN and can shift clock phase — establishes the *molecular substrate* by which metabolic state (leptin) can gate circadian entrainment. This is the exact pathway Fleury 2026 therapeutically engages.
2. **Circadian-gated leptin signalling (Osorio-Mendoza 2025):** the clock reciprocally gates leptin's homeostatic/hedonic feeding actions — a bidirectional metabolic↔circadian coupling, not one-way.
3. **Elevated leptin in ME/CFS (Domingo 2024):** ME/CFS patients show *higher* circulating leptin than controls — so the "leptin factory" approach would *raise* a hormone already elevated in ME/CFS, and the target pathway (leptin sensitivity/entrainment) may be desensitized in ME/CFS (leptin-resistance analogy). This is a critical caution: the Fleury lever and the ME/CFS direction-of-effect may be opposite.
4. **Chronobiological neuroendocrine framework (Thomas 2026, already in bib):** ME/CFS and Long COVID show neuroendocrine dysregulation with underexplored circadian/menstrual dynamics — provides the integration rationale that timing-structured hormone measurement is needed to detect a leptin-circadian signal in ME/CFS.

## Integration Notes

- Primary relevance is INDIRECT/mechanistic: no ME/CFS cohort, no clinical translation claim. Frame as `speculation`/`open-question`, never `clinical-finding`.
- The single most important caution for the main session: **leptin is already ELEVATED in ME/CFS (Domingo 2024) and baseline-null in the only controlled CFS measurement (Cleare 2001)** — a "leptin factory" would push a hormone in the *opposite* direction of a plausible therapeutic target, and the target tissue may be leptin-resistant.
- Integration environment types: trigger + mechanism papers → `speculation`; Domingo 2024 / Cambras 2026 → `achievement`/`clinical-finding` (direct ME/CFS data); Cleare 2001 → `limitation`/`null`; Mukherjee 2026 → `warning` (technology-safety precedent).
