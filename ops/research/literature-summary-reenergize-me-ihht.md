# Literature Summary: REenergizeME — Intermittent Hypoxia-Hyperoxia Treatment (IHHT) in ME/CFS

Date: 2026-08-29 | Stream: `reenergize-me-ihht`
Mode: MIXED /integrate-topic | Trigger: Nochi et al. 2026 BMJ Open 16:e117729 (REenergizeME protocol, NCT07317401)

## 1. PRIMARY / ANCHOR

### Nochi M, Kristensen SK, Mehrani N, et al. BMJ Open 2026;16:e117729. (REenergizeME protocol)
- **Design**: Randomised, placebo-controlled, participant- and assessor-blinded RCT protocol. 104 female ME/CFS (ICC), 20-59 y, mild-moderate (NICE), PEM documented, >6 months. 1:1 IHHT vs sham. 20 healthy controls (baseline comparisons). 6 treatment sessions over 8 weeks (6-8 day spacing). HypoxBreath device (Telmed, CE class IIa); 4-7 alternating hypoxia-hyperoxia cycles, 22-40 min; SpO2 9-13% hypoxic, 36% hyperoxic. Sham = room air 21% with mimicked airflow/acoustic cues.
- **Primary endpoint**: SF-36 vitality domain change (ANCOVA; effect assumption +20.5 vs placebo +6.8, between-group 13.7 — derived from a **non-controlled long-COVID IHHT cohort**, i.e. Kapel2025).
- **Secondary**: FSS, FUNCAP-27, COMPASS-31, painDETECT; d2-R; NIRS muscle oxygenation; handgrip; NLT orthostatic; Valsalva; Sudoscan; thermal QST; corneal confocal microscopy; skin biopsy (IENFD, VIP/DbH, macrophages, mitochondria, microvasculature); PBMC flux, oxidative/inflammatory markers, metabolomic/proteomic/transcriptomic.
- **Safety**: low risk; transient headache, short-lived fatigue; PEM >48h = AE; continuous SpO2/HR monitoring; exclusions (pregnancy, obstructive lung disease, claustrophobia, pneumothorax, interfering devices); no independent DMC.
- **Limitations (self-declared)**: dose not ME/CFS-validated; bedbound/very-severe underrepresented; female-only; sample-size from non-controlled data; sham masking uncertain; not powered for subgroups.
- **Timeline**: recruitment 1 Jun 2026; completion Jan 2029. Ethics: Danish Scientific Ethical Committees no. 2500959.
- **NOTE (cohort/provenance overlap)**: REenergizeME PI/corresponding author Olsen RJ and co-author Stokholm/Foldager also author the Kapel2025 non-controlled IHHT cohort used for the power calculation. Stokholm, Elmengaard, Foldager are co-founders/shareholders of SANA Medical Systems (SANA® Therapy / HypoxBreath®). Same Danish team + same device + same protocol → material COI/provenance overlap (see §6).

**Cert**: raw 0.95 (well-designed, top-peer-reviewed RCT protocol) × 1.00 ME/CFS weight = **discounted 0.95** (protocol only — results pending 2029; informational, not therapeutic efficacy).

## 2. DIRECT ME/CFS IHHT STUDIES
- **None exist.** Confirmed research gap. Only the REenergizeME protocol itself (anchor). No pilot, no results, no completed trial.

## 3. IHHT / INTERMITTENT HYPOXIA IN LONG COVID / POST-VIRAL FATIGUE (the 3 anchor refs 39-41)

### Doehner2024IHHTLongCovid — J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle 15:2781-91 (ref 39)
- **Design**: prospective, controlled, **open-treatment** (non-randomized) pilot. 145 long-COVID inpatients (74% female, mean 53 y) in inpatient rehab; 70 IHHT (10-12% O2 / 30-35% hyperoxic, 3×/week) + standard rehab vs 75 standard rehab.
- **Findings**: 6MWT improved 2.8× vs control (91.7 vs 32.6 m, ANCOVA p<0.001); stair-climbing power 3.7× (-1.91 vs -0.51 s); dyspnoea, fatigue (FAS), QoL (PGA/EQ-5D/MCRS) improved; BP/HR decreased, haemoglobin increased (control unchanged). **No adverse events.**
- **Cohort**: Germany (Charité Berlin / Median Klinikum). Independent of Danish REenergizeME team. No COI flagged on IHHT device.
- **Cert**: raw 0.60 (controlled but non-randomized open) × 0.80 post-viral/long-COVID overlap weight = **discounted 0.48**.

### Kapel2025IHHC — J Clin Med 14(5):1590 (ref 40)
- **Design**: open-label, **non-controlled** cohort of SANA® Therapy (individualized algorithm-based IHHC). 199 PCC patients (11-87 y, 67% female), moderate-severe fatigue, 2020-2023. Mean 6 sessions (2-21); hypoxia 9-13% O2 3-8 min, hyperoxia 34-36% 1-3 min. Primary: NRS pain + SF-36 at 6 wks and 6 mo.
- **Findings**: SF-36 +102 pts (p<0.001) at 6 wks, +106 at 6 mo; pain reduced 28-32% (above MCID). **No serious AEs** (self-reported safe).
- **Cohort**: Denmark, **SANA Medical Systems** (authors Stokholm, Elmengaard, Foldager are co-founders/shareholders). Overlaps REenergizeME team/device/protocol — see §6. No placebo control.
- **Cert**: raw 0.35 (open, no control, commercial-source data, small effect-size basis) × 0.80 post-viral = **discounted 0.28**.

### Zha2024IntermittentHypoxia — Respir Med 232:107763 (ref 41)
- **Design**: prospective **randomized controlled** study (normoxia comparator + routine therapy). 95 PASC patients; 47 intermittent-hypoxia-exposure (IHE; 5 min hypoxia / 5 min normal air ×5) vs 48 normoxia-exposure (NE); mean 10 days. ChiCTR2300070565.
- **Findings**: 6MWD, FVC, FEV1, VT, dyspnoea (Borg/mMRC), fatigue (FAS/CFQ-11) improved after IHE and greater than NE (all p<0.05); ≥10 days > <10 days. **No severe AEs.**
- **NOTE**: this is **intermittent hypoxia (IHE), not IHHT** (no hyperoxic phase) — the protocol's "one study using intermittent hypoxia." Controlled but normoxia, not blinded sham.
- **Cohort**: China (Wuhan). Independent of Danish team.
- **Cert**: raw 0.55 (RCT but normoxia comparator, non-blinded, single center) × 0.80 post-viral = **discounted 0.44**.

### IHHT long-COVID RCT — **none published.** The 3 above are the complete set. No blinded sham-controlled IHHT RCT in long COVID exists.

## 4. IHHT IN OTHER POPULATIONS (safety + physiology)

### Serebrovska2019IHHTvsIHT — High Alt Med Biol 20(4):383-91 (prediabetes)
- Placebo-controlled RCT, 55 prediabetic (51-74 y): 17 IHHT (5 min 12% O2 / 3 min 33% O2), 22 IHT (hypoxia/normoxia), 16 sham. 3 weeks, 5×/week.
- IHHT = IHT on fasting glucose, OGTT, LDL, cholesterol; equal improvement in hypoxic-test SpO2; persisted 1 mo. **No AEs reported.**
- Cert: raw 0.55 × 0.60 non-ME/CFS clinical = **discounted 0.33**.

### Bestavashvili2022IHHEmetabolic — Biomedicines 10(3):566 (metabolic syndrome)
- RCT, 65 MS patients: IHHE (3-wk, 5×/wk, 45 min) vs sham (room air). Improved SBP/DBP, lipid profile, cardiometabolic profile vs control. **No serious AEs.**
- Cert: raw 0.55 × 0.60 = **discounted 0.33**.

### Afina2021IHHTLipidInflammation — Front Cardiovasc Med 8:700826 (metabolic syndrome)
- **SAME cohort/trial as Bestavashvili2022** (Sechenov, n=65, NCT04791397) — companion analysis of lipids + inflammation (hs-CRP, galectin-3, Hsp70, NTproBNP, ALT/AST). Improved lipid profile, anti-inflammatory markers. **No serious AEs.** Flag as same-cohort companion (do not double-count).
- Cert: raw 0.50 × 0.60 = **discounted 0.30** (flagged cohort-overlap with Bestavashvili2022).

## 5. INDIRECT BIOCHEMICAL LINKS (mechanism)

### Zhang2023HypoxiaConditioning — Int J Med Sci 20(12):1551-61 (mechanism review)
- Xuanwu Hospital (leading hypoxic-conditioning center) review. Intermittent hypoxia conditioning hormesis: calibrated mild-moderate hypoxia, appropriate cycles → reduced oxidative stress, inflammation, apoptosis; enhanced hypoxic gene expression (HIF-1α → erythropoiesis, angiogenesis, vascular regulation, metabolism). Multi-organ protective strategy. Establishes the dose-dependence: low-dose hormetic IH = adaptive; excess = harmful.
- **Bridges directly to existing corpus `hypoxia-altitude-hif1a` stream**: IHHT is the controlled ambulatory instantiation of the altitude/HIF-1α preconditioning hypothesis already in the paper.
- Cert: raw 0.65 (authoritative review) × 0.75 general-mechanism = **discounted 0.49**.

### HIF-1α / ME-CFS direct link — **none found.** No direct HIF-1α×ME/CFS study (0 PubMed hits for "HIF-1 alpha myalgic encephalomyelitis"). Link is via mechanism reviews (Zhang2023) + existing corpus hypoxia/hypoxemia content (β2-adrenergic hypoxemia, HBOT downstream, vascular CBF hypoxia) + the pathological contrast (Erdem2026, She2026). IHHT's biochemistry (HIF-1α stabilization → mitochondrial antioxidant/repair) intersects documented ME/CFS pathophysiology (mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, vascular/oxygen-handling impairment) as hypothesized by the protocol, but **no direct empirical confirmation in ME/CFS**.

## 6. HARM — MILD HORMETIC IH vs PATHOLOGICAL CHRONIC IH

### Erdem2026HIF1aOSA — Medicina 62(7):1340 (OSA, humans)
- 52 OSA + 26 controls. Pathological chronic intermittent hypoxia (OSA) → serum HIF-1α, Netrin-1, Netrin-4 **progressively elevated with disease severity** (p<0.0001), independent of BMI/age/CRP/LDL. Demonstrates the pathological pole: sustained/chronic IH → persistent HIF-1α elevation + oxidative stress + inflammation + autonomic/cognitive harm.
- Cert: raw 0.55 × 0.60 general-harm contrast = **discounted 0.33**.

### She2026IHParadigms — Hypertens Res 49(5):1659-72 (rats)
- Experimental demonstration that the **structure/pattern** of intermittent hypoxia (duration + frequency of desaturation-reoxygenation cycles) produces **distinct** cardiovascular + autonomic outcomes even at equal cumulative burden. Directly supports the mild-hormetic-vs-pathological dosing distinction central to IHHT safety (low-frequency, brief cycles vs OSA-like high-frequency sustained desaturation).
- Cert: raw 0.50 (animal) × 0.50 human-relevance = **discounted 0.25**.

### Harm findings summary
- **IHHT as administered (mild hormetic, brief cycles, low frequency)**: reported safe in all human studies (Doehner2024, Zha2024, Kapel2025, Serebrovska2019, Bestavashvili2022, Afina2021). Reported transient AEs: headache, short-lived fatigue (protocol). No serious AEs in any study.
- **Pathological chronic IH (OSA analog)**: documented harm — oxidative stress, sympathetic activation, sustained HIF-1α elevation, inflammation, cognitive impairment (Erdem2026; well-established OSA literature). **Not** the IHHT profile.
- **Key caveat for ME/CFS**: the hormetic window is dose-dependent (Zhang2023); IHHT dose is NOT validated in ME/CFS; PEM exacerbation is a documented risk (protocol AE definition: PEM >48h). Sympathetic/autonomic stress could theoretically be relevant in dysautonomic ME/CFS patients. No ME/CFS-specific harm data exist.

## 7. COHORT-OVERLAP VERDICT (Instruction B)
- **Doehner2024** (Berlin, Germany) — independent. No overlap.
- **Zha2024** (Wuhan, China) — independent. No overlap.
- **Kapel2025 ↔ REenergizeME (Nochi2026)** — **SIGNIFICANT OVERLAP**:
  - Shared investigators: **Olsen RJ** (REenergizeME corresponding author) and **Stokholm**, **Foldager** on both; **Nochi Z** (Danish Pain Research Center, Aarhus — same Aarhus group) on Kapel2025.
  - Shared device/company: SANA Medical Systems (Stokholm/Elmengaard/Foldager co-founders/shareholders); SANA® Therapy uses HypoxBreath®/INVATIO; REenergizeME uses HypoxBreath (Telmed). Same class of device, near-identical protocol (9-13% O2 hypoxia, 34-36% hyperoxia, individualized hormetic dosing).
  - Shared provenance of effect-size: REenergizeME's power calculation (+20.5 SF-36 vitality) is extrapolated from the same team's non-controlled Kapel2025 cohort.
  - **Implication**: REenergizeME is not independent of the non-controlled evidence it uses to justify its design; there is a commercial COI (SANA) in a positive outcome. Discount certainty on the long-COVID IHHT pillar accordingly (Kapel2025 = 0.28).
- **The 3 long-COVID studies do NOT overlap with each other** (Doehner=Germany, Kapel=Denmark, Zha=China). Only Kapel overlaps REenergizeME.

## 9. OVERLAP WITH EXISTING CORPUS (cross-check, not duplicate)
- `hypoxia-altitude-hif1a` stream (bib/vascular.bib): altitude/hypoxia improves ME/CFS via HIF-1α/EPO preconditioning — **IHHT is the controlled ambulatory instantiation of this existing hypothesis**. Consistent, extends into clinical intervention.
- `experimental-hypoxia-virtual-hypoxia-brainstem` (Bader2026, HBOT): IHHT is **distinct from HBOT** (ambient-pressure alternating hypoxia+hyperoxia vs pressurized pure oxygen). Different mechanism (HIF-1α preconditioning vs oxygen delivery), different device, different population evidence base. Both downstream of the virtual-hypoxia/brain-metabolic concept but not the same intervention.
- Existing HBOT entries (Hadanny2024HBOTLongCovid, Mairal2021FDGHBOT, HBOT2025mecfs): do NOT cover IHHT. IHHT is a new treatment stream `reenergize-me-ihht`.
- Existing ME/CFS mitochondrial/oxidative/vascular hypoxia content: IHHT's proposed mechanism intersects, but no direct ME/CFS empirical IHHT data.
