# Fragments — Naik 2026 LDN Post-COVID Fatigue RCT (naik2026-ldn-postcovid-rct)

**Date:** 2026-10-27
**Purpose:** Literal supporting fragments (≥15 chars) for the central integration decision: LDN's first placebo-controlled RCT in post-viral fatigue is NULL.

## Driving claim: LDN does not beat placebo for post-COVID fatigue

**Source:** Naik2026LDNPostCOVIDRCT.pdf (medRxiv preprint), Results/Abstract.

- Primary outcome (verbatim abstract): "there was no difference in the mean FSS score between groups (LDN, 4.2; placebo, 4.4; adjusted mean difference (AMD) -0.76, 95% confidence interval (CI) -4.18 to 2.67)."
- Secondary outcomes (verbatim): "There were also no differences in pain VAS (AMD 0.03, 95% CI -0.50 to 0.57), PQSymp-12 (AMD -0.70, 95% CI -2.57 to 1.17), daily step count (AMD -5, 95% CI -720 to 729), or EQ-5D-5L utility (AMD 0.02, 95% CI -0.03 to 0.06)."
- Conclusion (verbatim): "Compared to placebo, LDN at doses up to 4.5 mg/day for 16 weeks was well tolerated but did not improve fatigue severity in people with PCFS."
- Safety (verbatim): "Adverse events grade 3 or higher were reported in 5 (6.2%) and 3 (3.8%) in the LDN and placebo groups respectively."
- Design (verbatim): "We randomized participants 1:1 to either the LDN or placebo arm." / "We estimated a sample size of 64 participants per arm to detect a 4.7-point difference".

## Prior observational signal (the claim the null RCT undercuts)

**Source:** Naik2026LDNPostCOVIDFatigue.pdf, Introduction (references Polo 2019, O'Kelly 2022 etc.).

- Protocol rationale (Naik2024, verbatim): "only small, non-controlled studies have assessed LDN in PCC, so randomised trials are urgently required."
- Byambasuren 2026 (in corpus): LDN long-COVID meta found "No RCTs identified; 4 observational pre-post studies".
