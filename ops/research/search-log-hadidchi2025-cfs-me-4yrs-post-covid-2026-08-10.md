### Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed original research (cohort studies, case-control studies)
- ME/CFS or post-infectious fatigue/chronic fatigue as primary outcome
- Post-COVID (SARS-CoV-2), post-SARS (SARS-CoV-1), or post-EBV population
- Published 2006–2026
- Large cohort preferred (n > 200)
- English language

### Exclusion Criteria
- Reviews without original data
- Acute COVID-19 studies without follow-up
- Case reports, case series (n < 20)
- Multiple sclerosis-focused studies (MS is a separate disease)
- Non-peer-reviewed preprints (medRxiv) unless no peer-reviewed equivalent exists
- Studies with fatigue-only outcome that do not reference ME/CFS
