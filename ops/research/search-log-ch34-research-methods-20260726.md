## Inclusion Criteria

- Papers specifically addressing ME/CFS research methodology
- Studies comparing diagnostic criteria and their impact on research findings
- Survey/registry studies documenting PEM as participation barrier
- 2-day CPET methodology, reliability, and validity papers
- Meta-epidemiology papers on blinding and subjective outcomes (general + ME/CFS-specific)
- Studies on control group selection and its impact on conclusions
- Machine learning biomarker studies with overfitting analysis
- Subgroup/heterogeneity papers relevant to statistical power
- Cross-criteria replication studies

## Exclusion Criteria

- Pure biomarker discovery papers without methodological analysis
- Clinical treatment papers without research methodology discussion
- Case reports without methodological implications
- ME/CFS pathophysiology papers not touching research methods
- Papers on Long COVID only (unless specifically discussing methodology transfer)
- Pre-ME/CFS papers (pre-1988) unless foundational to research methods
