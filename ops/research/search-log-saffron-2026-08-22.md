## Inclusion Criteria
- Randomized controlled trials of saffron (Crocus sativus) on fatigue, ME/CFS, or chronic fatigue — any population (direct fatigue evidence).
- Studies establishing saffron/tryptophan/serotonin/kynurenine-axis interactions (serotonin reuptake/SERT, tryptophan metabolism, kynurenine pathway, gut-microbial tryptophan metabolites) — the KEY relevance criterion.
- Saffron as a gut-microbiome modulator (prebiotic effects, microbial tryptophan/neurotransmitter metabolism).
- Null/negative saffron RCTs on fatigue or mood.
- Harm/adverse-effect sources: serotonin syndrome risk, SSRI/MAOI interaction, pregnancy safety.
- Mechanism reviews establishing saffron's biochemical profile (SERT inhibition, MAO inhibition, antioxidant via crocin/crocetin).

## Exclusion Criteria
- Papers on saffron only as general depression/anxiety/mood without any fatigue, tryptophan-axis, or microbiome link (relevance criterion not met by general mood alone).
- Saffron agricultural/agronomy papers (corm rot, cultivation, Trichoderma antagonism, rhizosphere) — no human/mechanism relevance.
- Saffron as a food-flavoring/colorant chemistry paper.
- Non-saffron phytotherapy (St. John's wort, valerian, etc.) without saffron-specific data.
- Case reports without mechanism or treatment relevance.
- Duplicate of already-integrated Elbaga2026 (crocin in Alzheimer's rat model, kynurenine-excitotoxicity stream) — kept as cross-reference, not re-added.

## Indirect Biochemical / Systemic Link Logic (documented)

Topic mechanism (what saffron does biochemically):
- Saffron's active principles: crocin, crocetin (carotenoids), safranal (monoterpene aldehyde).
- Serotonergic: crocin/crocetin bind the serotonin transporter (SLC6A4) at the fluoxetine site, increase serotonin; human post-ingestion metabolites inhibit SERT expression (Wauquier 2022; Mohammadi 2023).
- Monoamine oxidase: crocin and safranal inhibit human MAO-A and MAO-B (De Monte 2014).
- Tryptophan/kynurenine: saffron extract reduces serum kynurenine and increases melatonin (De la Fuente Muñoz 2023); safranal downregulates neurotoxic kynurenine-pathway components (Monchaux de Oliveira 2026).
- Microbiome/tryptophan: saffron remodels gut-microbial tryptophan metabolism, increasing tryptamine and indole acetic acid (Horvath 2026); modulates gut-microbiota-sleep axis (Lang 2025).
- Oxidative stress: saffron/crocin have antioxidant activity; protects neurons from oxidative stress (Wauquier 2022).

Indirect search pattern (map these mechanisms into ME/CFS):
- Serotonin reuptake / SERT → "saffron AND SERT/serotonin reuptake" (T1 → Wauquier, Mohammadi).
- Tryptophan/kynurenine → "saffron AND kynurenine" (T3 → De la Fuente Muñoz, Monchaux); "saffron AND tryptophan" (T2).
- Microbial tryptophan → "saffron AND gut microbiome" (M1 → Horvath).
- MAO → "safranal AND MAO" (MECH2 → De Monte).
- Sleep via microbiome → "saffron AND gut-brain" (M1/M2 → Lang).

Result:
- Direct saffron + ME/CFS: 1 PubMed-indexed paper (Dastan 2025), a COPD-comorbid CFS RCT; no PRIMARY ME/CFS saffron trial exists — a genuine gap.
- Direct fatigue RCTs in comorbid chronic-lung populations: Dastan 2025 (COPD), Moghimi Dehkordi 2026 (sarcoidosis).
- Strongest tryptophan/serotonin-axis bridge: saffron modulates SERT (Wauquier 2022 human ex vivo; Mohammadi 2023 animal), reduces kynurenine/increases melatonin (De la Fuente Muñoz 2023), modulates gut-microbial tryptophan (Horvath 2026), and inhibits hMAO (De Monte 2014).
- Critical null: Amadieu 2025 — no effect on fatigue/depression/anxiety in healthy subclinical adults.
- Harm: serotonin-syndrome risk with SSRIs/MAOIs (Căuș 2026), pregnancy uterine-stimulation risk at high dose (Alshdefat 2026).
- Net assessment: saffron has NO primary ME/CFS evidence but has a well-documented tryptophan/serotonin/kynurenine-axis and gut-microbiome-tryptophan mechanism profile — the CONDITIONAL relevance criterion (tryptophan/serotonin axis) IS met, via mechanism-level and comorbid-fatigue RCT evidence, NOT via primary ME/CFS trials.

## Relevance Criterion Reassessment (CONDITIONAL candidate)
The conditional candidate required evidence of relevance to ME/CFS specifically OR to the tryptophan/serotonin axis.
- ME/CFS specifically: NO primary ME/CFS saffron trial exists (0 hits). Only comorbid-secondary-fatigue RCTs (COPD, sarcoidosis).
- Tryptophan/serotonin axis: YES, met via mechanism-level evidence — saffron inhibits SERT (human ex vivo Wauquier 2022; animal Mohammadi 2023), decreases kynurenine and increases melatonin (De la Fuente Muñoz 2023), modulates gut-microbial tryptophan metabolism to tryptamine/indole acetic acid (Horvath 2026), and inhibits human MAO-A/B (De Monte 2014).
- Verdict: relevance criterion MET via the tryptophan/serotonin/kynurenine-axis + microbiome-tryptophan mechanisms, NOT via primary ME/CFS clinical evidence. General mood modulation is present (Dimech, Dastan, Moghimi) but is NOT the basis for proceeding.
