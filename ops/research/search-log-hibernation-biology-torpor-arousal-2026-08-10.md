### Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed journal
- Direct relevance to spontaneous interbout arousal mechanisms
- Molecular pathways: adenosine A1/A2A, eicosanoid/prostaglandin, mTORC1, BAT thermogenesis
- Cross-species transcriptomic/metabolomic hibernation datasets
- Primate hibernation (dwarf lemur)
- Review papers on hibernation molecular biology
- Drug-repurposing angle (adenosine agonists, thermogenic modulators)
- Null/contradicting evidence (arousal oxidative damage, translational limitations)

### Exclusion Criteria
- Preprints / non-peer-reviewed
- Papers solely about daily torpor without molecular arousal data
- papers exclusively about circadian/temperature without arousal mechanisms
- Pure ecological/behavioral papers without molecular data
- Papers pre-2015 (unless foundational)
