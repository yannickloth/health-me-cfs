## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed primary research or definitive review directly addressing at least ONE of:
  (a) the mild/lifetime/constitutional end of the energy spectrum in ME/CFS or chronic fatigue;
  (b) severity stratification and/or underdiagnosis of mild cases;
  (c) whether lifelong low-baseline energy is a distinct predisposing/prodromal trajectory vs acute-onset ME/CFS;
  (d) the "just tired more than expected at baseline" phenotype distinct from PEM.
- Prospective or adequately powered cohort design preferred for risk-factor claims.
- Full bibliographic record (DOI/PMID) verifiable.
- NOT already present in the corpus (checked against `bib/*.bib` before inclusion).

## Exclusion Criteria
- Papers already integrated in prior ADHD/ASD predisposition streams (`adhd-energy-mecfs`, `adhd-asd-comorbidity-mecfs`, `brain-energy-asd-mecfs`, `lower-baseline-predisposition`) — do NOT re-add (e.g. @Jason2015CFSvsME, Kato2006premorbid, Wirth2026prodromalICD, Chu2019onsetPatterns already present).
- Papers on sudden-onset ME/CFS specifically (already covered).
- Long COVID / PASC as the primary outcome where not generalizable to a lifelong-constitution question (scored 0.85 relevance, screened out unless directly on the mild/severity axis).
- Pure treatment/intervention outcomes (creatine, oxaloacetate, pacing RCTs) without a mild-severity/lifetime-constitution insight.
- Case reports, n<20 descriptive series lacking generalizable claim (exception: Smith1991 retained as the sole early pediatric prodrome-question source).
- Off-topic noise returned by broad searches (cirrhosis, dialysis, COVID ICU, craniocervical, etc.).
