## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed studies on adult hippocampal neurogenesis in major depressive disorder (human post-mortem, molecular, single-cell)
- Foundational / controversy papers establishing whether adult human hippocampal neurogenesis persists (Eriksson, Boldrini, Sorrells)
- Mechanistic animal and in-vitro studies linking neurogenesis to stress, HPA axis, and depressive behaviour
- Pattern separation as a neurogenesis-dependent hippocampal function in depression
- Indirect biochemical/systemic links: HPA-axis dysfunction in CFS, post-viral suppression of hippocampal neurogenesis (long COVID)
- Reviews (Anacker, Tartt) that synthesize the neurogenesis-depression framework

## Exclusion Criteria
- Papers on hippocampal neurogenesis in other diseases (Alzheimer's, epilepsy, TBI) without depression relevance
- Neurogenesis in adult neurogenic niches other than hippocampus (e.g., olfactory bulb) unless hippocampal
- Pure pharmacology of specific antidepressants without neurogenesis-mechanism relevance
- Treatment-efficacy-only trials with no neurogenesis mechanism
- Animal models of unrelated psychiatric conditions (schizophrenia, autism) without hippocampal-neurogenesis-depression link
- Duplicate / overlapping same-group reviews already covered by an included review

## Indirect Biochemical / Systemic Link Logic (documented)

Topic mechanism (what adult hippocampal neurogenesis does biochemically):
- Adult hippocampal neurogenesis is a BDNF-dependent process; newborn dentate gyrus neurons integrate into the trisynaptic memory circuit (DG → CA3 → CA1).
- Newborn neurons mediate pattern separation (discriminating similar experiences) and cognitive flexibility.
- Neurogenesis provides hippocampal negative feedback on the HPA axis (via glucocorticoid receptors); reduced neurogenesis impairs HPA-axis recovery after stress.
- Neurogenesis is suppressed by stress, glucocorticoids, and neuroinflammation (microglial reactivity, elevated CCL11, interferon signaling).
- Synaptic plasticity and energy metabolism are required for neurogenesis; their failure accompanies stalled neurogenesis.

Indirect search pattern (map these mechanisms into ME/CFS):
- BDNF signaling → "BDNF chronic fatigue syndrome", "BDNF myalgic encephalomyelitis" (M2, M6)
- Hippocampal structure → "hippocampus chronic fatigue syndrome", "hippocampal volume reduction ME/CFS" (M1, M3)
- Neuroinflammation → "hippocampal neurogenesis chronic fatigue" (indirect via Nakatomi 2014 neuroinflammation PET; covered in general neuroinflammation stream, not re-included here)
- HPA-axis modulation → "HPA axis chronic fatigue syndrome meta-analysis" (M5 → Papadopoulos 2011)
- Post-viral suppression of neurogenesis → "neurogenesis long covid OR SARS-CoV-2 hippocampus" (I2 → Fernández-Castañeda 2022)
