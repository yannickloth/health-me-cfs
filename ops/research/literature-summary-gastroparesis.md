# Literature Summary: Gastroparesis and Delayed Gastric Emptying in ME/CFS

**Date:** 2026-08-19
**Agent:** literature-integrator (Phase 1)
**Topic type:** Mechanism + diagnostic + treatment (severe-case integration)

## Context

Autonomic (vagal) gastric-motility failure. Direct ME/CFS evidence is sparse but present (Burnet 2004; already-integrated Vasquez-Marti 2023 / Steinsvik 2023). The topic is anchored by the mechanistic bridge from vagal/cholinergic denervation (Acanfora 2026, already integrated in the vagal-gastric Long COVID stream), enteric neuropathy / ICC loss (Grover 2011, Wang 2009), and the POTS / hypermobility overlap. This summary documents 13 papers across all mandatory search categories.

## Per-Paper Quality + Population-Relevance Discount

Discount weights: ME/CFS 1.00, Long COVID/post-viral 0.85, fibromyalgia/POTS/hypermobility 0.80, general 0.75, animal 0.50, in vitro 0.40, in silico 0.30. `discounted = round(raw × weight, 2)`, min 0.05.

### DIRECT (ME/CFS)

**1. Burnet 2004 — Gastric emptying is slow in chronic fatigue syndrome** (BMC Gastroenterol 4:32)
- n=32 CFS + 45 controls (questionnaire); 32 underwent radionuclide oesophageal clearance + simultaneous liquid/solid gastric emptying vs historical controls.
- 23/32 delayed liquid emptying; 12/32 delayed solid emptying; delay significantly correlated with mean symptom score (p $<$ 0.001). Nocturnal diarrhoea newly reported.
- Diagnostic criteria: pre-IOM/Fukuda-era CFS (criteria not stated in abstract; assumed CDC 1994).
- **Raw cert 0.60 | ME/CFS weight 1.00 | Discounted 0.60.** Peer-reviewed; n=32 moderate; direct target population; single study, not replicated as a cohort.

**2. Corrado 1998 — Normal gastric emptying time and myoelectrical activity in an adolescent with CFS** (Ital J Gastroenterol Hepatol 30(4):444-445)
- n=1 adolescent CFS; **normal** gastric emptying time and normal myoelectrical activity.
- **Null paper.** n=1 case report; low evidentiary weight; does not contradict the larger Burnet cohort.
- **Raw cert 0.40 | ME/CFS weight 1.00 | Discounted 0.40.**

### COMPETING MECHANISM (gastroparesis vs functional dyspepsia)

**3. Debourdeau 2024 — Gastric volumetry for fundic compliance and visceral hypersensitivity in gastroparesis** (Scand J Gastroenterol 59(3):254-259)
- Retrospective comparative; gastroparesis patients; volumetric assessment of fundic compliance + visceral hypersensitivity. Refines gastroparesis as partly an accommodation / visceral-hypersensitivity disorder (overlap with functional dyspepsia).
- **Raw cert 0.50 | general weight 0.75 | Discounted 0.38.** Peer-reviewed; retrospective; n small/unstated; overlaps the already-integrated functional-dyspepsia pattern in ME/CFS (Steinsvik 2023).

### DIAGNOSTIC

**4. Wise 2021 — Gastric Emptying Scans: Poor Adherence to National Guidelines** (Dig Dis Sci 66(9):2897-2906)
- Documents widespread non-adherence to standardized gastric-emptying scintigraphy (GES) protocols (4-hour imaging, meal standardization) — a methodological caution for interpreting diagnostic gastric-emptying studies in ME/CFS and elsewhere.
- **Raw cert 0.60 | general weight 0.75 | Discounted 0.45.** Peer-reviewed; multi-centre chart audit; relevant to diagnostic-pathway rigor.

### TREATMENT (prokinetics / anti-nausea / dietary)

**5. Ingrosso 2023 — Efficacy and Safety of Drugs for Gastroparesis: Systematic Review and Network Meta-analysis** (Gastroenterology 164(4):642-654)
- NMA of prokinetic/anti-nausea drugs for gastroparesis. Best evidence base for metoclopramide, domperidone, erythromycin, prucalopride, relamorelin; includes adverse-effect/safety comparisons. Camilleri (PI) is a lead author — the pre-eminent gastroparesis researcher.
- **Raw cert 0.80 | general weight 0.75 | Discounted 0.60.** Top journal; NMA; replicated method; general gastroparesis population (not ME/CFS).

**6. Patel 2024 — Safety and Efficacy of Highly Selective 5-HT4 Agonists for Diabetic and Idiopathic Gastroparesis: SR/Meta of RCTs** (Cureus 16(1):e51851)
- Meta-analysis of RCTs of 5-HT4 agonists (prucalopride, velusetrag, etc.) for gastroparesis; efficacy + safety (no excess cardiovascular/QT signals in pooled data).
- **Raw cert 0.65 | general weight 0.75 | Discounted 0.49.** Meta-analysis but Cureus (lower-tier journal); general gastroparesis.

**7. Gupta 2016 — Diet and Complementary Medicine for Chronic Unexplained Nausea and Vomiting and Gastroparesis** (Curr Treat Options Gastroenterol 14(4):401-409)
- Dietary (small-frequent meals, low-fat/low-fibre, liquid-nutrient optimisation) + complementary-medicine approaches for gastroparesis and chronic unexplained nausea/vomiting. Directly relevant to nutritional-management of a severe case (feeding strategies).
- **Raw cert 0.50 | general weight 0.75 | Discounted 0.38.** Peer-reviewed clinical review.

### MECHANISM (enteric neuropathy / ICC loss)

**8. Grover 2011 — Cellular changes in diabetic and idiopathic gastroparesis** (Gastroenterology 140(5):1575-85.e8)
- Human gastric antral tissue: loss of ICC, decreased enteric nerve fibres, altered macrophages in diabetic and idiopathic gastroparesis. Definitive mechanistic evidence that gastroparesis involves enteric neuronal + ICC loss, not merely functional vagal failure.
- **Raw cert 0.70 | general weight 0.75 | Discounted 0.52.** Top journal; multicentre tissue cohort; cross-disease (diabetic/idiopathic gastroparesis).

**9. Wang 2009 — Loss of intramuscular and submuscular ICC and enteric nerves related to decreased gastric emptying in streptozotocin-induced diabetes** (Neurogastroenterol Motil 21(10):1095-e92)
- Rodent model: streptozotocin diabetes → loss of ICC + enteric nerves → decreased gastric emptying. Mechanistic support for ICC/enteric-neuron loss as a cause of delayed emptying (causal, interventional).
- **Raw cert 0.60 | animal weight 0.50 | Discounted 0.30.** Animal model; cross-species inference.

### POTS / AUTONOMIC / HYPERMOBILITY OVERLAP

**10. Tseng 2019 — Factors Associated With Use of Nonoral Nutrition and Hydration Support in Adult Patients With POTS** (JPEN 43(6):734-741)
- POTS cohort (Mayo Clinic): identifies factors (incl. GI dysmotility / feeding intolerance) associated with need for nonoral nutrition — documents that autonomic cohorts require enteral/parenteral support, directly relevant to severe ME/CFS nutritional-failure risk.
- **Raw cert 0.55 | POTS weight 0.80 | Discounted 0.44.** Peer-reviewed; POTS population (shared features).

**11. Aziz 2025 — AGA Clinical Practice Update on GI Manifestations and Autonomic or Immune Dysfunction in Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome** (Clin Gastroenterol Hepatol 23(8):1291-1302)
- Expert consensus (AGA CPU) on GI dysmotility, gastroparesis, and autonomic/immune dysfunction in hEDS (with POTS overlap). Practical diagnostic + management framework transferable to the ME/CFS-hEDS-POTS severe case.
- **Raw cert 0.70 | POTS/hEDS weight 0.80 | Discounted 0.56.** Top-journal expert practice update; hEDS/POTS population.

### HARM (prokinetic safety)

**12. Shakhatreh 2019 — Metoclopramide for the treatment of diabetic gastroparesis** (Expert Rev Gastroenterol Hepatol 13(8):711-721)
- Review of metoclopramide (only FDA-approved drug for gastroparesis). Documents TD risk (black-box warning for use over 12 weeks, TD may be irreversible), drowsiness, restlessness, hyperprolactinaemia. Highlights the chronic-use safety conflict for gastroparesis.
- **Raw cert 0.60 | general weight 0.75 | Discounted 0.45.** Peer-reviewed expert review; general gastroparesis; primary harm source.

### POST-VIRAL / LONG COVID

**13. Montalvo 2022 — Autoimmune gastrointestinal dysmotility following SARS-CoV-2 infection successfully treated with IVIG** (Neurogastroenterol Motil 34(7):e14314)
- Case report (17-yo): after SARS-CoV-2, intractable nausea/early satiety, delayed gastric emptying on scintigraphy, slowed small-bowel transit, α3 nicotinic ganglionic AChR + anti-striational antibodies, sudomotor impairment. Dramatic response to IVIG; transit + sweat test improved. First report of post-SARS-CoV-2 AGID.
- **Raw cert 0.45 | Long COVID/post-viral weight 0.85 | Discounted 0.38.** Peer-reviewed case report (n=1); strong mechanistic plausibility (autoimmune ganglionic AChR blockade → gastric dysmotility); reversible/treatable.

## Evidence Landscape

- **Strong (discounted ≥ 0.52):** Ingrosso 2023 (0.60), Burnet 2004 (0.60), Aziz 2025 (0.56), Grover 2011 (0.52).
- **Medium (0.40–0.51):** Patel 2024 (0.49), Wise 2021 (0.45), Shakhatreh 2019 (0.45), Tseng 2019 (0.44), Corrado 1998 (0.40).
- **Weak (< 0.40):** Debourdeau 2024 (0.38), Gupta 2016 (0.38), Montalvo 2022 (0.38), Wang 2009 (0.30).
- **Missing:** No ME/CFS-specific gastric-emptying scintigraphy cohort using modern IOM/ICC criteria (all direct ME/CFS studies are Fukuda-era). No ME/CFS-specific prokinetic trial. No ME/CFS gastric histology (ICC/enteric nerve). These are gaps, not failures.
- **Null:** Corrado 1998 (n=1 normal emptying in CFS) — isolated, low weight.
