## Exclusion Criteria
1. Not peer-reviewed and preprint with <2 citations
2. Citizen science in non-health domains (ecology, wildlife, astronomy, environmental monitoring)
3. Clinical/biological ME/CFS research without patient-knowledge framing
4. Pre-2015 publications (except foundational works like Fricker 2007)
5. Studies on patient participation in clinical care (not knowledge production/research)
6. Opinion pieces without systematic argumentation
7. Non-English without English abstract
8. Duplicates across search domains
