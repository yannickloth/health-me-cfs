## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed journal article or systematic review with verifiable PMID/DOI.
- Directly addresses the drug-probe / diagnostic angle: pyridostigmine (C1), H1/H2/CysLT1/PAF mediator arms (C2, D1, D3, D4), non-response/N-of-1 methodology (C3), histamine-arousal (C4, D2).
- OR an indirect-biochemical link: histamine→TRPV1 sensitization (D1), histamine→orexin arousal relay (D2), PAF→platelet/microclot (D3), montelukast BBB/CysLT1 CNS (D4).
- Null/negative and harm evidence explicitly included (Abbotts2025 null; montelukast neuropsychiatric harm).

## Exclusion Criteria

- Papers whose topic is already covered by existing corpus bib entries (MCAS criteria Afrin2017MCAS/Valent2021criteria/Gulen2024mcascriteria; M3 AAb in ME/CFS Loebel2016/Bynke2020; microclot KrugerMicroclot2022longcovid/Pretorius2023triple; orexin Gaykema2009orexinSickness/LopezAmador2025orexin; TRPV1 Sarvaiya2016TRPV1CFS).
- Case reports of gastroparesis/HVM where pyridostigmine is incidental (Dahiya2021).
- PAF/microclot papers on unrelated disease (Ginkgo PAF review, E. coli meningitis) unless they establish the PAF-receptor or CysLT1-BBB mechanism.
- Mirtazapine Cochrane (antidepressant with H1 activity, not a histamine-probe).
- Preprints without peer review (only Pretorius2023triple already in corpus is preprint; not re-added).

## Key Negative / Research-Gap Findings

- **D5 (mast-cell degranulation energy drain): zero direct literature.** "mast cell degranulation energy metabolism fatigue" = 0 hits. The metabolic-cost-of-degranulation angle has no published support. Closest indirect evidence is NiijimaYaoita2012 (histamine favours endurance) + Abbotts2025 (histamine blockade null for exercise) — both concern *histamine*, not mast-cell ATP/Ca²⁺ cost.
- **C3 (MCAS non-response fingerprinting): no direct literature.** Non-response/negative-control classification is not an established research area in MCAS; only generic N-of-1 methodology (Ortiz2026) and a histamine null (Abbotts2025) anchor it.
- **D4 (montelukast brain-fog probe): no clinical trial.** "montelukast cognition brain fog" = 0 hits. CNS penetration and CysLT1-BBB neuroinflammation are supported mechanistically (Fong2025, Zhou2019), but no clinical brain-fog response data.
- **D3 (PAF→microclot): no direct PAF-microclot paper.** PAF receptor pharmacology (Hung2025) and microclot composition (Baker2023, KrugerMicroclot2022longcovid in corpus) exist separately; the specific "rupatadine PAF arm reduces microclots" claim is untested.
