- Population: long COVID (SARS-CoV-2 post-acute sequelae), ME/CFS, or post-infectious
  syndromes, with immune-profiling / biomarker / treatment content relevant to the
  long-COVID → ME/CFS convergence thesis.
- Addresses one of the three novel threads (long-COVID immune profiling, long-COVID →
  ME/CFS immune convergence, immunotherapy treatment angle).

## Key negative / coverage gaps

1. **"Long COVID vs ME/CFS immune differences" as a direct search phrase returns zero
   PubMed hits.** The comparison literature exists but is indexed under broader terms
   (recovered via "immune overlap" and author searches). The comparison field is
   dominated by small single-center Bulgarian cross-sectionals (Petrov/Ivanovska,
   Maes-collaborated) and narrative reviews — no large multi-site head-to-head
   immune-profiling trial of long COVID vs ME/CFS yet exists.
2. **Immunotherapy (checkpoint blockade, CAR-T) for ME/CFS:** no ME/CFS-specific
   trial exists. The treatment angle is restricted to IVIG/rituximab/TPE (Kaplan2026)
   with negative phase III/II results in unselected populations. The essay's
   checkpoint-inhibitor/CAR-T framing has no ME/CFS evidence base — correctly flagged
   as speculation only, gated by the existing autoimmune-risk limitation in ch08.
3. **AlphaFold/AI in immunology:** no ME/CFS-relevant structural immunology paper found.
   Deferred (methodological, not pathophysiology).
