## The news case (event documentation)

**Arnaud Denis**, French actor and stage director, died 2026-09-22 by euthanasia in Belgium (Namur), age 43, surrounded by family. Core facts verified across ≥4 independent national outlets (franceinfo/Radio France investigation cell; La Croix; RTL — lawyer Philippe Courtois via AFP; 20minutes).

- 2023: inguinal hernia repair with polypropylene mesh. Rapidly severe, unremitting pain thereafter ("comme des coups de poignard, des espèces de décharges électriques" — his words). Severe disability, marked weight loss.
- Whistleblower: founded Facebook group "Victimes de prothèses de hernies" (6,000+ members); challenged informed consent, mesh materials, and follow-up care; ANSM adverse-event reports rose following his advocacy; his criminal complaint was dismissed (classement sans suite — he called it "une humiliation"); he joined the collective action of lawyer Pierre Debuisson.
- Surgeon societies' counterview (SFCD/SFCP/CH/CNPCVD communiqué 2025, quoted by franceinfo): mesh repair is the internationally recommended standard; chronic pain occurs in 5–10% of patients, disabling in 1–5%; ~200,000 meshes implanted per year in France.
- **ME/CFS link:** 20minutes (2026-09-23) reports he "avait fini par être diagnostiqué d'une Encephalomyélite myalgique" (had ultimately been diagnosed with ME); his final message: "À moins de vivre 6 mois dans le corps d'un malade d'Encéphalomyélite myalgique en état sévère, pour comprendre ce que c'est que l'horreur absolue"; "À tous les malades de l'Encéphalomyélite Myalgique, dont on nie les souffrances dans le corps médical en France et dans le monde. Il y a urgence sanitaire"; "Honte à ceux qui psychiatrisent les malades, au lieu de se tenir au courant de la recherche internationale"; he attributed onset to a post-infectious trigger ("comme c'est le cas chez Arnaud Denis selon lui" — 20minutes' hedge, kept).
- **Epistemic caution:** mesh-complication and ME framings both derive from the same primary source (his testimony). No published evidence establishes that mesh complications caused or contributed to his ME. Any integration must write this as patient-reported attribution, not established causation.

## Included papers

### 1. Opala D, Villiger E, Levenfus I. Severe ME/CFS Leading to Assisted Suicide in a Patient in Her Late 30s: A Case Report. Cureus 2026. PMID 42222634, DOI 10.7759/cureus.109798 (OA, PMC13217520)
- Severe ME/CFS post-EBV; cytokine elevations (IL-2, IL-6, IFN-γ) without a recognized autoimmune/autoinflammatory entity; 2 years multidisciplinary treatment (medical, psychological, rehabilitative) → deterioration + treatment hypersensitivities → pursued assisted suicide.
- **First peer-reviewed case report of assisted death in severe ME/CFS** located in any database searched.
- Tier: case-report (cap 0.45). Raw 0.40 (n=1; peer-reviewed; unreplicated; adequate for design) × population 1.00 (ME/CFS) = **0.40**.
- Replication: not yet replicated. Diagnostic criteria: not stated in abstract (case-level detail not verified) — marked unknown.

### 2. Ahn SH et al. Association between CFS and suicidality among MERS survivors, 2-year follow-up. J Psychiatr Res 2021. PMID 33631632
- Prospective cohort, 63 MERS survivors; 29 with CFS at T1. Suicidality 22.2% → 15.1% → 10.0% over 2 years. CFS at T1 → suicidality RR 7.5 (95% CI 2.4–23.1); adjusted RR 7.6 (95% CI 2.2–26.0), GEE.
- Directly relevant as post-infectious chronic-fatigue precedent (MERS survivors — post-viral population).
- Tier: prospective (cap 0.60). Raw 0.55 (n=20–100; solid journal; prospective+adjusted; single study) × population 0.85 = **0.47**.
- Replication: not independently replicated (small n, wide CI — flag).

### 3. van den Berg V et al. Euthanasia and Physician-Assisted Suicide in Patients With Multiple Geriatric Syndromes. JAMA Intern Med 2021. PMID 33284324
- Dutch RTE case summaries 2013–2019: 53 published cases; none had a life-threatening condition; chronic tiredness in 42%; EAS granted for unbearable suffering from accumulating non-terminal syndromes.
- Context: regulated EAS practice explicitly extends to non-terminal chronic suffering; supports the paper's framing that severe ME patients legally access assisted death in BE/NL while French patients cannot.
- Tier: retrospective (cap 0.60). Raw 0.55 × population 0.75 = **0.41**.

### 4. Rahimian Z et al. What medical conditions lead to a request for euthanasia? A rapid scoping review. Health Sci Rep 2024. PMID 38515545
- PRISMA-ScR; 197/3,323 records; leading categories: terminal cancer 45.4%, Alzheimer/dementia 19.8%, "constant unbearable physical or mental suffering" 19.8%, treatment-resistant mood disorders 12.2%.
- Context: ME/CFS is not a listed category — assisted death for ME/CFS rides the generic "unbearable suffering" category. Honest framing: no epidemiological estimate of ME/CFS EAS exists.
- Tier: systematic-review class (scoping — no quality appraisal; treat as ceiling-limited). Raw 0.60 × population 0.75 = **0.45**.

## Evidence landscape (feeds Phase 2)

- **Strong:** nothing ≥0.60 discounted. This topic has no large-cohort or replicated evidence on ME/CFS→assisted death. 
- **Moderate:** Ahn2021 (0.47), Rahimian2024 (0.45), Opala2026 (0.40), VanDenBerg2021 (0.41).
- **Missing:** any epidemiological count of assisted death among ME/CFS patients; Belgian FCEC condition-level data (gray literature).
- **Null:** none found (no study reports absence of suicidality/elevated risk in severe ME/CFS; existing literature uniformly reports elevated risk).
- **Indirect links:** the topic's evidence base IS indirect by nature (post-viral cohort + policy registry + case report). Documented per search log.
