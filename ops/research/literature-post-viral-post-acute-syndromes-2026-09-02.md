## Paper 1 (NEW): Pridgen & Putrino 2026 — IMC-2 ± Paxlovid case series

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Study design | Single-center, **open-label, prospective case series**, two arms with crossover opportunity. **No placebo, no control, no randomization, not blinded.** |
| Sample | 27 approached; **24 completed** (12 IMC-2-only [IO], 12 IMC-2+Paxlovid [IP]); 3 crossovers (IO→IP). 20 female/4 male. |
| Setting / dates | Single Alabama outpatient LC clinic; data April 2022 – Feb 2024. |
| Regimen | **IMC-2 = valacyclovir + celecoxib** (NOT famciclovir). IO: valacyclovir 1500 mg BID + celecoxib 200 mg BID × 120 d. IP: same 120 d, PLUS Paxlovid days 13–28 (15 d); valacyclovir reduced to 750 mg BID during Paxlovid window. |
| Outcome measures | Primary: Patient Global Impression of Change (PGIC), fatigue, at day 120. Secondary: PGIC brain fog + dysautonomia at 120 d; all three at 305 and 731 d. VAS (10-pt) used in parallel. All **subjective patient-reported only** (no objective/gold-standard instruments). |
| Follow-up | 120, 305, 731 d (durability ~600 d past protocol end). |
| Key results | IP vs IO: PGIC fatigue 2 points higher (p<0.0001, Cohen's d=1.8); VAS IP avg 55.3% greater fatigue reduction (p<0.0001). Dysautonomia & brain fog also favored IP. IP responses durable (no sig. change 120→731 d). |
| Harms/AE | IO: no AEs reported. IP (during 15-d Paxlovid): increased fatigue 40%, dysgeusia/bad taste 86.7%, body aches 26.6%, flu-like 13.3%, depression 13.3%, headache/dizziness 13.3%, diarrhea 13.3%. No dropouts from side effects. Renal/hepatic function checked pre + q6–8 wk; stopped if abnormal. |
| COI / ethics | **Major COI:** W. Pridgen is partner/founder of PridCor Therapeutics, holder of patent covering IMC-2 components. Ethics: IRCM-2025-446 — Institute of Regenerative and Cellular Medicine (for-profit IRB), HHS OHRP-registered. |
| Quality | LOW — small open-label case series, no control, subjective endpoints, major inventor COI, single site. |
| Certainty (raw) | 0.25 |
| Population weight | 0.85 (Long COVID, strong post-infectious overlap with ME/CFS) |
| Discounted certainty | **0.21** |

### Key Findings
- In an open-label sample, adding a 15-day Paxlovid course to 120 days of valacyclovir+celecoxib (IMC-2) was associated with larger self-reported fatigue, brain-fog, and dysautonomia improvement than IMC-2 alone (PGIC fatigue p<0.0001; VAS 55.3% greater reduction).
- Reported benefits appeared durable to ~600 days post-protocol in the IP group.
- Regimen tolerated; minor transient side effects confined to the Paxlovid window (dysgeusia, increased fatigue most common); no discontinuations for side effects.
- Authors recommend a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial as the necessary next step.

### Limitations (as stated by authors + assessor)
- Open-label, no placebo/control → placebo effect and selection bias (patients actively seeking care) unaddressed.
- Pronounced female bias (20/4), limiting generalizability (consistent with LC demographics but relevant to ME/CFS).
- Subjective PGIC/VAS endpoints only; no validated LC instrument, no objective measures.
- Single site, small n (12/arm), retrospective review of prospectively-consented quality-improvement data.
- Major inventor COI (patent-holder author) and for-profit IRB review.
- **Not randomized, not blinded; no demonstration vs placebo — cannot support causal efficacy.**

### Accuracy of the Nature feature's description
- **Drug identity:** Feature's phrasing ("famciclovir+celecoxib or valacyclovir-pair + paxlovid trio") conflates two distinct Pridgen products. The LONG-COVID case series (this paper) uses **valacyclovir + celecoxib (IMC-2)**. **Famciclovir + celecoxib** is Pridgen's **fibromyalgia** combination (Pridgen et al. 2017, J Pain Res; PMID 28260944). The paper itself uses valacyclovir only. Any famciclovir mention in the feature refers to the fibromyalgia program, NOT this long-COVID paper.
- **Phase III fibromyalgia / FDA IND for long-COVID trio:** **NOT verifiable from this paper.** The paper's text mentions neither a fibromyalgia phase III trial nor an FDA IND exemption for the trio. These claims in the feature draw on Pridgen's program context/company activity outside this publication; they should not be attributed to the paper. Flag for Phase-2/main-session verification if the feature's framing is cited.
- **n=24:** accurate (24 completed; 27 approached).

## Harm-search findings (mandatory, treatment topic)

Sources: Drugs.com medically-reviewed labels (celecoxib/Celebrex; valacyclovir/Valtrex; famciclovir/Famvir) + established pharmacology interaction analysis vs the ME/CFS co-prescription list. **Not a substitute for pharmacist/clinical review.** Interaction searches for every listed co-prescription were not individually run as isolated queries; classes flagged below derive from the label contraindications/warnings cross-referenced to each co-prescription's known pathway.

### Per-drug safety
| Drug | Contraindications | Key warnings | Common AE |
|------|-------------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Valacyclovir** (paper dose 1500 mg BID = 3000 mg/d — high, within acute-zoster range) | Hypersensitivity to valacyclovir/acyclovir | **Nephrotoxicity** (esp. with renal impairment + other nephrotoxins); neuro/psychiatric (confusion, hallucinations, seizures — more likely elderly/renal/high-dose); TTP/HUS in immunocompromised; dose-adj in renal impairment | Nausea, headache |
| **Celecoxib** (200 mg BID) | Sulfonamide allergy; prior asthma/SARI to ASA/NSAID; active GI bleed; peri-CABG; recent MI; late pregnancy | **GI bleeding** (fatal, esp. elderly); **CV risk** (MI/stroke); fluid retention; raises BP; renal/hepatic effects | GI upset, edema, dizziness |
| **Famciclovir** (NOT in this paper — Pridgen fibromyalgia product) | Hypersensitivity to famciclovir/penciclovir; galactose intolerance; not <18 y | Nephrotoxicity; confusion; renal/liver-disease caution | Headache, nausea |

### Interactions with common ME/CFS co-prescriptions (analysis; verify clinically)
The practical ME/CFS-interaction concerns for the paper's actual regimen (valacyclovir 1500 BID + celecoxib 200 BID ± 15-d Paxlovid):

| Co-prescription | Celecoxib | Valacyclovir | Paxlovid (ritonavir) — only 15 d |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSAIDs (ibuprofen/naproxen/aspirin) | **Avoid — additive GI-bleed + additive nephrotoxicity** | +nephrotoxicity | + |
| **Fludrocortisone** | NSAID fluid retention offsets mineralocorticoid; GI-bleed additive | — | Ritonavir may raise corticosteroid levels (monitor) |
| **Beta-blockers (POTS)** | **NSAIDs reduce antihypertensive efficacy / raise BP** | — | Modest (some CYP2D6/3A4) — monitor HR/BP |
| **Midodrine** | Both can raise BP — additive pressor; renal monitoring | — | Monitor BP |
| **LDN (naltrexone)** | Minimal | — | Minimal |
| **Mestinon (pyridostigmine)** | Minimal | — | Minimal |
| **Antihistamines** | — | — | Ritonavir ↑ some (loratadine/astemizole-class) levels |
| **Gabapentin** | Minimal | Minimal | Ritonavir may ↑ levels — monitor sedation |
| **Trazodone** | Minor bleeding additive | — | **Ritonavir ↑ trazodone (sedation/orthostatic hypotension)** |
| **Amitriptyline (TCA)** | Bleeding additive | — | Ritonavir ↑ levels (CYP) |
| **SSRIs** | Additive bleeding risk | — | Ritonavir ↑ some SSRIs |
| Anticoagulants | Bleeding risk | — | — |

**Bottom line for the paper:** valacyclovir + celecoxib is renally cleared and both are nephrotoxic-capable → renal monitoring essential (the study did check LFTs/renal q6–8 wk). Celecoxib's CV/BP/GI-bleed and NSAID-NSAID interactions matter for the POTS/autonomic ME/CFS population (many on fludrocortisone/midodrine/beta-blockers). The 15-day Paxlovid (ritonavir, strong CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitor) creates a transient interaction window raising trazodone/amitriptyline/some beta-blocker/antihistamine/gabapentin levels — the study reduces valacyclovir during this window but does not address these CYP co-prescriptions. Off-label; high-dose valacyclovir; no pregnancy/lactation data from this paper. Given LOW certainty + COI, this regimen must NOT be presented as a clinical recommendation; it is a research-stage hypothesis generating pilot.
