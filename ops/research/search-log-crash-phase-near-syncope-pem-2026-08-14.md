## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed journal article (original study, RCT, or systematic review).
- ME/CFS, Long COVID, or comorbid-autonomic (POTS/NMH) population.
- Addresses syncope, presyncope, near-syncope, orthostatic intolerance/collapse, cerebral blood flow/hypoperfusion, or post-exertional malaise symptom exacerbation.
- Directly relevant to whether a near-syncopal quality attaches to the PEM crash vs to upright orthostasis.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Single unverifiable social-media/patient-forum posts (the triggering anecdote) — not integrable.
- Case reports, editorials, opinion pieces without empirical data.
- Papers on syncope/NMH/POTS in non-ME/CFS populations with no relevance to post-exertional fatigue.
- Treatment papers without a NMH/OI/cerebral-perfusion component.
- Pure symptom-questionnaire validation without syncope/orthostatic content.
- Duplicates across queries; papers already in the bib corpus.
