## Gap Assessment (strong vs sparse)

**Strong evidence:**
- **Gap 3 (estrogen-progesterone-mast cell mechanism)**: 4 mechanistic papers (in vitro + animal) establish a robust direct pathway from estradiol/progesterone to mast cell activation and degranulation. Discounted certs 0.22–0.28 (low due to population weight, but mechanism is well-established and replicated across independent groups).
- **Gap 2 (HRT benefit/harm)**: Harm evidence is strong and current (BMJ nationwide trial, systematic review). Direct HRT intervention data in ME/CFS is ABSENT (no RCT exists), but route-specific safety (transdermal > oral for VTE) is well supported.
- **Gap 4 (menstrual cyclicity)**: Mixed. Fu 2010 (luteal-phase orthostatic protection in POTS) vs Stickford 2015 (no sympathetic effect, null) — the contrast is instructive: hormonal phase modulates hemodynamics/RAAS but not sympathetic firing. No direct ME/CFS menstrual-cyclicity study exists.
- **Gap 1 (perimenopause transition)**: Moderate. Stewart 2024 directly shows menopause symptoms highly prevalent in post-COVID clinics (most relevant); fibromyalgia-menopause overlap papers support the concept. Direct ME/CFS-perimenopause transition data remains absent.

**Sparse / flagged for Phase 2:**
- **Gap 5 (PCOS co-occurrence)**: NO direct PCOS-ME/CFS co-occurrence evidence found beyond Compton/endocrine. Only PCOS-fatigue study (Cureus 2026) is low-quality (n=37, no control, null fatigue correlation, low-tier journal) and fails inclusion bar. **PCOS co-occurrence remains unsupported by direct evidence — treat as open question, not established.**
- **Gap 6 (obstetric)**: Only 1 new Long-COVID-in-pregnancy cohort added; ME/CFS-specific obstetric management data remains sparse (Allen 2008 + Slack 2023 remain the ME/CFS-specific base).
- **Gap 7 (endometriosis mechanistic)**: Not pursued — Compton 2025 meta-analysis already covers the association; no strong new mechanistic update surfaced.

## Replication Status Summary

- **Replicated (independent groups)**: estrogen→mast cell mechanism (Zaitsu 2007, Narita 2007, Muñoz-Cruz 2015, Jensen 2010).
- **Single study / not replicated**: Stewart 2024 (post-COVID menopause prevalence), Adeyemi 2025 (pregnancy long-COVID), Fu 2010 + Stickford 2015 (different POTS menstrual studies, not exact replication of each other).
- **Contradicting evidence**: Fu 2010 (luteal-phase orthostatic benefit) vs Stickford 2015 (no sympathetic neural effect across phases) — not a direct contradiction (different outcomes: hemodynamics/RAAS vs MSNA) but shows the menstrual-cyclicity claim is not uniformly supported.
- **HARM (treatment safety)**: Johansson 2024 is a single large emulated trial; supported by consistent VTE literature (Goldstajn 2023 review).

## Integration Notes for Phase 2

- Gap 1, 3, 4 have the strongest support; Gap 5 (PCOS) is weakest (treat as open question); Gap 2 HRT benefit in ME/CFS is theoretical (no direct trial) but harm/safety is well characterized.
- Discounted certs are low (0.22–0.68) because no included paper is a direct ME/CFS cohort study — the population-weight discount drives this. Highest discounted certs: Shah 2025 (0.68, sex differences), Johansson 2024 (0.64, HRT harm), Fu 2010 (0.52, menstrual POTS).
- These are hypothesis/speculation/open-question territory (not achievement) for the chapter; the estrogen-mast cell mechanism is the most mechanistically secure.
- Severity applicability: none of the included studies stratify ME/CFS by severity — flag as "severity applicability unknown" in integration.
