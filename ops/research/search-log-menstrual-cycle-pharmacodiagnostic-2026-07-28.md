## Search Limitations

1. **Database coverage:** PubMed only. Google Scholar not systematically searched (rate-limited API).
2. **Date range:** No date restriction applied. Found papers from 1998–2026.
3. **Language:** English only (PubMed default).
4. **Grey literature:** Not searched (conference abstracts, preprints except where indexed in PubMed).
5. **MeSH indexing:** Newer papers (2024–2026) may not be fully indexed — keyword searches may miss some.
6. **The probe concept:** No MeSH term exists for "menstrual cycle as pharmacodiagnostic probe" — all searches were keyword-based, with unavoidable false positives and some false negatives.
7. **Circadian × menstrual interaction:** Difficult to search effectively — most hits conflate the two rhythms rather than studying their interaction.
