## Primary Study (Anchor)

### Liu et al., 2026 — VMAT2 PET in Long COVID
- **Citation:** Liu YK et al. Loss of vesicular monoamine transporter 2 in striatum of long COVID and relationship to neuropsychiatric symptoms. _eBioMedicine_. 2026. DOI: 10.1016/j.ebiom.2026.106339. PMID: 42431745.
- **Access:** Abstract obtained via PubMed E-utilities; full-text behind Lancet paywall (403). ScienceAlert secondary report URLs both returned 404.
- **Design:** Case-control PET study. (+)[11C]DTBZ VMAT2 binding, n=24 long COVID vs 24 age-matched HC (extended to 43).
- **Key findings:**
  - VMAT2 BPND reduced 16-20% in ventral striatum, dorsal putamen, dorsal caudate (LME P=4×10⁻⁵)
  - COVID-recovered controls: normal VMAT2 (P=0.77 vs never-COVID)
  - Apathy severity ↔ ventral striatum VMAT2 (r=−0.54, P=0.0069)
  - Motor slowing ↔ dorsal putamen VMAT2 (r=0.51, P=0.010)
  - Memory decline ↔ dorsal caudate VMAT2 (r=0.58, P=0.0029)
  - No relationship to BDI depression score or peripheral DA metabolites
- **Treatment implication:** DA augmentation (L-dopa, MAOIs) as therapeutic direction
- **Limitations:** Young sample (mean 32.2), apathy-predominant phenotype, n=24, no pre-COVID baseline
- **COI:** JHM has pending patent for rasagiline + tyramine for long COVID; holds Moderna stock
- **Raw certainty:** 0.72; Population weight: 0.85 (Long COVID cohort); **Discounted certainty:** 0.61
- **Already in bib:** Yes (`Liu2026VMAT2longcovid` in `bib/long-covid.bib`)

### 3. Lee et al., 2021 — TGF-β1 Suppresses Striatal Dopamine
- **Bib key:** `Lee2021TGFbetaDopamine` → `bib/long-covid.bib`

### 4. Song et al., 2021 — Myelophil Restores DA in Reserpine Model

- **Raw certainty:** 0.45 | **Discounted:** 0.38 (pop. weight 0.85, animal model)
- **Value:** Reserpine = direct VMAT2 inhibitor. Pharmacological reversibility: Myelophil restored striatal DA, reduced neuroinflammation. Direct animal-model parallel to human VMAT2 PET finding.

### 5. Chang et al., 2024 — Spike Protein → α-Synuclein Aggregation

## Cross-Cutting Themes
1. **Multi-level convergence:** Structural (VMAT2 PET) + functional (7T fMRI FC) + therapeutic (solriamfetol RCT) + mechanistic (in vitro/animal) evidence all point to striatal DA deficiency as a real pathophysiological feature of post-viral chronic illness.
2. **Gliosis → DA loss pathway:** Braga 2023 (TSPO), Braga 2025 (MAO-B), Lee 2021 (TGF-β → TH suppression), and Capuron 2012 (interferon → DA deficiency) form a coherent mechanistic chain.
3. **Gene-environment-virus interaction:** Chatterjee 2024 shows SARS-CoV-2 alone insufficient; primes for "second hit" — explains heterogeneity.
4. **Important null:** Aregawi 2026 found CSF DA pathway NORMAL in PI-ME/CFS and PASC — only noradrenergic deficiency. This creates productive tension: peripheral DA markers may not reflect striatal terminal integrity, or the DA phenotype may be subset-specific.
5. **Direct VMAT2 pharmacological model:** Reserpine (Song 2021) is a VMAT2 inhibitor — the same transporter measured by Liu 2026 — providing a validated animal model of VMAT2 deficiency → fatigue.
