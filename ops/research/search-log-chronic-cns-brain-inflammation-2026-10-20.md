## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed (or recent high-value preprint); 2018–2026, prioritized 2024–2026 for chronic/persistent framing.
- Addresses SUSTAINED/chronic CNS inflammation (not acute neuroinflammatory injury): persistent microglial/astrocyte activation, priming/senescence, compartmentalisation (CSF vs blood vs PET), downstream amplifiers, or null/negative refutations.
- ME/CFS, Long COVID / post-COVID (parallel population), or indirect biochemical mechanism of chronic CNS inflammation (TREM2/sTREM2, GFAP, YKL-40/CHI3L1, microglial senescence/SASP, IL-6, complement, IL-1β, NLRP3).
- NET-NEW: not already present in `bib/neuroinflammation.bib` or adjacent bib files.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Already integrated: Nakatomi2014, Raijmakers2021 (both TSPO null/positive already in bib), VanElzakker2019, Alshelh2020, Omdal2026, Yu2026diffusion-neuroinflammation, Zhu2025MetabolicNeuroimaging, Nutma2023, Wendeln2018/Scholz2024/Zhang2025 (trained immunity), Frank2024 (microglial priming), kynurenine-excitotoxicity cluster, P2X7 cluster.
- Acute neuroinflammatory injury / acute encephalitis as primary focus (Wang2025 is retained specifically for its sTREM2↔TSPO-PET marker validation, not acute-encephalitis biology).
- Neurodegenerative disease biomarkers with no chronicity/relevance bridge (e.g., MSA sTREM2, AD CHI3L1 papers) — screened out as off-topic.
- Pure peripheral (non-CNS) inflammation.
