## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed primary studies, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, or high-value reviews.
- (a) Autism first-impression / thin-slice / reduced-willingness-to-interact stigma (anchor field + replications + in-group + double empathy).
- (b) Mechanism: first-impression formation from presentational "style" (facial affect, prosody, movement, gaze) — searched in chronic-illness/ME/CFS-relevant contexts.
- (c) ME/CFS presentational style / clinician perception (searched; sparse).
- (d) Autism + ME/CFS comorbidity stigma (searched; sparse).
- (e) Transdiagnostic comparators: Parkinson hypomimia, schizophrenia flat affect, chronic pain — supporting "style not substance" drives negative first impressions across conditions.
- NULL/CONTRADICTING evidence explicitly sought: in-group (autistic observers), disclosure-reduces-bias, replication meta-analysis.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Autistic burnout / masking / camouflaging double-burden / diagnostic overshadowing of neurodivergent ME/CFS (ALREADY INTEGRATED under neurodivergent-psychological-consequences-mecfs — Raymaker, Higgins, Kentrou, Ali, Clarey, Quadt already in corpus).
- General ME/CFS stigma / diagnostic invalidation / epistemic injustice / media stigma / psychologization / GET-BPS (already in stigma.bib).
- Purely treatment / pharmacology outcomes unrelated to first-impression stigma.
- Animal models / non-human social-behavior papers.
