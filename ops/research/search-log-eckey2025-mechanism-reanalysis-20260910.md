# Search Log: Eckey 2025 Mechanism Re-analysis

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Topic:** Retrospective re-analysis of the Eckey 2025 ME/CFS + long-COVID treatment
survey (n=3,925; >150 treatments) as a mechanism-ranking pilot — projecting the
treatment-benefit ranking onto molecular targets/mechanisms.
**Slug:** `eckey2025-mechanism-reanalysis`
**Database:** PubMed (E-utilities `esearch`/`esummary`/`efetch`); PMC (full text
`efetch`/HTML); Crossref (DOI verification)

## Framing

This is a **methodological / secondary-analysis** topic, not a drug-efficacy topic.
The driving dataset (@Eckey2025PatientReported) is already in the corpus, so the
search did **not** re-find Eckey 2025; it established (a) the dataset's design and
re-analysis feasibility, and (b) the **separable methodological literature** that
makes or breaks the projection — drug-to-pathway enrichment, data-driven subgroup
identification, placebo/expectation structure, popularity confounding, PROM
validity, and drug-to-target mappings for the high-ranking treatment classes.
A direct study of the proposed re-analysis does not exist (expected); the closest
precedent is the parent pharmacodiagnostic-corpus search, whose corpus is reused.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Drug-to-pathway / drug-set enrichment methods that project a drug list onto
  mechanism classes (mandatory core method).
- Data-driven subgroup/responder identification methods with validation.
- Evidence that placebo/expectation forms a structured, non-random signal.
- Operational measures of treatment popularity / community discussion volume as a
  confounder (search volume, social-media volume).
- Patient-reported outcome measurement validity in ME/CFS (direct population).
- A second independent ME/CFS patient-reported benefit ranking.
- Drug-to-molecular-target mappings for the top treatment classes in the Eckey
  survey (LDN, autonomic modulators) — indirect biochemical links (mandatory).
- Dataset documentation establishing re-analysis feasibility (design, NAS,
  availability of public Supplementary Data).
- Null/negative/critique evidence on any of the above (mandatory).

## Exclusion Criteria

- Generic network-pharmacology / plant-extract mechanism papers with no
  drug-response-corpus relevance (~excluded at screening).
- Drug-response papers used only predictively (pharmacogenomic toxicity) with no
  mechanism inference.
- Disease-specific prognostic-index or cancer-biomarker papers returned by generic
  "rank reproducibility" queries (off-topic).
- Narrative reviews with no data or method contribution.
- Non-English without English abstract.
- Papers already in the corpus under a different key (reused, not re-added).

## Search Terms by Database

- **PubMed E-utilities:** `esearch.fcgi` with `retmax=6–8`, `sort=relevance`;
  metadata via `esummary.fcgi` and `efetch.fcgi` (XML); count and PMID list read
  from `esearchresult`.
- **PMC (PubMed Central):** `efetch.fcgi db=pmc` XML and the PMC article HTML for
  `PMC12280984` (Eckey 2025) to read the Methods, NAS definition, cluster
  structure, limitations, and Data Availability statement.
- **Crossref API:** `/works` query for DOI verification of candidate titles.
- **Google Scholar / Semantic Scholar / preprint servers:** not queried. Result
  differentiation was insufficient for this methodological topic at Scholar, and
  the PubMed/PMC/Crossref yield was adequate; preprint servers (medRxiv/bioRxiv)
  were not needed because the included methods are published, peer-reviewed, and
  the driving dataset is peer-reviewed PNAS.
