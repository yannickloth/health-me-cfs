**Agent:** literature-integrator (Phase 1)
**Topic type:** NULL-evidence treatment topic — LDN 30% responder re-analysis of the FINAL trial in fibromyalgia (FM). FM is a high-overlap comorbidity of ME/CFS; population-weight discount applied (FM ≠ ME/CFS cohort).

## Primary paper

### Nielsen MJ, Vaegter HB, Due Bruun K (2026) — Symptom Response to Low-Dose Naltrexone in Fibromyalgia (FINAL exploratory re-analysis)
- **Journal:** Pain Management Nursing (2026 Aug 12, online ahead of print) · **DOI:** 10.1016/j.pmn.2026.07.020 · **PMID:** 42586891
- **Design:** Exploratory secondary analysis of the FINAL RCT (n=99 women with FM, 1:1 LDN 6 mg vs placebo × 12 weeks).
- **Finding:** Assessed 30% responder rates for six secondary outcomes. NO significant differences for any outcome. Highest RR = memory (1.67, 95% CI 0.82–2.95), then sleep (1.28, 0.92–2.64), stiffness (1.26, 0.84–2.89), tenderness (1.16, 0.88–2.67), fatigue (1.10, 0.78–3.13), depression (0.96, 0.92–2.66).
- **Conclusion:** 30% responder rates for non-pain FM outcomes similar between LDN and placebo — NULL. The memory signal from the primary FINAL paper did not reach significance as a responder category.
- **Certainty:** 0.60 raw × 0.80 FM weight = **0.48 discounted**.

## Null / positive / harm findings

**Null (6 papers):**
- Nielsen 2026 — no 30%-responder difference on any of 6 non-pain outcomes.
- Due Bruun 2026 — re-analysis: lower efficacy than previously reported.
- Rodríguez-Freire 2026 (INNOVA) — 12-month RCT, no pain benefit over placebo (p=0.236, d=0.19).
- Due Bruun 2025 (CPM) — CPM group-difference random, no association with clinical improvement.
- Häuser & Fitzcharles 2024 — editorial framing LDN as possible "treatment disappointment."
- Ologunowa 2025 — meta-analysis: within-group improvement but no between-group superiority vs placebo.

**Positive/competing (1 paper):**
- Vatvani 2024 — meta-analysis (4 RCTs): significant pain reduction (MD -0.86) and higher pressure pain threshold; FIQR/PCS not different. Diverges from Ologunowa 2025 (trial set + methods dependence). Includes erratum note.

**Harm/safety (1 paper):**
- Bolton 2019 — no excess serious adverse events for oral naltrexone vs placebo (RR 0.84, 95% CI 0.66–1.06). Supports favorable-safety framing despite null efficacy. General-population weight (0.50).

## Indirect biochemical links (2 papers)

**TLR4/microglia (Gao 2025):** (+)-naltrexone enantiomer antagonizes TLR4 without opioid activity; optimized derivative CIAC101 is ~6200× more potent, blocks LPS→NF-κB and microglial activation. Establishes the TLR4→microglial anti-neuroinflammatory mechanism hypothesized for LDN. Implication for the paper: even if the TLR4 mechanism is real, it may not translate to measurable FM symptom benefit at LDN's clinical doses — consistent with the null clinical results.

**OGFr/enkephalin rebound (Zagon & McLaughlin 2018):** LDN acts via intermittent blockade of the opioid growth factor (OGF)/OGFr axis; restores serum enkephalin; clinical reports document reduced fatigue in FM. Frames the endogenous-opioid rebound mechanism explicitly including fibromyalgia and fatigue — but no controlled FM efficacy data.

## Integration relevance

This is a NULL-evidence topic. The FINAL trial primary paper (`DueBruun2024LDNFibromyalgia`) already established no significant pain benefit and a possible memory signal. The new responder re-analysis closes that memory signal (RR 1.67, CI 0.82–2.95, non-significant), and the INNOVA 12-month RCT independently replicates the null. The Vatvani 2024 positive meta-analysis is the sole competing result and should be flagged as divergent (small trial set, erratum issued).

**Key takeaway for the paper's LDN narrative:** LDN's FM efficacy is a null finding across independent RCTs and re-analyses; its favorable safety profile (Bolton 2019) is not in question, but the clinical-benefit case has weakened substantially. The existing LDN integration in the paper (ch07, ch15, ch18, ch30, hormesis framework) should be audited against this null evidence — the "memory improvement" hint from the FINAL primary paper must NOT be cited as a positive finding without the responder-analysis caveat.

**Population-relevance note:** all clinical papers are fibromyalgia (weight 0.80), not ME/CFS. Direct ME/CFS LDN evidence remains the retrospective Polo 2019 cohort (already in bib). This topic strengthens the null/negative direction for LDN generally but does not add new ME/CFS-specific efficacy data.
