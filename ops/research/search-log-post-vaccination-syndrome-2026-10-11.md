## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed (preferred) or recent preprint directly characterizing PVS/PCVS/PACVS phenotype, biomarkers, or clinical overlap with ME/CFS/Long COVID.
- Null/negative vaccine-safety registry or self-controlled studies testing vaccine→CFS/ME/POTS/SFN causation (HPV and COVID-19 vaccines).
- Indirect biochemical/mechanistic links: spike persistence, GPCR/anti-nerve autoantibodies, SFN, POTS, mast-cell activation, adjuvant NLRP3 — and their ME/CFS intersections.
- Harm: myocarditis/pericarditis risk after mRNA COVID-19 vaccination.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Post-vaccination syndromes unrelated to ME/CFS/LC/dysautonomia (Gianotti-Crosti, post-vaccinal encephalomyelitis/hemiplegia historical reports, ocular-only manifestations).
- Existing alum-adjuvant/MMF/ASIA cluster (already integrated: Gherardi2015biopersist, Eisenbarth2008nalp3, Perez2026adjuvants, Ruiz2024hpa, Skare2024, Mitkus2011aluminum, Principi2018aluminum) — deliberately NOT re-added; only Ameratunga2017 (ASIA-refutation) was considered and excluded as alum-cluster territory.
- MCAS-vaccine-safety studies (Giannetti2022, Kaakati2021, Rama2022) — these concern vaccination *safety in* MCAS patients, not PVS causation.
- Already-integrated vaccine keys (Magnus2015influenza, Prinsen2012vaccine, Tsuchida2022vaccineLC, Buchner2025Psychologization) — reused, not re-added.
- Pure case reports without ME/CFS/cohort relevance (Finsterer/Finterer myalgia case, Schelke tinnitus case, Waheed, Mastropaolo, Bernheimer).

## Key null/negative finding (recorded for reproducibility)

- `COVID-19 vaccine no increased risk myalgic encephalomyelitis chronic fatigue syndrome registry` → **0 results** (no single combined null-query; the null is carried by four separate registry studies: Feiring2017, Hviid2020, Jensen2025, Kwan2022).
- The strongest *direct* population-level null is Jensen2025 (Danish BiCoVac): vaccinated unconcerned individuals had LOWER odds of post-acute symptoms; only vaccine-concerned individuals reported higher odds (nocebo).
- No prospective controlled cohort of PVS with a comparison group and objective outcome measures exists — flagged by YangWang2026 as the critical gap.
