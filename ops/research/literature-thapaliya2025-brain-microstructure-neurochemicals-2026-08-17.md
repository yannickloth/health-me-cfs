# Literature Summary: Thapaliya et al. 2025 — Brain Microstructure & Neurochemicals in Long COVID and Recovered COVID-19 (Multimodal MRI)

**Topic slug:** `thapaliya2025-brain-microstructure-neurochemicals`
**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Mode:** MIXED (governor ledger present; scope by explicit file lists only — bib/long-covid.bib + appendix-h only)

## Primary paper

**Thapaliya K, Marshall-Gradisnik S, Inderyas M, Barnden L. 2025, Brain, Behavior, & Immunity - Health** (PMID 41404601; DOI 10.1016/j.bbih.2025.101142)

- Multimodal 3T MRI (Siemens Prisma), n=47: **long COVID=19** (WHO Delphi criteria), **COVID-recovered healthy controls [COVID-RHC]=12**, **healthy controls without COVID [Non-COVID-HC]=16**.
- Three imaging arms: T1w/T2w ratio maps (myelin proxy, Ganzetti 2015 method), DTI (FA/AD/MD/RD, TBSS), single-voxel 1H-MRS (posterior cingulate cortex, STEAM TE=6.5 ms, LCModel fit; 18 metabolites).
- **Funding:** ME Research UK + Fred & Joan Davies Bequest + Stafford Fox Foundation; no COI declared. Griffith University NCNED (Gold Coast, Australia).

### Region-level findings (from Results, read directly)

**T1w/T2w (myelin proxy):**
- Long COVID > Non-COVID-HC: **precentral gyrus** (pfdr<0.001), **middle temporal gyrus** (pfdr=0.005).
- COVID-RHC > long COVID: **pons** (pfdr<0.001), **left & right midbrain** (pfdr<0.001), **cerebellar tonsil** (pfdr<0.001), **left SLF** (pfdr<0.001), **right SLF** (pfdr=0.005), **precentral gyrus** (pfdr=0.009), **forceps minor** (pfdr=0.005), **right optic radiation** (pfdr=0.017), **Brodmann area 18** (pfdr=0.005).
- COVID-RHC > Non-COVID-HC: **precentral gyrus** (pfdr=0.001), **posterior cingulate cortex** (pfdr=0.004).

**DTI (tissue microstructure):**
- Long COVID vs Non-COVID-HC: lower **MD in dentate regions** (pfdr=0.041).
- Long COVID vs COVID-RHC: higher **FA in right superior longitudinal fasciculus (SLF)** (pfdr=0.001).
- COVID-RHC vs Non-COVID-HC: lower **AD/MD/RD in left caudate** (AD pfdr=0.001; MD pfdr=0.005; RD pfdr=0.020); lower **AD in right supramarginal gyrus** (pfdr=0.008).

**MRS (neurochemicals, posterior cingulate cortex):**
- Only long COVID vs COVID-RHC differed after FDR: **NAA higher in long COVID** (15.041±0.525 vs 14.525±0.420; pfdr=0.009); **glutamine higher in COVID-RHC** (5.612±0.505 vs 5.358±0.569; pfdr=0.019). Choline (higher in COVID-RHC) did NOT survive correction.
- (Long COVID vs Non-COVID-HC neurochemicals not re-tested — previously reported in Thapaliya 2024.)

**Correlations:** T1w/T2w middle temporal gyrus ↔ physical function (p=0.024, r=0.56); midbrain T1w/T2w ↔ cognitive score (p=0.01, r=−0.64). No significant DTI–clinical correlations.

### Primary-paper interpretation caveats (critical for integration)
- Authors interpret **elevated T1w/T2w as possible remyelination**, but explicitly state it "may also indicate inflammation or gliosis" (citing Chen 2025) — direction is **not** resolved (myelin vs iron vs gliosis).
- Authors explicitly link their finding to the **prior Griffith ME/CFS finding** (Thapaliya 2020 elevated T1w/T2w in ME/CFS), and note their long-COVID lower-MD parallels prior ME lower-MD (Thapaliya 2021).
- **Limitations (self-stated):** cross-sectional; small n; exploratory (cluster threshold may inflate Type I error); single lab; needs replication + longitudinal follow-up.

## Cohort/lab-overlap note (Instruction F)

The primary paper is from the **Griffith University NCNED group** (Marshall-Gradisnik / Barnden / Thapaliya / Inderyas / Eaton-Fitch). Within this search, the following share that SAME lab:
- Thapaliya2020T1 (T1w/T2w ME/CFS), Thapaliya2021DTI (DTI ME/CFS), Singh2026DTIDKI (DTI+DKI ME/CFS vs long COVID), Maksoud2020Neuro (systematic review).

**→ This is a 5-paper single-lab cluster for the "elevated T1w/T2w + altered DTI" finding. NOT independent replications.**

**Independent labs (organic replication of brain microstructural pathology in ME/CFS / post-COVID):**
- **Sunshine Coast / Shan group (Yu, Kwiatek):** Yu2026NII (n=67 ME/CFS, diffusion-NII neuroinflammation), Yu2025PI (PI vs GO ME/CFS, AD-specific). Independent of Griffith.
- **Beijing Anding (Wu):** Wu2026Cingulum (n=100 CFS, cingulum FA↓).
- **Emory/Stony Brook (Huang/Luft):** Huang2026N (n=54 N-PASC, multi-shell DTI).
- **Goethe Frankfurt (Arendt):** Arendt2026 (n=145, TBSS — partial null, severity-dependent).
- **Fudan Shanghai (Lu):** Lu2020Micro (recovered COVID, lower MD/AD/RD).
- **Guangxi (Qin):** Qin2024 (post-COVID sleep disorder, IL-1β link).

Net independent labs supporting the **microstructural alteration in post-viral/ME/CFS brain**: Sunshine Coast + Beijing + Emory/Stony Brook + Frankfurt + Fudan + Guangxi = ≥6 organic labs (with direction heterogeneity and one severity-gated partial null).

## Supporting — same-lab methodological precursors (Griffith cluster)

- **Thapaliya2020T1 (NeuroImage: Clinical):** T1w/T2w ratio elevated in ME/CFS (n=45 Fukuda + 27 HC), both WM and subcortical grey matter, no decreases. Same method as primary → anchors the long-COVID T1w/T2w finding to a prior ME/CFS finding.
- **Thapaliya2021DTI (Eur J Neurosci):** DTI in ME/CFS (ICC vs HC): decreased AD/MD in cortico-cerebellar tract (midbrain/pons), decreased mode of anisotropy in SLF. Same midbrain/SLF regions as primary.
- **Singh2026DTIDKI (Front Med):** DTI + DKI in ME/CFS (37) + long COVID (19) + HC (27); ME/CFS cingulum/SMA/corpus callosum; long COVID fusiform/precentral; ME/CFS vs LC differ in left corona radiata (p=0.001). Direct cross-disease comparison, same registry.
- **Maksoud2020Neuro (PLoS One):** systematic review of 55 ME/CFS neuroimaging studies — findings widespread but inconsistent; context/caution.

## Supporting — independent ME/CFS diffusion microstructural studies

- **Yu2026NII (Hum Brain Mapp):** n=67 ME/CFS + 67 HC; diffusion-NII model shows cerebral edema (↓hindered-water ratio), cellular infiltration (↓restricted fraction), axonal reorganisation (↑fibre fraction). Conventional DTI showed minimal differences → standard DTI may under-detect. Strongest independent microstructural anchor.
- **Yu2025PI (Sci Rep):** PI-ME/CFS higher AD (association/projection fibres); GO-ME/CFS lower AD (corpus callosum); NO FA/MD/RD effect — partial null constraining metric specificity.
- **Wu2026Cingulum (Brain Res Bull):** n=100 CFS + 100 HC; FA↓ in left cingulum bundle, 85% diagnostic accuracy, tract recovery ↔ fatigue improvement. Large-n independent cingulum replication.

## Supporting — independent long-COVID / recovered-COVID DTI studies

- **Huang2026N (BBH):** n=54 N-PASC (2.7 yr) vs 26; global/tract WM alterations; bilateral fornix + minor forceps (axonal injury/demyelination); diffusion ↔ fatigue/executive/anosmia/mood (NOT brain fog).
- **Arendt2026 (Brain Res Bull):** n=145 unvaccinated; NO overall FA difference; only older hospitalised subgroup showed decreased FA/increased RD/reduced qT1 in frontoinsular-cingulate WM. Severity-gated partial null.
- **Lu2020Micro (EClinicalMedicine):** n=60 recovered COVID; lower MD/AD/RD + higher FA in WM; higher GMV in olfactory/hippocampus/insula. Basis for primary's lower-diffusivity COVID-RHC finding.
- **Qin2024 (Sleep Med):** post-COVID sleep-disorder cohort; IFOF/CST WM changes track IL-1β and normalise at 3 months — indirect biochemical link to neuroinflammation.
- **Jahanshahi2026 (Brain Imaging Behav):** systematic review — post-COVID DWI alterations in longitudinal fasciculi, thalamic radiations, corpus callosum, CST, etc.; corona radiata/longitudinal fasciculus nearly consistent.
- **RaneLevendovszky2025 (Br J Radiol):** review — PASC GM reductions, WM hyperintensities, DTI oedema (sagittal stratum/thalamic radiation), PET hypometabolism. Context layer.

## Strengths / weaknesses / missing / null

- **Strong:** Multimodal (myelin proxy + DTI + MRS) in the SAME participants; three-group design (long COVID vs recovered vs never-infected) is novel; independent-lab support for brain microstructural pathology in ME/CFS and post-COVID (≥6 organic labs); large-n independent cingulum finding (Wu n=100/100); primary uses robust FSL/SPM/Osprey/LCModel pipelines.
- **Weak:** Primary n=47 (subgroups 12–19) is small and exploratory; T1w/T2w direction ambiguous (myelin vs iron vs gliosis — authors concede); 5-paper Griffith cluster is NOT independent replication; DTI metric/direction heterogeneity across labs (FA↑ vs FA↓, AD↑ vs AD↓ depending on cohort/onset type); no longitudinal.
- **Missing:** No direct MR measure to disambiguate myelin vs iron vs gliosis (e.g., myelin water imaging, quantitative susceptibility mapping, or TSPO PET in the same cohort); no perfusion measure to separate hypoperfusion from remyelination; no NAA/glutamine mechanistic confirmation (e.g., mitochondrial/energetic assay).
- **Null:** Arendt2026 (no overall FA difference in mild/unselected COVID — severity-gated); Yu2025PI (no FA/MD/RD group effect in ME/CFS); primary's choline finding did not survive FDR; primary found no significant DTI–clinical correlations.

## Indirect biochemical/systemic links (Instruction C reasoning)

- **T1w/T2w (myelin proxy) ↔ myelin/iron/gliosis:** elevated signal is read as remyelination OR inflammation/gliosis. Systemic link: neuroinflammation (microglial activation) can both drive demyelination and stimulate (re)myelination; the direction cannot be resolved from T1w/T2w alone. Cross-maps to the document's neuroinflammatory/glymphatic/BBB chapters (ch08/ch09).
- **DTI microstructure ↔ neuroinflammation/edema/cellular infiltration:** Yu2026NII explicitly models diffusion change as cerebral edema + cellular infiltration (neuroinflammation), not just axonal loss. Qin2024 links post-COVID WM change to IL-1β. This maps microstructural change onto the neuroinflammatory cascade, not a purely structural/neurodegenerative reading.
- **MRS NAA↑ + glutamine↓ (long COVID vs recovered):** NAA elevation read as compensatory response to reduced metabolic efficiency / osmotic stress; glutamine depletion read as increased glutamine utilisation for ATP/immune regulation. Maps onto the brain energy-metabolism / mitochondrial stress theme (consistent with the Godlewska 7T-MRS brain-lactate-in-ME/CFS finding and the Thapaliya 2024 glutamate↑ finding).
- **Lower MD/AD/RD in recovered COVID (caudate):** restricted diffusion interpreted as microstructural reorganisation/remyelination — consistent with Lu2020Micro; but could also reflect cellular swelling (edema) per Yu2026NII's edema interpretation. Direction ambiguity is a recurring theme.
