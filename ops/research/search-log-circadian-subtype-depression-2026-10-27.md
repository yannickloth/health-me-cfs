## Rival framings searched (generated before queries)

1. **Circadian disruption is a transdiagnostic ME/CFS–depression mechanism.** Search for circadian abnormalities in ME/CFS and whether they share genetic/metabolic features with the new depression subtype.
2. **Circadian disruption is not a core ME/CFS mechanism; sleep complaints are secondary to autonomic/immune dysfunction.** Search explicitly for null circadian findings in CFS/ME.
3. **The depression subtype reflects ADHD/metabolic polygenic risk rather than a primary circadian disorder.** Search for overlap between circadian depression features and ADHD/insulin resistance/inflammation in ME/CFS.
4. **Chronobiological interventions (melatonin, light, zinc) are actionable in ME/CFS.** Search for RCT evidence of circadian-targeted treatments in ME/CFS.

Papers per framing included: F1=3 (Cambras 2018, McCarthy 2022, Castro-Marrero 2021); F2=1 (Rahman 2011); F3=1 (Tonini 2026); F4=1 (Castro-Marrero 2021, also counted under F1).

## Inclusion criteria

- Peer-reviewed primary research, systematic review, or narrative review.
- Addresses circadian/sleep/chronobiology in depression, ME/CFS, or both.
- Provides quantitative or directly interpretable qualitative data relevant to cross-disease mechanisms.
- English language.

## Exclusion criteria

- Opinion pieces, editorials, or conference abstracts without primary data (except where explicitly noted).
- Animal-only or in-vitro-only studies for the cross-disease inference question (kept as indirect if highly relevant).
- Duplicate reports of the same study population.
