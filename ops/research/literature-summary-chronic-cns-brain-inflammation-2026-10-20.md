## Per-paper summaries

### 1. Martins 2026 — Post-COVID neuroinflammation (chronicity review)
- **Key:** `Martins2026postcovidneuroinflammation` | Transl Psychiatry 2026;16:564 | Review | Long COVID (PCS)
- **Key findings:** Frames sustained microglial/astrocyte activation, BBB disruption and aberrant cytokine signalling as chronic neuroinflammation driving PCS. Reviews GFAP, sTREM2, S100β, IL-6, TNF-α as fluid biomarkers; TSPO-PET (limbic/frontal), MRS (myo-inositol/choline), free-water diffusion as imaging. Proposes local sleep intrusions, impaired memory reconsolidation, astrocyte-mediated network destabilisation.
- **Raw certainty 0.45** (narrative review cap 0.50) | **population weight 0.85** (Long COVID) | **discounted 0.38**
- **Tier:** narrative review | **Diagnostic criteria:** N/A (review)
- **Framing:** F2/F4

### 2. Visser 2025 — Whole-body TSPO-PET post-COVID (compartmentalised)
- **Key:** `Visser2025TSPOfatigue` | J Nucl Med 2025;66(11):1787–1794 | Cross-sectional cohort, n=47 | Amsterdam UMC
- **Key findings:** Whole-body [18F]DPA-714 TSPO-PET (second-generation tracer, arterial input) in n=47 post-COVID (~27 mo post-infection). Globally increased cerebral binding in SOME individuals with persistent fatigue/concentration; no group-level extracerebral differences; cerebral↔extracerebral binding uncorrelated with each other or severity. Concludes an "inflammatory subtype."
- **Raw certainty 0.55** (cohort cap 0.60) | **population weight 0.85** | **discounted 0.47**
- **Tier:** prospective cohort | **Diagnostic criteria:** post-COVID persistent fatigue (custom)
- **Framing:** F2 (low-grade, compartmentalised, subtype-specific)

### 3. Kempuraj 2026 — Neurosenescence/inflammaging (chronicity mechanism)
- **Key:** `Kempuraj2026neurosenescence` | Front Aging 2026;7:1756670 | Review | Klimas + Theoharides
- **Key findings:** Senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP: IL-6, IGFBPs, TGF-β, MMP-10) and microglial senescence drive low-grade persistent neuroinflammation (inflammaging).
- **Raw certainty 0.45** (review cap 0.50) | **population weight 0.75** | **discounted 0.34**
- **Tier:** narrative review | **Diagnostic criteria:** N/A (review)
- **Framing:** F1 (chronicity)

### 4. Wendimu 2022 — Microglia phenotypes in aging (chronicity mechanism)
- **Key:** `Wendimu2022microgliaaging` | Cells 2022;11(13):2091 | Review, 275 citations
- **Key findings:** Chronically activated microglia documented across neuropathological disease; aged microglia adopt dystrophic/senescent morphology with a primed hyper-responsive phenotype; biphasic M1/M2 response depends on insult duration.
- **Raw certainty 0.50** (review cap 0.50) | **population weight 0.75** | **discounted 0.38**
- **Tier:** narrative review | **Diagnostic criteria:** N/A (review)
- **Framing:** F1 (chronicity)

### 5. Matsushita 2021 — Sustained microglia in area postrema (animal, F4)
- **Key:** `Matsushita2021areapostrema` | Arthritis Res Ther 2021;23(1):273 | Animal (CIA mice)
- **Key findings:** Sustained microglial activation (number/morphology + IL-1β mRNA) in the area postrema (BBB-deficient circumventricular organ) during chronic peripheral arthritis, persisting beyond acute phase.
- **Raw certainty 0.35** (animal cap 0.40) | **population weight 0.50** | **discounted 0.18**
- **Tier:** animal | **Diagnostic criteria:** N/A (animal)
- **Framing:** F4 (peripheral → CNS, downstream)

### 6. Morton 2026 — Persisting microglia + synaptic imbalance (animal, F4/chronicity)
- **Key:** `Morton2026persistentmicroglia` | Glia 2026;74(11):e70208 | Animal (DSS colitis mice, 3 cycles + remission)
- **Key findings:** Cortical microglia sustain an iNOS-enriched activation state during REMISSION (after peripheral inflammation resolved): VGLUT1+ glutamatergic synaptosomes ↑, VGAT+ GABAergic ↓, c-fos suppressed → excitatory-inhibitory imbalance. Hippocampal microglia ARG1-dominant, TREM2/P2Y12R-enriched (reparative).
- **Raw certainty 0.40** (animal cap 0.40) | **population weight 0.50** | **discounted 0.20**
- **Tier:** animal | **Diagnostic criteria:** N/A (animal)
- **Framing:** F4 + F1 chronicity (CNS "memory" outlasting peripheral trigger)

### 7. Carpanini 2025 — TREM2 in chronic prion neuroinflammation (animal, F1)
- **Key:** `Carpanini2025trem2prion` | Front Neurosci 2025;19:1525017 | Animal (ME7 prion mice, Trem2 KO)
- **Key findings:** Trem2−/− worsens neuropathology despite unchanged misfolded-protein deposition; blunts disease-induced microglial reactivity → TREM2 regulates chronic microglial reactivity and neuroprotection.
- **Raw certainty 0.35** (animal cap 0.40) | **population weight 0.50** | **discounted 0.18**
- **Tier:** animal | **Diagnostic criteria:** N/A (animal)
- **Framing:** F1 (TREM2 chronicity)

### 8. Wang 2025 — DPA714-PET & CSF sTREM2 (human, marker validation)
- **Key:** `Wang2025dpa714strem2` | Eur J Neurol 2025;32(3):e70107 | n=46 anti-LGI1 encephalitis
- **Key findings:** Increased [18F]DPA-714 TSPO uptake (hippocampus, frontal, caudate); CSF sTREM2 correlates with hippocampal SUVRc (R²=0.18, p=0.04); hippocampal uptake predicts prognosis. Validates sTREM2 as CSF microglial-activation biomarker tracking TSPO-PET.
- **Raw certainty 0.50** (cohort cap 0.60) | **population weight 0.75** | **discounted 0.38**
- **Tier:** prospective cohort | **Diagnostic criteria:** anti-LGI1 antibody encephalitis
- **Framing:** indirect (sTREM2 measurement, supports F1/F2)

### 9. De Boni 2022 — Null GFAP/NfL post-COVID headache (F3 null)
- **Key:** `DeBoni2022postcovidheadache` | Neurol Res Pract 2022;4(1):53 | Pilot serological letter
- **Key findings:** NfL and GFAP lower in blood of persistent post-COVID headache vs severe COVID-19; no evidence of neuronal damage/reactive gliosis. NULL for chronic post-COVID CNS symptoms.
- **Raw certainty 0.35** (case-series cap 0.45) | **population weight 0.85** | **discounted 0.30**
- **Tier:** case-series | **Diagnostic criteria:** post-COVID persistent headache (>12 weeks; custom)
- **Framing:** F3 (contradicting/null)

### 10. Abdelrheem 2026 — Leptin → microglia in fibromyalgia (F4, indirect)
- **Key:** `Abdelrheem2026leptinfm` | Int Immunopharmacol 2026;180:116657 | Review
- **Key findings:** Leptin links peripheral metabolic cues to CNS inflammation; leptin receptor JAK2/STAT3, MAPK/ERK, PI3K/Akt promote reactive microglia and central sensitization in fibromyalgia (chronic pain/fatigue).
- **Raw certainty 0.40** (review cap 0.50) | **population weight 0.80** | **discounted 0.32**
- **Tier:** narrative review | **Diagnostic criteria:** N/A (review)
- **Framing:** F4 (indirect metabolic → CNS)

## Indirect biochemical/systemic link analysis (MANDATORY)

| Mechanism | What it does (source) | Searched against ME/CFS / Long COVID | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Microglial senescence / SASP** | Sustains low-grade neuroinflammation via IL-6, TGF-β, MMP-10 (@Kempuraj2026neurosenescence; @Wendimu2022microgliaaging) | "microglial senescence chronic neuroinflammation" | 2 reviews found (no direct ME/CFS study) |
| **TREM2 / sTREM2** | Regulates chronic microglial reactivity + neuroprotection (@Carpanini2025trem2prion); sTREM2 is a CSF microglial-activation marker tracking TSPO-PET (@Wang2025dpa714strem2) | "sTREM2 microglial activation long COVID", "sTREM2 cerebrospinal fluid" | Omdal2026 (blood TREM2 null, already in bib) + Wang2025 (CSF positive) |
| **GFAP (astrocyte reactivity)** | Reactive astrogliosis marker (@Martins2026postcovidneuroinflammation) | "GFAP astrocyte reactivity long COVID" | Null in post-COVID headache (@DeBoni2022) and Omdal2026 (already in bib) |
| **Persistent microglial activation (area postrema / gut-brain)** | Sustained microglia after peripheral inflammation (@Matsushita2021areapostrema; @Morton2026persistentmicroglia) | "persistent microglial activation chronic fatigue" | 2 animal studies (no human ME/CFS) |
| **Leptin → microglial** | Adipokine drives reactive microglia + central sensitization (@Abdelrheem2026leptinfm) | "leptin microglial fibromyalgia" | 1 review (fibromyalgia) |
| **CHI3L1/YKL-40** | Astroglial/microglial activation marker | "chitinase 3-like 1 YKL-40 ME/CFS CSF" | **0 ME/CFS studies** (gap) |
| **IL-6 trans-signalling** | Soluble IL-6R/gp130 chronic inflammation route | "IL-6 trans-signaling chronic fatigue syndrome" | **0 ME/CFS studies** (gap) |

**Key indirect connections:** (1) sTREM2↔TSPO-PET validated in humans (Wang2025) — gives the paper a fluid readout for the chronic-microglia state it already claims via Nakatomi2014. (2) Animal models show microglial activation OUTLASTS peripheral inflammation (Morton2026; Matsushita2021) — mechanistic basis for "chronicity" absent from the human TSPO null/positive literature.

## Cohort-overlap / site notes (deduplication aid)
- Visser2025: Amsterdam UMC post-COVID cohort (van Berckel/Boellaard/Knoop/Tolboom; Appelman co-author — also on muscle post-COVID studies). Distinct from Raijmakers2021 (Nijmegen CFS/QFS) and from Omdal2026 (Stavanger, Norway).
- Wang2025: Huashan Hospital/Fudan Shanghai (Zuo, Chen). Distinct anti-LGI1 encephalitis cohort.
- De Boni2022: Bonn, Germany (Petzold GC). Post-COVID headache cohort.

## Certainty landscape
- **Strongest (discounted ≥0.40):** Visser2025 (0.47) — second-gen TSPO-PET, n=47, compartmentalised/subtype evidence in a post-viral fatigue population.
- **Moderate:** Martins2026, Wendimu2022, Wang2025 (0.38 each); Kempuraj2026 (0.34); Abdelrheem2026 (0.32); De Boni2022 (0.30).
- **Weak (animal, ≤0.20):** Matsushita2021 (0.18), Carpanini2025 (0.18), Morton2026 (0.20).
- **Highest raw (research-quality):** Visser2025 (0.55), Wendimu2022 (0.50), Wang2025 (0.50). All others ≤0.45.
- **Strongest null/negative:** De Boni2022 (0.30 discounted) — consistent with the already-integrated Omdal2026 (blood NfL/GFAP null) and Raijmakers2021 (TSPO null).

## Open gaps (for Phase 2 attention)
1. No direct ME/CFS CHI3L1/YKL-40 study exists.
2. No direct ME/CFS IL-6 trans-signalling study exists.
3. All chronicity-mechanism evidence (senescence, TREM2, persisting microglia) is animal or review; no human ME/CFS study directly measures the *chronicity* axis (senescent microglia, primed state persistence) beyond one-time TSPO-PET.
