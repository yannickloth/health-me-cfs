# Search Log: Longitudinal Mild/Moderate → Severe Transition in Established ME/CFS

Topic slug: `years-mild-moderate-then-severe` | Date: 2026-09-02

## Focus

Single gap question: does a longitudinal cohort report the rate and/or predictors of
progression across a severity boundary (mild→moderate, moderate→severe, toward
housebound/bedbound) over years, in patients with ESTABLISHED (non-conversion,
non-prodromal) mild/moderate ME/CFS?

## Inclusion Criteria (all must hold)
- Longitudinal cohort design with follow-up ≥ 1 year.
- Follows ESTABLISHED ME/CFS (baseline already meets ME/CFS criteria). NOT a
  post-infectious/prodromal-conversion cohort only, NOT a Long-COVID-conversion cohort.
- Baseline severity is mild and/or moderate (or cohort has a mild/moderate severity
  distribution measured and reports change over time).
- Reports a rate and/or predictors of progression across a SEVERITY BOUNDARY
  (mild→moderate, moderate→severe, toward housebound/bedbound) over the follow-up.
- Peer-reviewed, full bibliographic record (PMID/DOI) verifiable.
- NOT already present in `bib/*.bib` (checked before inclusion).

## Exclusion Criteria
- Already-integrated natural-history/prognosis papers (Maksoud2020natural,
  Lacourt2022prognosis, Chu2019, Brown2013EnergyEnvelope, McManimen2016,
  Pendergrast2020housebound, kingdon2018functional, Roberts2016, Rowe2019pediatric,
  Cairns2005prognosis, GenRe2023, Polo2019, Sirotiak2025) — do NOT re-add.
- VanCampenEtAl2023 (PMID 38138257; ICC severity distribution shift 51/45/4%→1/72/27%
  in worsening-selected patients) — ALREADY in corpus under `autonomic-cardiovascular`;
  worsening-selected subgroup, not a population progression rate. Excluded on overlap.
- Sudden severe onset, recovery-rate-only studies (unless they also report the
  deterioration proportion across a severity boundary).
- Post-infectious conversion / IM→ME/CFS / post-COVID conversion cohorts.
- Pediatric-only cohorts (separate stream; Rowe2019pediatric already covers).
- Pure symptom-fluctuation (not severity-boundary) longitudinal studies (continuous
  fatigue-scale worsening without severity-tier reclassification).
- Treatment/intervention outcome trials, case reports, protocols.
- Off-topic noise (Long COVID, MS, Sjögren, Guillain-Barré, fibromyalgia, COPD,
  interstitial cystitis, Q-fever fatigue, Cochrane exercise reviews).
