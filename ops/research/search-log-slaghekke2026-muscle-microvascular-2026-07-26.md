## Inclusion Criteria
- Human subjects with ME/CFS and/or Long COVID studied for skeletal muscle microvascular/capillary pathology
- Muscle biopsy studies (IHC, EM) documenting capillary basement membrane thickness, capillarization, or tortuosity
- Exercise physiology studies assessing VO₂max, O₂ extraction, or CPET in ME/CFS relevant to capillary-level O₂ delivery
- Review papers synthesizing direct muscle biopsy evidence relevant to the microvascular hypothesis
- Physiology reference papers on capillary BM thickness and exercise capacity (general population)
- Publication date: 2010--2026 (post-2020 preferred for Long COVID)

## Exclusion Criteria
- Papers on cerebral blood flow, cardiac output, or macrovascular function without muscle-level capillary data
- Pure mitochondrial studies without capillary/microvascular component
- Papers focused exclusively on bed rest/deconditioning without patient data
- Animal studies (unless directly addressing capillary BM thickening mechanisms)
- Papers not in English
- Duplicate entries (use most recent/complete version)
