## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed primary studies, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, high-value reviews, or editorials with expert counterweight value.
- Directly about anti-TS-HDS and/or anti-FGFR3 antibodies in SFN / sensory neuropathy / dysautonomia / fibromyalgia (diagnostic utility, phenotype, mechanism, immunotherapy response).
- RCT / trial-level IVIG evidence in SFN (both antibody-positive and general).
- Validating OR validity-challenging/replication papers given equal weight (task requirement).
- ME/CFS-cohort serology (searched; none found — see research-gap note).

## Exclusion Criteria
- Case reports (Finsterer 2021/2023 vaccination cases; Mastropaolo 2023) — low-evidence, redundant signal for a contested-biomarker review.
- Editorials replying to other editorials (Zeidman 2023 "jury is still out" self-defence reply dropped to avoid duplication with Katzberg 2023).
- Preprints already superseded by peer-reviewed (Salih 2025 bioRxiv → superseded by Salih 2026 Adv Sci).
- POTS/pure-autonomic reviews without an SFN-antibody focus (Geerts protocol-only de Greef 2016 Trials dropped — superseded by Geerts 2021 result paper; Kesterson 2023 SCIG/POTS off-target).
- Off-topic FGFR3 hits (Zhou 2006 oligodendrocyte lineage; metabolic-syndrome neuropathy Kazamel 2021).
- Already-present corpus keys (listed in driver note above) — reused, not re-added.

## Research-gap findings (documented, NOT padded)
1. **No direct ME/CFS-cohort study** of anti-TS-HDS or anti-FGFR3 antibodies exists (query 8 = 0 ME/CFS-cohort hits). All ME/CFS relevance is inferential via (a) the fibromyalgia overlap (Malik 2019, Nissan 2023) and (b) general SFN substrate documented in ME/CFS (already in corpus).
2. No RCT has stratified ME/CFS or post-COVID SFN patients by TS-HDS/FGFR3 serostatus for IVIG/immunomodulation.
3. The two best population proxies to ME/CFS (fibromyalgia) show null (Malik 2019) or reverse-direction (Nissan 2023) anti-TS-HDS results — this is the most directly ME/CFS-relevant validity evidence and it is negative.
