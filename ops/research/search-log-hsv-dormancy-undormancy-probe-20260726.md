## Inclusion Criteria
- Direct relevance to HSV-1/2 latency or reactivation mechanisms
- HSV-1/2 serological or clinical data in ME/CFS populations
- Stress-catecholamine-HSV reactivation mechanistic studies
- HSV recurrence epidemiology (baseline rates for probe interpretation)
- Acyclovir/valacyclovir safety and resistance data
- Treg-CD8+ T cell immune surveillance of HSV latency

## Exclusion Criteria
- Herpes zoster / VZV only (unless mechanistic pathway shared)
- EBV/HHV-6 studies without HSV-1/2 data (unless mechanistic insight transferable)
- Pure antiviral pharmacokinetics without ME/CFS or HSV-recurrence context
- Case reports of single HSV episodes (unless directly connected to post-exertional context)
- Pre-1990 animal studies without translational framework

## Null/Negative Results
3 studies reported null/negative results for HSV in ME/CFS:
- Buchwald 1996 — n=548, no HSV-1/2 serological differences
- Koelle 2002 — 22 twin pairs, no differences
- Blomberg 2019 (already in bib) — no significant overall herpesvirus IgG differences
