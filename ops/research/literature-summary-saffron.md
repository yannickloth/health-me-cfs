# Saffron (Crocus sativus) — Literature Summary
Date: 2026-08-22
Topic slug: saffron-tryptophan-serotonin
Phase: 1 (literature research) of /integrate-topic conditional candidate

## Purpose
Assess whether saffron (proposed by Tomaso as a microbiome/tryptophan modulator) warrants integration into the ME/CFS paper. Saffron is entirely absent as a treatment topic (only pre-existing entry: Elbaga2026, a crocin/Alzheimer's rat study in the kynurenine-excitotoxicity stream). CONDITIONAL candidate: evidence must establish relevance to ME/CFS or the tryptophan/serotonin axis.

## Headline verdict
Relevance criterion **MET via the tryptophan/serotonin/kynurenine-axis + gut-microbiome-tryptophan mechanisms**. NOT met via primary ME/CFS clinical evidence (0 primary-ME/CFS saffron trials exist). General mood modulation alone would NOT have sufficed; the axis mechanisms provide the required specificity.

## Evidence by relevance criterion

### 1. Direct ME/CFS / chronic fatigue evidence — WEAK (no primary ME/CFS trials)
- **Dastan 2025** (Iran J Pharm Res): RCT, saffron 30 mg bid x 8 wk in COPD patients with CFS. Significant CFS reduction (MCFS P<0.001), QOL improvement. Secondary CFS comorbid with COPD, NOT primary ME/CFS. Discounted cert 0.18.
- **Moghimi Dehkordi 2026** (Iran J Pharm Res): RCT, saffron 30 mg bid x 60 d in pulmonary sarcoidosis. Reduced fatigue (FAS, PROMIS-fatigue P<0.001), improved sleep, QOL. Comorbid chronic-lung disease. Discounted cert 0.17.
- **Hajhashemy 2026** (Food Funct): RCT, saffron 100 mg/d x 12 wk in Parkinson's. Reduced fatigue (P<0.001), CRP, depression, sleep disturbance. Cross-disease oxidative-stress population. Discounted cert 0.20.
- **Gap:** 0 saffron trials in primary ME/CFS. The direct-fatigue evidence is entirely secondary-fatigue/comorbid or cross-disease.

### 2. Tryptophan / serotonin / kynurenine axis — STRONG (mechanism-level; KEY criterion) ✓
- **De la Fuente Muñoz 2023** (Antioxidants): rat, affron standardized saffron extract DECREASED serum kynurenine and increased melatonin. Direct kynurenine-pathway modulation — bridges to serotonergic-bottleneck, kynurenine-dopamine, IDO-trap models. Discounted cert 0.11 (animal).
- **Mohammadi 2023** (Phytomedicine): crocin/crocetin bind SERT (SLC6A4) at the fluoxetine site, increase serum serotonin, reduce corticosterone; crocetin inhibits NMDA NR2B. Discounted cert 0.11 (animal).
- **Wauquier 2022** (Nutrients): HUMAN ex vivo — post-ingestion saffron metabolites inhibit SERT expression, stimulate dopamine/serotonin release, protect neurons from oxidative stress, increase BDNF. Discounted cert 0.28 (human ex vivo).
- **Monchaux de Oliveira 2026** (Food Funct): mouse, safranal selectively downregulates neurotoxic kynurenine-pathway components (neuroprotective shift); crocins modulate dopamine. Discounted cert 0.11 (animal).
- **De Monte 2014** (Eur J Med Chem): crocin/safranal inhibit human MAO-A/B (mechanism + harm: MAOI-like action). Discounted cert 0.14 (in vitro).

### 3. Gut microbiome modulation — MODERATE (microbiome-tryptophan) ✓
- **Horvath 2026** (Food Sci Nutr): in vitro defined human gut consortium — saffron REDUCES microbial tryptophan and increases its downstream products TRYPTAMINE and INDOLE ACETIC ACID; elevates GABA/glutamate/glycine/dopamine; shifts SCFAs. Direct microbiome-tryptophan/AHR-pathway modulation. Discounted cert 0.14 (ex vivo microbial).
- **Lang 2025** (Food Funct): human pilot, saffron improves sleep via gut-microbiota-brain axis (increases Faecalibacterium, Prevotella, etc.). Discounted cert 0.33 (healthy older adults).

### 4. Null / negative evidence — PRESENT (mandatory to report)
- **Amadieu 2025** (Am J Clin Nutr, NCT05690126): RCT in healthy adults with subclinical depression — saffron did NOT improve composite depression/anxiety/fatigue nor individual symptoms. Only weak SF-12 mental-health effect. Discounted cert 0.42. CRITICAL NULL.
- **Fazilat 2025** (Bioimpacts, screened, not added): systematic review of phytotherapy for depression notes saffron efficacy but "not all trials yielded favorable results" — effect heterogeneity.

### 5. Harm / adverse effects — PRESENT (mandatory for treatment topics)
- **Căuș 2026** (Pharmaceuticals): herbal serotonergic supplements (incl. Crocus sativus) carry serotonin-syndrome risk with SSRIs/MAOIs, CYP450 drug interactions, excessive sedation, adulteration. Discounted cert 0.47.
- **Alshdefat 2026** (J Evid Based Integr Med): pregnancy review — low-dose saffron may facilitate cervical ripening; HIGHER doses linked to uterine stimulation (abortifacient risk). Discounted cert 0.47.
- Mechanism-based harm: saffron's SERT inhibition (Wauquier 2022, Mohammadi 2023) + MAO inhibition (De Monte 2014) together mean serotonin-syndrome risk when combined with SSRIs/MAOIs or other serotonergic drugs — the paper's serotonin-syndrome/contraindication content should reflect this.

### General mood context (NOT the basis for proceeding, but documented)
- **Dimech 2026** (Cureus): review — saffron (30 mg/d) comparable to fluoxetine/imipramine in mild-moderate depression, underpowered. Discounted cert 0.47.
- Zhong 2026 (Front Nutr, screened): culinary-spices antidepressant review (saffron among turmeric, chili, etc.).

## Go/No-Go Honesty
- **Strong:** tryptophan/serotonin/kynurenine-axis mechanisms (SERT inhibition, kynurenine reduction, melatonin increase, gut-microbial tryptophan→tryptamine/indole-acetic-acid) — multiple independent lines, some human (Wauquier ex vivo).
- **Weak:** NO primary ME/CFS clinical evidence; all fatigue RCTs are comorbid-secondary (COPD, sarcoidosis) or cross-disease (Parkinson's).
- **Missing:** no saffron trial in ME/CFS, no saffron microbiome study in ME/CFS, no saffron fatigue RCT powered for primary fatigue.
- **Null:** Amadieu 2025 shows no effect on fatigue/depression in healthy subclinical adults.
- **Harm:** serotonin-syndrome risk with SSRI/MAOI combination; pregnancy uterine-stimulation risk at high dose.

## Recommended disposition for Phase 2 decision
The topic satisfies the conditional relevance criterion via the tryptophan/serotonin-axis mechanisms (the paper's core serotonergic-bottleneck and tryptophan-kynurenine models) and the gut-microbiome-tryptophan pathway. It should be framed as a **serotonergic/kynurenine-axis and microbiome modulator** whose clinical case in ME/CFS is UNPROVEN — suitable for a low-certainty speculation/consideration entry in the treatment chapter (serotonergic bottleneck) and gut-microbiome content, NOT as an established ME/CFS treatment. The null result (Amadieu) and harm profile must be stated.
