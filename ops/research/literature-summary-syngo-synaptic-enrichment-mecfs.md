## Scope & purpose

The driving source is an unpublished (blog-documented) MAGMA gene-set enrichment run of SynGO v1.3 against DecodeME and DecodeME+MVP GWAS summary statistics. This summary gathers the citable, peer-reviewed evidence that: (1) establishes the SynGO knowledge base itself, (2) establishes the SV2A synaptic-density PET correlate (the proposed next step), (3) provides indirect biochemical links between synaptic/glutamatergic dysfunction and ME/CFS / Long COVID, and (4) records cross-disease glutamatergic-synapse precedents.

**Already-integrated context (NOT re-added):** Maccallini 2026 meta-GWAS (n=19,470) — already establishes glutamatergic synaptic dysfunction as a genetically-driven core ME/CFS mechanism; cited as `@Maccallini2026metaGWAS`. Wirth & Scheibenbogen 2026 glutamate/GABA imbalance review — already `@WirthScheibenbogen2026glutamate`.

## Included papers (8)

### Group A — SynGO methodology (direct)

**1. Koopmans et al. 2019 — SynGO: An Evidence-Based, Expert-Curated Knowledge Base for the Synapse.** *Neuron* 103(2):217-234.e4. PMID 31171447. DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2019.05.002.
- **Quality:** High. Landmark methods paper in top journal (Neuron). Multi-institutional expert consortium (CNCR Amsterdam, Broad/Stanley Center, MPI Göttingen, Yale, Stanford, UCSF, etc.).
- **Sample/design:** Not a patient study — a knowledge base. 87 synaptic locations + 179 synaptic processes, 2,922 annotations for 1,112 genes, exclusively expert-curated evidence.
- **Key claim relevant here:** Synaptic genes are exceptionally conserved and intolerant to LoF mutations; SynGO terms overrepresented among genes associated with intelligence, educational attainment, ADHD, autism, bipolar disorder, and neurodevelopmental disorder de novo variants (incl. schizophrenia).
- **Certainty:** raw 0.85 (top-journal methodology, no replication needed — it is the reference resource). **Population weight:** 0.30 (in silico/knowledge-base — no patient cohort). **Discounted:** 0.26. *Role: tooling/grounding, not a patient finding.*

**2. (reused) MAGMA gene-set methodology — de Leeuw 2015, already in bib (`deLeeuw2015MAGMA`).** Not re-added; noted as the analytic method the driving source used.

### Group B — SV2A synaptic-density PET correlate (the proposed imaging next step)

**3. Onwordi et al. 2020 — Synaptic density marker SV2A is reduced in schizophrenia patients and unaffected by antipsychotics in rats.** *Nature Communications* 11(1):246. PMID 31937764. DOI 10.1038/s41467-019-14122-0.
- **Quality:** High. Nature Communications. n=18 SCZ vs 18 controls, [11C]UCB-J PET + rat pharmacology control.
- **Key finding:** SV2A lower in frontal/ACC cortex in schizophrenia (Cohen's d=0.8–0.9); antipsychotic exposure does not confound (rat validation).
- **Certainty:** raw 0.75. **Population weight:** 0.50 (other-disease model — schizophrenia). **Discounted:** 0.38.

**4. Onwordi et al. 2021 — The relationship between synaptic density marker SV2A, glutamate and N-acetyl aspartate levels in healthy volunteers and schizophrenia.** *Translational Psychiatry* 11(1):393. PMID 34282130. DOI 10.1038/s41398-021-01515-3.
- **Quality:** High. n=40 (18 SCZ, 22 HV), [11C]UCB-J PET + 1H-MRS in hippocampus/ACC.
- **Key finding:** SV2A correlated with Glu/Cr and Glx/Cr in healthy volunteers but NOT in schizophrenia — i.e., the MRS glutamatergic signal tracks synaptic density in health, and this coupling is lost in schizophrenia. The thread cites this as the SV2A precedent.
- **Certainty:** raw 0.70. **Population weight:** 0.50. **Discounted:** 0.35.

**5. Asch et al. 2024 — Challenges and rewards of in vivo synaptic density imaging, and its application to the study of depression.** *Neuropsychopharmacology* 50(1):153-163. PMID 39039139. DOI 10.1038/s41386-024-01913-3.
- **Quality:** High (Neuropsychopharmacology, Yale PET Center group). Review of SV2A tracer development ([11C]UCB-J, [18F]SynVesT-1) + depression application + ketamine synaptogenesis challenge.
- **Key finding:** Establishes SV2A PET as the in-vivo synaptic-density method for neuropsychiatric disorders; [18F]SynVesT-1 patent (Carson is inventor — COI disclosed).
- **Certainty:** raw 0.70 (review, top journal). **Population weight:** 0.75 (general/methodological). **Discounted:** 0.53.

**6. Serrano et al. 2022 — Imaging Synaptic Density: The Next Holy Grail of Neuroscience?** *Frontiers in Neuroscience* 16:796129. PMID 35401097. DOI 10.3389/fnins.2022.796129.
- **Quality:** Medium (Frontiers review). Critical assessment of SV2A PET vs ex-vivo vs MR-based glutamate methods.
- **Key finding:** SV2A PET has limited specificity; MR-based glutamate methods are non-invasive alternatives. Good for methodological caveats.
- **Certainty:** raw 0.60. **Population weight:** 0.75. **Discounted:** 0.45.

### Group C — Indirect biochemical links (synaptic/glutamatergic dysfunction in ME/CFS / Long COVID)

**7. Thapaliya et al. 2024 — Imbalanced Brain Neurochemicals in Long COVID and ME/CFS (MRS).** *American Journal of Medicine* 138(3):567-574. PMID 38588934. DOI 10.1016/j.amjmed.2024.04.007.
- **Quality:** Medium. n=17 ME/CFS + 17 Long COVID + 10 HC; posterior cingulate MRS (3T).
- **Key finding:** Glutamate significantly higher in both ME/CFS (P=.017) and Long COVID (P=.02) vs controls; NAA higher in Long COVID. No ME/CFS vs LC difference.
- **Cohort overlap:** Griffith University NCNED group (Marshall-Gradisnik, Barnden, Thapaliya) — same group as Thapaliya 2022/2023 brainstem MRI papers already in bib. **Not the same cohort as the Maccallini 2026 GWAS** (different modality, different recruitment), but shares PI group with existing neuroinflammation bib entries.
- **Certainty:** raw 0.55. **Population weight:** 1.00 (ME/CFS cohort). **Discounted:** 0.55.

**8. Williams et al. 2019 — Epstein-Barr Virus dUTPase Induces Neuroinflammatory Mediators: Implications for ME/CFS.** *Clinical Therapeutics* 41(5):848-863. PMID 31040055. DOI 10.1016/j.clinthera.2019.04.009.
- **Quality:** Medium. In vitro (astrocytes/microglia/BMEC) + in vivo mouse (C57Bl/6).
- **Key finding:** EBV dUTPase protein alters expression of genes controlling BBB integrity AND **synaptic plasticity** — 12/15 genes in vitro, 34/84 in vivo, including pain synapse structure/function, dopamine/serotonin/GABA/glutamate pathways.
- **Indirect-link logic:** Topic's biochemistry is synaptic structure/function; this paper connects a documented ME/CFS viral trigger (EBV) to altered synaptic-plasticity gene expression — a mechanistic bridge from the infection-trigger narrative to the synaptic-genetic signal.
- **Certainty:** raw 0.55. **Population weight:** 0.50 (in vitro + animal + ME/CFS-subset serology). **Discounted:** 0.28.

**9. Wang et al. 2021 — Emerging role of glutamate in the pathophysiology and therapeutics of Gulf War illness.** *Life Sciences* 280:119609. PMID 33991547. DOI 10.1016/j.lfs.2021.119609.
- **Quality:** Medium (review). Cross-disease glutamatergic-synapse precedent in a chronic fatigue/multisymptom illness (GWI).
- **Key finding:** GWI rodent models show elevated extracellular glutamate + impaired glutamatergic synapse structure/function; restoring glutamatergic synapses ameliorates deficits; low-glutamate diet reduces GWI symptoms.
- **Certainty:** raw 0.55. **Population weight:** 0.80 (cross-disease chronic-fatigue/multisymptom). **Discounted:** 0.44.

## Cohort-overlap verdict (MANDATORY, skill Augment B)

**No paper in this set shares a GWAS cohort with the driving source (DecodeME / DecodeME+MVP).** All included papers are either (a) knowledge-base/methods papers with no patient cohort (Koopmans 2019), (b) schizophrenia/healthy-volunteer imaging cohorts (Onwordi 2020/2021, Asch 2024 — Imperial/KCL/Yale, unrelated to DecodeME), or (c) small neuroimaging/mechanistic ME/CFS studies (Thapaliya 2024 Griffith NCNED; Williams 2019 Ohio State; Wang 2021 OSU).

**Overlap flags within the *included* set:**
- Onwordi 2020 and Onwordi 2021: **same PI (Howes O.D.) + same MRC London Institute of Medical Sciences / Imperial College London / KCL + same [11C]UCB-J platform** — flag as shared-group; the 2021 cohort is largely the same SCZ/HV imaging population. Treat the two Onwordi papers as one group, not two independent replications.
- Thapaliya 2024: shares PI group (Marshall-Gradisnik, Barnden, Thapaliya) with pre-existing bib entries (Thapaliya 2022/2023 brainstem MRI) — not within this topic's new additions, but noted for cross-topic consistency.
- Williams 2019 vs Cox 2022 / Palomo 2026 / Ariza 2025 (already in bib): same Ohio State dUTPase group; Williams 2019 is the upstream synaptic-plasticity mechanistic paper of that line.

**Verdict:** No duplicate/overlapping patient cohorts among the new additions relative to the DecodeME GWAS. The only within-set overlap (Onwordi 2020 ↔ 2021) is flagged above.

## Null / contradicting / competing / indirect papers

- **Null:** No published study tests or refutes SynGO synaptic enrichment in ME/CFS (queries returned zero). The enrichment claim is currently single-investigator (unpublished) + converging indirect support — NOT yet independently replicated or falsified. Record this as a key limitation.
- **Competing mechanism (immune):** DecodeME's own published finding (and the abstract already states it) is that genetic risk maps to neuronal/brain cell types with **zero** immune-cell enrichment — so the "competing" immune-enrichment hypothesis is already resolved *against* by the primary GWAS, not by any paper added here. No new immune-enrichment paper added (nothing new found).
- **Competing mechanism (mitochondrial):** Keele 2026 and Huang 2026 mGWAS (already in bib) support mitochondrial/metabolic perturbation — complementary to, not exclusive of, synaptic enrichment (synaptic transmission is energetically the most expensive process; mitochondrial and synaptic findings can co-exist). No new paper needed.
- **Indirect biochemical links:** Thapaliya 2024 (glutamate↑ in ME/CFS/Long COVID), Williams 2019 (EBV→synaptic-plasticity genes), Wang 2021 (GWI glutamatergic synapse) — these map the topic's biochemistry (synaptic structure/glutamatergic signaling) onto documented ME/CFS / chronic-fatigue pathophysiology even absent a direct SynGO-in-ME/CFS study.
