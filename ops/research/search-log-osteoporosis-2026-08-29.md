## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed journal article (BMJ Open, Calcif Tissue Int, J Biol Chem, Front Sports Act Living, JBMR, BMJ, Core Evidence, Indian J Orthop).
- Directly addresses vitamin D status / bone health / osteoporosis risk in ME/CFS, OR in a directly analogous deconditioned/immobilized/bedbound population.
- Indirect-biochemical: mechanism linking mechanical unloading / reduced muscle contraction to bone remodeling (RANKL/OPG, sclerostin, osteocyte mechanosensing).
- Harm/safety: bisphosphonates (alendronate, risedronate), anabolic therapies (teriparatide), calcium supplementation — adverse effects, contraindications, cardiovascular and fracture risk.
- Bedrest precedent: disuse osteoporosis in immobilization, spinal cord injury, bed rest.
- Metadata (PMID, DOI, author, volume/page) verified via esummary/efetch.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Direct "osteoporosis AND chronic fatigue" / "calcium deficiency AND chronic fatigue" searches returned only irrelevant results (PBC, celiac, runners, jawbone defects, Ehlers-Danlos, hypogonadism) — documented as research gaps, not included.
- Animal-model unloading papers (tail suspension, 3D-printed unloading device, melatonin/cell-free-fat rescue) — superseded by the Rolvien2022 clinical-mechanistic review and Qin2013 RANKL/OPG mechanism paper.
- Individual bisphosphonate case reports where a task-force report / review supersedes the class signal (Shane2014, Arjunan2023).
- Vitamin D fatigue trials in non-ME/CFS populations (cancer, pediatric rheumatic, runners, dysmenorrhea) — not generalizable; Earl2017 is the direct null.
- Teriparatide meta-analyses in postmenopausal/general OP (2025) where TaylorSaag2019 already provides the anabolic safety review.

## Notes

- Earl2017 is the single most decision-relevant paper: it is a direct, peer-reviewed cohort finding that **contradicts** the MEA claim of universal vitamin D deficiency in ME/CFS. It does not exclude deficiency in a severely-housebound subset (the Earl cohort was not severity-stratified), so the MEA prevention advice retains face validity for the most severe patients but loses its universal-evidence basis.
