# Literature Summary: EpiSwitch 3D-Genomic Diagnostic for ME/CFS

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Compiled by:** literature-integrator
**Research stream:** `episwitch`
**Seed paper:** Hunter et al. 2025 (_J Transl Med_, PMID 41057909) — ALREADY in bib as `Hunter2025` (n=47 severe ME/CFS + 61 controls; 200 chromosome-conformation markers; 92% sensitivity / 98% specificity / 96% accuracy; IL-2/JAK-STAT enrichment). Not re-added.

---

## Headline Findings

1. **No independent replication exists.** Zero published EpiSwitch ME/CFS validation cohort in PubMed. The 92%/98% figures derive from a single retrospective case/control study with an internal (not external) validation split.
2. **Cross-condition specificity is UNTESTED.** The 98% specificity was against healthy controls only. No EpiSwitch study has ever compared ME/CFS against fibromyalgia, depression, post-viral fatigue, or other fatiguing conditions.
3. **The platform is real and cross-disease validated (vendor lineage).** EpiSwitch has clinical-utility data in prostate cancer (PSE), immunotherapy response (CiRT), and prior-art in RA (MTX response) and ALS. But all such papers are authored by Oxford BioDynamics employees — commercial COI throughout.
4. **Independent mechanistic support for the IL-2/JAK-STAT axis exists** (TRIM28→IL-2, CTCF→STAT5/JAK inhibitor rewiring, IL-2-driven 3D genome restructuring, miR-146a enhancer in lupus). This is non-vendor, high-quality, but *not* ME/CFS-specific.
5. **Competing blood epigenetic biomarkers are active** and one (HERV profiling) achieves perfect ME/CFS-vs-fibromyalgia separation — a claim EpiSwitch has not tested.

## Replication Status

**Not replicated.** The EpiSwitch ME/CFS test is a single retrospective case/control study (EPI-ME). The paper's own "independent validation cohort" is an internal split of the retrospectively collected samples, not an external cohort. No follow-up, no registry trial, and no third-party validation exists in PubMed as of 2026-08-15.

Supporting context:
- The *platform* (not the ME/CFS test) has accumulated cross-disease clinical-utility evidence (prostate PSE, CiRT immunotherapy, RA MTX-response, ALS) — but exclusively from Oxford BioDynamics and its collaborators (commercial COI).
- The *mechanism* (3D chromatin ↔ IL-2/JAK-STAT) is independently supported by non-vendor work (Wei 2025, Lee 2025, Ward 2025, Zhu 2024).

## Cross-Condition Specificity Findings

**Critical gap.** Hunter 2025 reports 98% specificity versus healthy controls only. The test has never been assessed against:
- Fibromyalgia — where a competing approach (HERV profiling, Giménez-Orenga 2025) achieves *perfect* ME/CFS-vs-FM group separation.
- Depression / major depressive disorder (fatigue-dominant).
- Long COVID / post-viral fatigue (large phenotypic overlap).
- Idiopathic chronic fatigue (the classic diagnostic-discrimination challenge).

Implication: the headline specificity cannot be interpreted as "specific to ME/CFS" — it is only "specific against healthy individuals." This is a major evidential ceiling for any diagnostic claim and must be stated explicitly wherever the test is cited.

## Integration Decision Input (for the main session)

1. **Is there replication evidence?** No. Single study, vendor-affiliated, no external cohort. The platform is real and the mechanism is independently plausible, but the ME/CFS diagnostic itself is un-replicated.
2. **Does EpiSwitch distinguish ME/CFS from other fatiguing conditions?** Unknown — never tested. Its specificity is against healthy controls only. A competing method (HERV) has demonstrated ME/CFS-vs-FM discrimination that EpiSwitch has not.
3. **Recommended classification for integration:** treat the EpiSwitch ME/CFS result as an **early-stage, vendor-affiliated, single-cohort finding** (certainty ~0.50, matching the existing bib entry), NOT as a validated diagnostic. Any prose citing "98% specificity" must carry the "healthy controls only" caveat and note the absence of fibromyalgia/depression/long-COVID discrimination testing. The IL-2/JAK-STAT mechanistic axis is the strongest independent element and can be integrated with higher confidence via Wei 2025 / Lee 2025.
