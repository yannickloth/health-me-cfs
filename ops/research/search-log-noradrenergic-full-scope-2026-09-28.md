## Inclusion Criteria

- Directly addresses noradrenergic synthesis, release, reuptake, receptors, or noradrenergic-tone consequences.
- Human ME/CFS/POTS, OR a mechanistic human/animal result that specifies a noradrenergic node relevant to ME/CFS.
- Peer-reviewed; reviews admissible for mechanism framing only.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Parkinson's-disease motor pharmacology with no fatigue/cognition transfer (COMT inhibitors).
- Preclinical-only α2 antagonist tool compounds with no human safety data.
- Antidepressant adverse-effect reviews that do not isolate the noradrenergic arm.
- Papers already in the bib (Fedorowski2017, Li2019, Hauglund2025, Zhu2025, Young2025, Mwesigwa2024, Sulheim2014).
