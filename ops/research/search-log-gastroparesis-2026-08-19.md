## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed original research, reviews, or consensus guidelines
- Direct relevance to gastric emptying / gastroparesis / gastric dysmotility, OR to the autonomic/vagal/cholinergic or ICC/enteric mechanisms that mediate it, OR to prokinetic treatment and its safety
- Relevance to ME/CFS, Long COVID / post-viral, POTS / hypermobility / dysautonomia, diabetic gastroparesis (cross-disease comparison), or gastroparesis generally (for mechanism/treatment)
- Provides clinical, mechanistic, diagnostic, or safety data (not merely epidemiological)
- Publication date: any (foundational papers may be older)

## Exclusion Criteria

- Non-English language
- Duplicate of existing bib entries (GastricDysmotility2023 Vasquez-Marti 2023, Gijsbers2023gastric, Steinsvik2023GIdysmotility, Pimentel2009prokinetics, SIBO/butyrate stream papers, vagal-gastric Long COVID stream papers incl. Acanfora2026)
- Purely epidemiological without mechanistic or treatment relevance
- Case reports of marginal relevance (e.g., GJ-tube placement mechanics, perioperative pancreatic surgery scintigraphy)
- Preprints without verification (none met relevance bar)
- Papers about gastroparesis in unrelated surgical/postsurgical contexts (pancreatic surgery, cholecystectomy)

## Null / Negative Results

- **Corrado 1998** (Ital J Gastroenterol Hepatol 30(4):444-445) — a single-adolescent case report of CFS with **normal** gastric emptying time and myoelectrical activity. This is the only null/negative finding recovered. It is an n=1 case report (low evidentiary weight) and does not contradict the larger n=32 Burnet 2004 finding.
- Queries specifically targeting failed replications / null evidence (`delayed gastric emptying chronic fatigue failed replication`, `gastric emptying ME/CFS null`) returned 0 hits. No systematic null-replication literature exists for ME/CFS gastric emptying.

## Cohort-Overlap / Diagnostic-Criteria Notes

- **Burnet 2004** (n=32 CFS) and **Corrado 1998** (n=1 adolescent) are separate cohorts from different eras/institutions; no overlap.
- **Grover 2011** and **Wang 2009** are independent gastroparesis tissue studies (human diabetic/idiopathic gastric antral tissue vs streptozotocin mouse model); no overlap.
- **Tseng 2019** (POTS, Mayo Clinic) and **Aziz 2025** (AGA hEDS/autonomic, multi-author expert panel) are separate.
- Diagnostic criteria: the 2004 Burnet study predates the IOM 2015 criteria and used the contemporary CDC/Fukuda-style CFS definition (criteria not stated precisely in abstract; assumed Fukuda-era). Corrado 1998 likewise used an earlier pediatric CFS definition. All other clinical papers (gastroparesis, POTS, hEDS) use disease-specific (non-ME/CFS) criteria. Flagged: **no modern paper uses ICC/IOM criteria for the ME/CFS cohorts** — a real diagnostic-anchoring gap.
