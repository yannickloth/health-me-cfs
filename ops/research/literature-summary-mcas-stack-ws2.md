# Literature Summary: MCAS-Stack Drug Mechanisms — Workstream 2

**Topic slug:** `mcas-stack-drug-mechanisms` (Workstream 2 of 3: diagnostic instruments + pain/arousal bridges)
**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Ideas researched:** C1–C4 (drugs as diagnostic instruments) + D1–D5 (pain/arousal bridges)

## Bottom Line

Nine ideas researched. **Five have genuine mechanistic literature support** (C1, C2, C4, D1, D2) but almost entirely at the *indirect-biochemical / general-population / animal* level, not ME/CFS-specific. **One idea (D5) is a zero-hit gap.** **Three ideas (C3, D3, D4) are partial** — the mechanism is real but the specific diagnostic/probe claim is untested. The strongest single mechanistic links are histamine→TRPV1 sensitization (D1, Nikolaeva-Koleva 2021) and histamine→orexin arousal (D2, Sakurai 2021). The strongest clinical-population anchor is the 36.3% MCAS prevalence within POTS/dysautonomia (Kulin 2026), which defines the patient subset in whom these drug probes would apply.

## Evidence Landscape

**Strong:** histamine→TRPV1 sensitization (D1), histamine→orexin arousal relay (D2), MCAS prevalence in POTS (C2), anti-M3R diagnostic in SjD (C1), montelukast CNS activity + harm (D4). All are real, but none are ME/CFS-specific — the best clinical anchors are POTS (0.80 weight) and SjD (0.75 weight), not ME/CFS cohorts (1.00).

**Weak / missing:** no pyridostigmine-M3-bioassay study; no mediator-response clustering; no MCAS non-response fingerprinting; no montelukast brain-fog trial; no PAF-microclot trial; no mast-cell energy-cost measurement.

**Null:** Abbotts2025 (histamine blockade does not change exercise capacity) is the one direct null, and it constrains both C4 and D5 — histamine is not simply coupled to exercise capacity in healthy adults.

## Contradiction Detection

- **NiijimaYaoita2012 (histamine favours endurance, mouse) vs Abbotts2025 (histamine blockade no effect on severe-domain exercise, human).** Resolvable: different species (mouse vs human), different histamine manipulation (HDC knockout/H1 blockade vs acute combined H1/H2 blockade), different exercise domains. Not a fundamental contradiction, but it bounds the strength of the histamine→fatigue claim. Recommend framing C4 as a narrow *central-arousal* (sedation) effect, not a broad *exercise-capacity* effect.

## What This Evidence Does / Does Not Support

- **Supports:** histamine and its receptors bridge the mast-cell system to pain (TRPV1) and arousal (orexin/wakefulness); M3 autoantibodies are a real diagnostic signal in the SjD/ME/CFS autoimmune terrain; a large MCAS-overlap subset exists within POTS/dysautonomia; montelukast reaches the CNS with real neuropsychiatric effects.
- **Does NOT support:** any claim that the four drugs function as validated diagnostic instruments; any response-profile clustering into MCAS subtypes; any mast-cell energy-cost quantification; any PAF→microclot therapeutic effect.
- **Clinical relevance:** LOW–MEDIUM as diagnostic instruments (untested); MEDIUM as mechanistic context; the montelukast harm signal is HIGH-relevance for safety framing. Severity applicability: unknown for all ideas (no study stratified by ME/CFS severity).
