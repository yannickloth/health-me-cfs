## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed journal article (or preprint if recent + directly relevant).
- Directly addresses: L-theanine, caffeine, L-theanine-caffeine combination, ADHD/selective attention, OR downstream mechanisms (adenosine receptor, GABA/glutamate, purinergic signaling, caffeine metabolism) intersecting ME/CFS, chronic fatigue, PEM, or attention/fatigue.
- Published 2012–2026.
- Harm/safety papers retained where they document caffeine or L-theanine adverse-effect/contraindication signals relevant to fatigue or sleep.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Animal feed/agriculture reviews (Wu 2026).
- Bioproduction/engineering reviews (Ren 2026).
- In vitro neural-stem-cell mechanism with no fatigue/attention clinical link (Yoneda 2020).
- General psychiatric tea-component reviews with no ME/CFS (Han 2024).
- Bioethics commentary (Ricci 2020).
- Non-relevant incidental caffeine mentions (Arnaout 2023 IBS; Rastogi 2018 nephrology; Gopaluni 2016 CKD-RLS; Tobback 2020 PLMS).
- Duplicate PMIDs across queries.
