# Literature Summary: LDN-vs-Metformin Mechanistic Bifurcation Triage

Topic: an explicit phenotype-guided first-line selection rule — low-dose
naltrexone (LDN) for a neuroimmune/TRPM3-dominant phenotype vs metformin for an
immunometabolic phenotype (insulin resistance, AMPK/mTOR dysregulation) — in
ME/CFS / post-infectious disease.

Date: 2026-09-09
Companion files: `search-log-ldn-metformin-bifurcation-20260909.md`, plan
`ops/plans/ldn-metformin-bifurcation-triage-integration-plan.md`.

---

## Core question

Does ANY evidence base support phenotype-guided first-line selection between LDN
and metformin in ME/CFS/post-infectious disease? If not, are the phenotype
markers / drug-response predictors needed for such a triage present?

**Answer: NO direct evidence exists for the triage rule.** No study has compared
LDN vs metformin head-to-head in ME/CFS or long COVID, and no published marker
predicts a differential response to one over the other. The two arms rest on
entirely separate evidence threads that have never been joined, and each thread
lacks the specific predictive marker the triage would require. The rule is a
plausible-but-unvalidated stratification hypothesis → `#speculation` / `#open-question`.

## Per-paper summaries

### LDN arm — neuroimmune/TRPM3

**1. Sasso2026trpm3 (multisite TRPM3 confirmation) — REUSE, PMID 41585253**
ME/CFS NK-cell TRPM3 dysfunction confirmed at scale (36 ME/CFS vs 42 HC, 2 labs);
no laboratory-site effect → consistent biomarker. ME/CFS weight 1.00. Certainty
0.70. Establishes TRPM3 as a plausible LDN-arm phenotype marker in ME/CFS. Does
NOT link TRPM3 level to clinical LDN response.

**2. Sasso2025LDNtrpm3LC (NEW, immune.bib) — PMID 40458265**
Companion study: LDN-treated long-COVID patients show restored TRPM3 NK-cell
function (n=9 per group, patch-clamp). Extends the LDN-TRPM3 restoration to LC.
In-vitro/ex-vivo, single group (Griffith). LC weight 0.85, certainty 0.55 →
discounted 0.47. Mechanism/response-plausibility plank; NO predictive-validity
evidence (does not show TRPM3 assay predicts who benefits from LDN clinically).

**3. Corpus reuse (LDN evidence & harm) — Gouda2026LDNevidence** (105-study
umbrella review: LDN generally safe; early positives rarely survive
placebo-controlled testing), **Byambasuren2026LDNlongcovid** (LDN-LC meta: no
serious AEs, low certainty), **FINAL-trial LDN null re-analyses** (fibromyalgia
responder heterogeneity, null secondary outcomes). Together: LDN has a plausible
TRPM3 mechanism but a weak, heterogeneous clinical-response record and NO
predictive biomarker. Certainty on clinical efficacy low.

### Metformin arm — immunometabolic

**4. Ursini2018MetforminAutoimmunity (NEW, immune.bib) — PMID 29915588**
Review: metformin suppresses Th17, promotes Treg, via AMPK/mTOR + complex I.
Defines the "immunometabolic phenotype" target mechanism. Autoimmune weight 0.75,
certainty 0.70 → 0.53. No predictive marker.

**5. Sun2020MetforminSLE (NEW, immune.bib) — PMID 38268156**
Multicentre double-blind placebo RCT: metformin add-on safe + modestly efficacious
in non-diabetic SLE. Supports metformin's disease-modifying immune action at
tolerated doses. Autoimmune 0.75, certainty 0.70 → 0.53.

**6. Sun2020MetforminSLEflare (NEW, immune.bib) — PMID 33093216**
Post hoc pooled of two SLE RCTs: metformin reduced flares in low-activity SLE.
Autoimmune 0.75, certainty 0.60 → 0.45.

**7. Gharib2021MetforminRA (NEW, immune.bib) — PMID 34630103**
RCT (n=66): metformin add-on improved RA activity + metabolic parameters.
Single-centre, single-blind. Autoimmune 0.75, certainty 0.55 → 0.41.

Note (4-7): all support that metformin modulates inflammatory/immune pathways, but
NONE stratified by an insulin-resistance/AMPK/mTOR biomarker, and none is in a
fatigue disease. They supply mechanistic plausibility, not phenotype predictors.

**8. Zhou2011ATMmetformin (NEW, treatments.bib) — PMID 21186350**
Canonical GoDARTS+UKPDS GWAS: rs11212617 near ATM predicts metformin glycemic
response (n=3,920, OR 1.35, P=2.9e-9). The best-documented metformin-response
genetic signal; even so, explains a small fraction of response variance, in the
CLEAREST indication (T2DM). T2DM 0.75, certainty 0.80 → 0.60. Implication: in a
heterogeneous fatigue population with no metabolic indication, a metformin-response
predictor would be far weaker. No HOMA-IR/metabolomics predictor has prospective
predictive validity equivalent to even this weak genetic signal.

**9. Corpus reuse (metformin in ME/CFS): Fineberg2025Metformin** (perspective,
AMPK→mTOR rationale, certainty 0.35), **Brown2018AMPK** (ME/CFS muscle cells:
pharmacological AMPK activation works, in-vitro, ME/CFS 1.00), **Shankar2025oxidativestress**
(PNAS; metformin reduces T-cell hyperproliferation). All mechanistic, no clinical
trial, no predictor.

### Timing matters — prevention vs established disease

**10. Reis2023COVIDOUT — REUSE, PMID 37302406**
Early metformin (within days of positive test) reduced long-COVID incidence ~41%.
PREVENTION trial. LC 0.85, certainty 0.80 → 0.68.

**11. Reis2026fluvoxamine / REVIVE — REUSE, PMID 41911553 (pmid field added)**
Adaptive RCT of fatigue in established long COVID: metformin arm halted for
futility; fluvoxamine superior. LC 0.85, certainty 0.75 → 0.64.

**12. Lim2026MetforminPASC (NEW, long-covid.bib) — PMID 41771135**
Double-blind placebo RCT (n=396): NEITHER metformin (1500 mg/d) nor UDCA
effectively treated established PASC. Direct negative counterpart of COVID-OUT.
LC 0.85, certainty 0.75 → 0.64.

(10-12) form the ONE robust signal in the entire topic: **metformin works at
acute-infection prevention but fails in established post-acute/fatigue disease.**
This is the strongest argument against metformin as first-line for established
ME/CFS under the triage — and it does not discriminate an immunometabolic
phenotype.

## Answer to the core question

1. **No evidence supports the phenotype-guided LDN-vs-metformin triage rule.**
   There is no head-to-head, no combined-stratification, and no predictive marker
   trial. Both a direct LDN-vs-metformin comparison (search Q2) and any
   biomarker-stratified fatigue drug trial (Q12-14) returned zero relevant hits.

2. **The triage inputs are absent.**
   - LDN arm: TRPM3 NK-cell function is a reproducible ME/CFS/LC biomarker
     (Sasso2026trpm3, Sasso2025LDNtrpm3LC) and LDN restores it in vitro — but no
     study shows baseline TRPM3 (or any immune subtype marker) predicts who
     RESPONDS to LDN. Responder evidence is weak/null (FINAL nulls; Gouda).
   - Metformin arm: no HOMA-IR/AMPK/mTOR/immunometabolic marker has been tested
     as a metformin-response predictor in any fatigue population. Even in T2DM,
     the strongest genetic predictor explains little variance (Zhou2011). The
     autoimmunity trials (Sun, Gharib) did not stratify by such markers.

3. **Strongest usable signal is timing, not phenotype**: metformin prevents long
   COVID when started acutely (COVID-OUT) but fails in established disease (REVIVE
   futility, Lim2026 PASC negative). This counsels AGAINST metformin as a first-line
   in established ME/CFS regardless of phenotype — undercutting the metformin arm
   of the triage as a chronic-disease first step.

4. **Verdict for the plan's tracking table**: candidate is at best
   `#speculation` / `#open-question` — a hypothesis-generating bifurcation with no
   evidence base and missing triage inputs. Non-integration (or integration as an
   explicitly flagged speculative decision-framework with the timing caveat) is the
   evidence-consistent outcome. Metformin-first in established ME/CFS specifically
   lacks positive support (futile/negative established-disease trials).
