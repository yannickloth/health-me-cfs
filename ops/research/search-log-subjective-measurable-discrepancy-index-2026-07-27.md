# Search Log: Subjective-Measurable Discrepancy in ME/CFS

**Topic slug:** subjective-measurable-discrepancy
**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Stream description:** The pattern where patients report symptoms that standard objective measurements don't capture or contradict. Spans pupillometry-vs-self-report, IgE null despite self-reported allergy, haptoglobin depletion with normal blood tests, normal resting HR/BP with orthostatic symptoms, normal standard labs despite severe symptoms.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Direct comparison of subjective symptom report vs objective measurement in ME/CFS or CFS
- Twin studies discordant for CFS that compare subjective vs objective outcomes
- Systematic reviews documenting discordance between subjective and objective measures
- Studies measuring physical activity, sleep, cognition, or autonomic function both subjectively and objectively
- Allergy/IgE null studies in ME/CFS
- Orthostatic intolerance studies documenting normal resting measurements vs provocation abnormalities
- Negative/null biomarker studies in the context of severe symptoms
- Patient perspective papers on normal lab findings despite severe disability
- English language
- Published 1997--2026

## Exclusion Criteria

- Non-English
- Animal studies
- Pediatric CFS without relevance to adult ME/CFS
- Papers where subjective-objective comparison is incidental to main finding
- Papers already in bib with different research_stream (noted as cross-referenced)
