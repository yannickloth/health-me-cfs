## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed, English-language cohort/case-control study, systematic review/meta-analysis, or landmark mechanistic study.
- Directly tests or informs the acute-CNS-inflammation (encephalitis) → long-term dementia/neurodegeneration relationship.
- Establishes the neuroinflammation → neurodegeneration mechanism pathway (microglia/astrocyte/NLRP3), OR grounds the ME/CFS neuroinflammation analogy.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Case reports / case series (single-patient anecdotes).
- Off-target neurodegeneration (MS, Parkinson's, CSF1R) without an encephalitis/infection-dementia link.
- Papers already in the bib (Tsilioni 2022, Nakatomi 2014, Renz-Polster 2022, VanElzakker 2019 — ME/CFS microglia/TSPO evidence reused, not re-added).
- Review protocols / treatment reviews without mechanistic value.
