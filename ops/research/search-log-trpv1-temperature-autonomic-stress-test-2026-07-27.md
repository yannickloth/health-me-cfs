## Inclusion Criteria
- Direct TRPV1-autonomic-thermal relationship relevant to ME/CFS or related conditions
- Thermal challenge protocols (cold pressor, sauna, Waon therapy) with autonomic outcome measures
- Autonomic recovery measures (HRV, HRR, CBF recovery) in ME/CFS or Long COVID
- Biochemical downstream mediators (CGRP, substance P) in ME/CFS
- Competing thermoregulatory mechanisms (circadian, vasomotor)
- Null/negative results in thermal therapy or CPET recovery in ME/CFS

## Exclusion Criteria
- TRPV1 papers exclusively about pain/nociception with no autonomic/thermal component
- Animal studies without translatable autonomic-recovery protocol
- Thermal therapy papers without specific ME/CFS or CFS diagnosis
- Cold pressor test papers in non-CFS populations with no autonomic recovery endpoint
- Conference abstracts without full text
- Non-English publications without accessible abstract
