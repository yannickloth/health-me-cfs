## Inclusion Criteria
- Studies examining dopaminergic function, VMAT2, or striatal dopamine in Long COVID, ME/CFS, or post-viral fatigue
- Mechanistic studies on SARS-CoV-2 neuroinvasion in dopaminergic regions (substantia nigra, VTA, striatum)
- Therapeutic trials of dopamine-modulating agents in ME/CFS or Long COVID
- Animal models of VMAT2/dopamine deficiency relevant to fatigue
- Systematic reviews of brain imaging (PET/SPECT) in COVID-19
- Peer-reviewed publications; preprints accepted if recent and high-quality (bioRxiv → journal pipeline)
- English language

## Exclusion Criteria
- General dopamine biology without fatigue or post-viral context
- Tardive dyskinesia / VMAT2 inhibitor clinical management (e.g., Bera 2022 — excluded)
- Liver cirrhosis fatigue (e.g., Bhandari 2022 — excluded)
- Herbal supplement papers without VMAT2/dopamine mechanism (e.g., Bartkowiak-Wieczorek 2024 — excluded)
- Childhood tic disorders / pediatric neuropsychiatric without fatigue (e.g., Sun 2026 — excluded)
- Op-eds, letters, editorials (unless directly linked to primary paper)
- Pure serotonergic mechanisms without dopamine
