## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed journal articles (Nature Medicine, Nature Communications, HGG Advances, Molecular Pain, Annals of the Rheumatic Diseases, PLoS Genetics, Brain Behavior & Immunity, Pain Reports, Autoimmunity Reviews, Translational Psychiatry)
- Address fibromyalgia genetic architecture (GWAS, heritability, genetic correlation) OR nociplastic/chronic-overlapping-pain genetics OR ME/CFS genetic overlap with fibromyalgia
- Direct relevance to the primary paper's findings (CNS enrichment, shared genetic architecture, autoimmune debate, neural gene mechanisms)
- English-language

## Exclusion Criteria
- Non-genetics reviews/updates of fibromyalgia diagnosis/treatment (e.g. Sarzi-Puttini 2020, Al Sharie 2024, Jurado-Priego 2024, Badaeva 2024, Aoe 2026, Sommer 2026, Bhargava 2026, Chinn 2016, Knadler 2011, Li Y 2025 pharmacologic update)
- Papers already in the corpus (Steen2026sharedgenetic, Hirsch2025comparativeGWAS, Hirsch2025GWASPTLDSMECFS, Hajdarevic2022gwas, Schlauch2016gwas, DecodeME2025) — referenced as existing context, not re-added
- Candidate-gene / pharmacogenetic studies of single genes in chronic pain (Lotsch 2009, Samaan 2014, Pacian 2020, Gaertner 2022)
- Unrelated phenotypes (osteoarthritis Henkel 2023, suicide attempt Campos 2023, tinnitus Ma 2023, CRPS Limerick 2023, trigeminal migraine Karsan 2025, alchohol Jennings 2024)
- Duplicate/preprint of included journal paper (Johnston KJA 2024 medRxiv preprint = same as Johnston 2025 HGG Adv)
- Gut-microbiome Mendelian randomization of fibromyalgia (Wang 2023) — covered by another cycle
- Peripheral-mechanism pain studies (Cai 2025 gut microbiota pain, TRPA1 Marchi 2023) — not genetic-architecture focus
