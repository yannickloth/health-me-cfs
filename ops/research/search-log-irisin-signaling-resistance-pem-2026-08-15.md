## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed journal article (or preprint if recent + directly relevant).
- Directly addresses: irisin OR FNDC5 OR thrombospondin-1(TSP-1)/CD47/NO OR HSP90α/extracellular signaling, AND intersects ME/CFS, fatigue, PEM, fibromyalgia, sarcopenia, depression-fatigue, or the irisin-receptor mechanistic chain.
- Published 2012–2026 (irisin field post-dates 2012 discovery).
- Foundational mechanistic papers (Boström 2012, Kim 2018, A 2023) retained despite non-ME/CFS population because they define the molecular machinery Souma 2026 invokes.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Retracted papers (Samanci 2019, _North Clin Istanb_ — RETRACTED 2025).
- Non-fatigue/pain-irrelevant irisin work (pure obesity/diabetes/browning, athletic performance without fatigue angle).
- TSP-1 fibrosis papers with no NO/vascular/redox link to fatigue/energy metabolism.
- Duplicate PMIDs across queries.
- Commentary/erratum letters without novel data.
