## Inclusion Criteria
- Human subjects with ME/CFS and/or Long COVID studied for satellite cells, muscle regeneration, or FAP cells
- Muscle biopsy studies documenting regenerative markers (Pax7, PDGFRα, embryonic myosin, central nuclei)
- Foundational satellite cell / FAP biology reviews needed to interpret primary findings
- Satellite cell depletion mechanisms (inflammation, oxidative stress, metabolic dysfunction)
- Wüst lab publications on ME/CFS/LC muscle (same cohort as primary abstract)
- Publication date: 1990--2026 (2015+ preferred except historical muscle biopsy papers)

## Exclusion Criteria
- In vitro-only studies without human/clinical relevance
- Animal studies without translational relevance to human SC biology
- Pure mitochondrial studies without satellite cell/regeneration data
- Papers not in English
- General muscle fatigue/exercise papers without biopsy or regeneration component

## Cohort Overlap Detection
**CRITICAL FINDING:** Charlton 2026 AMS C14, Charlton 2025 medRxiv, Slaghekke 2026 AMS F5, and Appelman 2024 Nature Communications all share:
- Same PI: Rob Wüst, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam
- Same biobank: Amsterdam UMC/VU longitudinal post-COVID cohort
- Same sample sizes: 26 ME/CFS, 24--25 LC, 30 controls
- Same tissue: vastus lateralis biopsies from same participants

**FLAG:** "COHORT OVERLAP — All 4 publications (Appelman 2024, Charlton 2025, Slaghekke 2026 F5, Charlton 2026 C14) are analyses of the SAME patient cohort, not independent replications. The satellite cell/FAP findings are one facet of a multi-omics dissection of a single biobank."
