# Search Log: Kay 2025 — Stimulant Arousal/Reward vs Attention Networks

**Topic slug:** `kay2025-stimulant-arousal-reward`
**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Searcher:** Phase 1 literature-integrator (integrate-topic pipeline)
**Primary source:** Kay BP et al. *Cell*. 2025;188(26):7529–7546.e20. doi:10.1016/j.cell.2025.11.039 (PMID 41448140). PDF: `Literature/neurological/1-s2.0-S009286742501373X-main.pdf`.

**Core claim under evaluation:** In humans, therapeutic doses of methylphenidate/amphetamine improve subjective arousal (feeling awake/energized) and reward valuation (wanting to engage) but do NOT improve objective attention-network function or cognitive capacity. This reframes the ME/CFS stimulant differential inference: a positive stimulant response reveals low arousal/reward salience, not augmented attention capacity.

## Inclusion Criteria
- Human studies of prescription stimulants (methylphenidate, amphetamine, lisdexamfetamine, modafinil) examining attention, cognition, arousal, reward, effort, or motivation.
- Meta-analyses or systematic reviews quantifying cognitive-enhancement effects in healthy or clinical populations.
- Human task-based studies of effort/reward valuation with a fatigue or motivational frame.
- Safety/adverse-event syntheses for stimulant medications.
- Peer-reviewed preferred; preprint accepted only for the primary paper (Kay bioRxiv) where a peer-reviewed version exists.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Animal-only mechanism studies (unless no human evidence exists and the mechanism is load-bearing).
- Papers already cited in the workspace corpus under an existing key (e.g., `Koonce2024LongCovidStimulant`, `Hendrix2025AdrenergicDysfunction`, `Eckey2025PatientReported`, `Blockmans2006MPHCFS`, `Young2025solriamfetol`, `Minzenberg2008modafinil`, `Randall2005modafinil`, `Graveling2023stimulantmitochondria`).
- Sleep-deprivation-only performance studies not addressing the arousal vs attention dissociation.
- Non-stimulant cognitive enhancers (donepezil, piracetam, etc.) unless directly informational about the dissociation.
- Abstract-only records without retrievable metadata.
- Narrative/trade commentary without primary or synthesized effect estimates.
