## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed primary research or review 2020–2026
- Directly examines skeletal muscle metabolism/mitochondrial function in ME/CFS or Long COVID context
- In vitro exposure of muscle cells/tissues to patient serum/plasma
- Methodological relevance: 3D biofabricated muscle, bioenergetics, PDH/lactate metabolism, or serum-factor-induced myotube effects
- Known Wüst-lab / Scheibenbogen / Fluge group muscle papers

## Exclusion Criteria
- Studies not in English or Spanish
- Purely exercise-intervention without muscle biopsy/mechanistic component
- Non-muscle tissue focus (unless serum-factor transfer experiment)
- Papers already in corpus (Godlewska2025MRS, Charlton2026MuscleBedrest, Charlton2026SCdepletion used as cross-refs)
- Duplicate papers (same study in multiple journals)

## Key Observations
1. **Literature gap confirmed:** Mughal 2025 is the ONLY published study using 3D biofabricated skeletal muscle exposed to ME/CFS/LC patient sera. This is genuinely novel methodology.
2. **Fluge 2016 is the methodological precursor:** Exposed myoblasts (2D) to severe ME/CFS serum — found metabolic adaptations, increased mitochondrial respiration, excessive lactate. Mughal 2025 extends this into 3D.
3. **Schreiner 2020 provides the viral trigger mechanism:** HHV-6 → mitochondrial fragmentation → metabolic shutdown — patient serum transfers this phenotype to naive cells.
4. **Nilsson 2020 is an important null:** Rules out anti-mitochondrial antibodies as the serum factor — points toward cytokines/metabolites/miRNA/exosomes instead.
5. **No competing 3D muscle negative studies found.** The field is too small. Limitations acknowledged by Mughal et al. (small patient n, heterogeneous sera).
