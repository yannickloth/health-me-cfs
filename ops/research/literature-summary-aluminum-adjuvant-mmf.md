# Literature Summary: Aluminum Adjuvant → MMF → ME/CFS

Topic slug: `aluminum-adjuvant-mmf`
Date: 2026-09-01
Phase 1 (literature-integrator).

## TL;DR

The MMF→ME/CFS hypothesis is advanced almost entirely by ONE group (Gherardi/Authier,
Henri-Mondor, Créteil, INSERM U955). Their clinical evidence is a single referral
cohort analysed across multiple papers (cohort overlap). No independent replication
of the MMF→ME/CFS link was found. The strongest independent, large-n, registry-based
counter-evidence (Norwegian nationwide, Magnus 2015 — already in corpus) shows that
adjuvanted pandemic influenza vaccine does NOT raise CFS/ME risk (HR 0.97) while
infection does (HR 2.04). Regulatory safety assessments (Mitkus 2011 FDA) conclude
vaccine aluminium does not accumulate to toxic body burden. The mechanistic core
(aluminium activates the NLRP3 inflammasome → IL-1β; adjuvants biopersist and may
reach brain) is real and mechanistically plausible as a *chronic immune stimulation*
vector, but its translation to ME/CFS is not established.

## Indirect biochemical/systemic links (MANDATORY strand)

Mechanism logic: **aluminium oxyhydroxide adjuvant does the following**: (a) activates the NLRP3 inflammasome → IL-1β/IL-18 (Eisenbarth2008nalp3); (b) biopersists in phagocytes and can translocate to brain (Gherardi2015biopersist, animal); (c) low-dose long-term neurotoxicity (Crepeaux2017dose, animal); (d) in MMF cells drives LAP autophagic flux, pain chemokines, mitochondrial dysfunction (Masson2024). Searched "downstream effect AND ME/CFS": NLRP3/IL-1β inflammation is already a documented ME/CFS mechanism in the corpus (inflammasome/cytokine chapter); chronic immune stimulation and mitochondrial dysfunction are documented ME/CFS themes. The intersection is mechanistically *plausible* (a chronic immune-stimulation + neuroinflammatory + mitochondrial-stress vector), but no independent study connects aluminium adjuvants to ME/CFS. Indirect plausibility exists (≥2 papers, cert ≥0.40) but is single-group for the MMF-specific claims.

## Clinical relevance
- MEDIUM-to-LOW for clinical decision-making. MMF is a recognized histopathological entity (deltoid biopsy) but its association with systemic ME/CFS is contested and single-site. No ME/CFS diagnostic-criteria study (Fukuda/CCC/ICC/IOM) was found applying formal criteria to MMF patients — Gherardi's papers describe symptom overlap but do not systematically apply IOM (2015) criteria.
- Severity applicability: not stratified by severity in the source papers → unknown.
- Patient subset: MMF-diagnosed patients with arthromyalgia/fatigue after intramuscular vaccine; small, referral-biased.

## Integration decision support (for Phase 2)
- Direct evidence: MMF→ME/CFS rests on single-site cohort + in vitro, no independent replication. Highest discounted direct cert ≈ 0.48 (VanDerGucht2015). No paper with certainty ≥ 0.60 in ME/CFS-specific population.
- Counter-evidence is strong and independent (Magnus2015, discounted 0.85): vaccine (adjuvanted) does NOT increase CFS/ME risk in the largest registry.
- → The burden of proof is NOT met for a causal claim. Phase 2 will likely decide PARTIAL (mechanistic plausibility via NLRP3/chronic-immune-stimulation, integrated as #speculation/#open-question) or REJECT for the strong causal claim — NOT PROCEED to hypothesis-box. The indirect NLRP3→ME/CFS mechanism (≥2 papers cert ≥0.40) is the main warrant for a PARTIAL/speculative integration rather than full REJECT.
