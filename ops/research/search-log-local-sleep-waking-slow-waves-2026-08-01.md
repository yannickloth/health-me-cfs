## Inclusion Criteria
- Primary literature, reviews, or perspectives on local sleep / waking slow waves
- Papers examining slow wave sleep architecture in ME/CFS with EEG power spectral analysis
- Papers linking inflammation to sleep-wake boundary disruption
- Post-viral cognitive dysfunction studies with behavioral markers (RT variability, slowing) consistent with local sleep

## Exclusion Criteria
- Papers on conventional sleep architecture without EEG spectral analysis
- General sleep fragmentation/quality studies without mechanistic links to local sleep
- Papers already present in bib files (e.g., Jackson 2012, Moldofsky 1975, systematic reviews already in bib)
- Adenosine/sleep homeostasis papers not specifically linking to ME/CFS or local sleep framework
