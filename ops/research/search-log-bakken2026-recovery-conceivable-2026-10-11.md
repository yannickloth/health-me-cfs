## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed papers addressing: (a) BPS/psychobehavioural/CBT/GET approaches to CFS efficacy or harm; (b) recovery definition/narratives in ME/CFS; (c) epistemic trust / therapeutic alliance (in CFS or as transferable mechanism); (d) HCP/clinician perspectives on CFS; (e) epistemic injustice / psychosomatic-attribution critique.
- PMID/DOI-verifiable (no unverifiable transcription).
- Null/negative and contradicting evidence included by design.
