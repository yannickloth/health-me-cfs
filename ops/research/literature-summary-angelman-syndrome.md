## Bottom Line

Direct AS + ME/CFS literature does **not exist** (0 PubMed-indexed co-occurrence papers). The overlap is a **cross-disease mechanistic analogy**, not an established shared pathophysiology. UBE3A (E6AP E3 ubiquitin ligase) loss causes mitochondrial/energy deficits (Su 2011; Panov 2020), oxidative stress (Simchi 2023), GABAergic disinhibition (Watanabe 2026), and sleep disturbance (Qu 2024) — each a domain implicated in ME/CFS. But no paper links UBE3A or Angelman syndrome to ME/CFS directly, and autonomic/neuroinflammation overlap is unestablished.

**Net verdict for the integrate-topic pipeline:** the analogy is defensible as a cross-disease comparison (GABAergic E/I imbalance and mitochondrial-energy deficit are the strongest bridges), but it is weak and speculative. It should be presented as hypothesis-generating only, with no certainty bump, unless a downstream phase finds convergent independent support.

## Mechanistic Bridges to ME/CFS (strongest → weakest)

1. **Mitochondrial / energy deficit.** Su 2011 (Ube3a-null mouse CA1 neurons, reduced ATP) and Panov 2020 (Ube3a-dependent mitochondrial transcriptome) show UBE3A loss impairs oxidative phosphorylation. This mirrors the mitochondrial-dysfunction/energy-deficit literature in ME/CFS. Discounted certainty 0.33 / 0.18 — real but low.
2. **GABAergic E/I imbalance.** Watanabe 2026 (reduced tonic GABA-A inhibition in AS model) parallels the E/I imbalance proposed in ME/CFS (Wirth 2026, already in bib as `WirthScheibenbogen2026glutamate`). Discounted 0.30.
3. **Sleep disturbance.** Qu 2024 (fragmented sleep in AS) overlaps ME/CFS sleep pathology. Discounted 0.48 — symptom-level only.
4. **Oxidative stress.** Simchi 2023 (elevated ROS) mirrors oxidative-stress hypotheses in ME/CFS. Discounted 0.30.
5. **Proteostasis.** Kurmashev 2026 (UBE3A dosage framework) — conceptual only; no ME/CFS proteostasis link.

## Null / Negative / Competing Evidence

- **No direct AS–ME/CFS co-occurrence** (D1/D2/N1 = 0 PubMed hits). AS and ME/CFS have never been linked clinically.
- **No UBE3A + ME/CFS link** (M1 = 0).
- **No autonomic overlap** (M8, G3 screened, excluded).
- **No neuroinflammation overlap** (M6, G1 screened, excluded).
- **GABA-A + ME/CFS** direct evidence is weak — only indirect hits (benzodiazepine hypothesis LaCorte 2018; neurosteroid fatigue in cirrhosis Ahboucha 2008). The ME/CFS E/I case rests on Wirth 2026, not on AS data.
- **Competing/nuance:** UBE3A dosage sensitivity (Kurmashev) shows both loss (AS) and duplication (Dup15q) produce overlapping neurodevelopmental outcomes — the proteostasis/dosage model is not AS-specific to ME/CFS.

## Research Gaps (candidate agenda)

1. No AS–ME/CFS co-occurrence or clinical-link study exists.
2. No study has measured mitochondrial or GABAergic function specifically linking AS mechanisms to ME/CFS-relevant outcomes in adults.
3. Autonomic dysfunction in AS (distinct from epilepsy) is uncharacterized.
