## Inclusion Criteria
- Peer-reviewed, English-language systematic review/meta-analysis or primary study of systemic inflammatory markers in a relevant chronic condition (ME/CFS, dementia, depression/anxiety/PTSD)
- Directly tests or informs the inflammatory-mediated neurodegeneration hypothesis (mental-illness/stress → inflammation → CNS pathology)
- Establishes ME/CFS, mental-illness, or cross-disease inflammatory-marker profiles for comparison

## Exclusion Criteria
- Preclinical/animal/in-vitro cytokine studies (covered elsewhere in the paper)
- Papers already in the bib (Nakatomi 2014 TSPO-PET, Blundell 2015 cytokines, Dantzer 2008)
- Review protocols or tangential treatment reviews (e.g., Gao 2022 antibiotic-AD protocol)
- Pure dementia-treatment or dementia-epidemiology papers without a psychiatric/ME/CFS comparator
