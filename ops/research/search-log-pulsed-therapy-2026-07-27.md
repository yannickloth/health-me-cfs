## Key Finding
**The topic is novel at the intersection.** Three separate literatures converge:
1. **GPCR trafficking** (tachyphylaxis, internalization, recycling, resensitization) — strong mechanistic literature
2. **Rapamycin intermittent dosing** — strong translational evidence that intermittent > daily
3. **Time-dependent hormesis** (Calabrese corps) — theory supports temporal dosing windows

No papers were found that explicitly connect these three streams into a unified "pulsed therapy framework for chronic illness pharmacology." This is a gap the paper can fill.
