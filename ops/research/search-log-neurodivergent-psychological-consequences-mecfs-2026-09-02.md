## Inclusion Criteria
- ND-specific (autism / ADHD / neurodivergent) psychological-consequence content relevant to ME/CFS: autistic burnout overlap with chronic-fatigue-like exhaustion, masking + central-sensitivity / energy limits, diagnostic overshadowing (physical fatigue misattributed), ND identity/mental-health impact of chronic illness, ND fatigue comorbidity.
- Lived-experience definitions and empirical (quantitative, qualitative, register) studies preferred; high-quality reviews accepted as supporting.
- Populations may be ND-only (autistic burnout) where the phenotype is directly the exhaustion/energy-loss construct under study (target overlap claim).

## Exclusion Criteria
- Papers already covered under the predisposition / brain-energy angle (`literature-adhd-energy-mecfs-predisposition.md`, ch07/ch14d): prevalence-only ADHD/ASD→ME/CFS risk, catecholamine/metabolic mechanism, hypermobility-mediation replication of Quadt 2024.
- Papers on ND traits in physicians/health-professionals with no chronic-illness population (e.g., resident burnout, Watanabe 2026).
- Generic ND psychological distress without chronic-illness / fatigue relevance.
- Case reports (single patient).
- Off-target hits (dry eye, strabismus, parenting strain, nutrition).

## Flow
- Unique candidate PMIDs across all queries screened: ~45 (after dedup of overlapping hits).
- Title/abstract excluded: ~30 (off-target, predisposition-only, generic ND psych, no chronic-illness link).
- Full abstract review: 15.
- Final included: **11** (see literature file).
- Notable null finding: direct studies measuring psychological consequences in individuals with BOTH formal ME/CFS AND autism or ADHD are essentially absent; evidence is concentrated in (a) autistic burnout phenomenology overlapping energy loss, (b) masking + central-sensitivity syndromes, (c) perceived CFS misdiagnosis in autistic adults, (d) ADHD-vs-CFS transdiagnostic comparison.

## Limitations
- PubMed only. Google Scholar, PsycINFO, medRxiv preprints, and reference-chaining of included full texts NOT run. Additional masking/special-interest/ADHD-burnout grey literature likely exists on Scholar/preprints and is a stated gap.
- Query #9 (identity) returned few usable hits; loss-of-special-interests / cognitive-identity literature in ME/CFS specifically was not found as primary data — only Clarey 2026 (identity disruption in burnout) is proximate.
- ADHD-specific psychological-consequence literature with ME/CFS is sparse (counts of 2–6, mostly overlapping autism/overshadowing or case reports).
