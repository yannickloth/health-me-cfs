## Inclusion Criteria
- Published 2015–2026 (IOM 2015 onwards, captures the modern PEM-centric era)
- Peer-reviewed journal or major institutional report (IOM)
- Addresses the definitional/construct distinction between PEM and fatigue
- Addresses PEM as operationalized in diagnostic criteria (IOM/CCC/Fukuda)
- Provides psychometric or factor-analytic evidence on PEM structure
- ME/CFS or Long COVID population (with comparative relevance)

## Exclusion Criteria
- Pure PEM mechanism papers (energy metabolism, immune, vascular) — covered by separate cycles
- Treatment trials without construct-validity component
- Case reports, editorials, opinion pieces without empirical data
- Pre-2015 papers except Fukuda 1994 and CCC 2003 (historical reference points, already in corpus)
- Duplicate PMID across queries
