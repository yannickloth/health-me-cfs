## Missing Evidence

1. **No US-specific cost-of-illness study using administrative claims data** — CDC/IOM estimates are extrapolations from survey data and prevalence estimates, not direct cost measurement
2. **No longitudinal lifetime-cost study** — all data are cross-sectional snapshots
3. **No formal ROI analysis for ME/CFS research funding** — Mirin2020 shows disparity but does not model returns
4. **No caregiver economic valuation study specific to ME/CFS** — Brittain2021 documents impact without monetary quantification
5. **No cost-effectiveness study of ME/CFS diagnosis pathway** — Cochrane2021 confirms this gap
6. **No developing-country economic data** — all studies from US, UK, Australia, NZ, Western Europe
