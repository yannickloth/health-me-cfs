# Pathway–Drug Trace: Guanfacine + NAC Inter-Cluster Bridge (2026-08-22)

## Mechanism chain (≥3 steps, with drug interception)

1. Chronic neuroinflammation / infection trigger → sustained oxidative stress (ROS) + inadequate glutathione/Nrf2 antioxidant response (ME/CFS documented oxidative stress).
2. Parallel deficits emerge:
   - **PFC noradrenergic deficit** — low catecholamine tone / inadequate α2A signaling for working-memory maintenance (Walitt2024 low CSF catecholamines; ATP-limited executive circuits).
   - **Redox/glutathione depletion** — GSH/GSSG imbalance, Nrf2/ARE pathway inadequacy.
3. Both converge on **prefrontal executive dysfunction / brain fog** (cognitive endpoint).

**Drug interception:**
- **Guanfacine** intercepts step 2a: PFC-selective α2A agonism restores working-memory signaling (catecholamine cluster).
- **NAC** intercepts step 2b: cysteine precursor → glutathione synthesis / Nrf2 antioxidant support (Nrf2 cluster).

**Combination rationale:** two non-overlapping bottlenecks addressed in parallel (inter-cluster bridge). NOT proven molecular synergy — no direct α2A→Nrf2 cross-talk established (0 PubMed hits).
