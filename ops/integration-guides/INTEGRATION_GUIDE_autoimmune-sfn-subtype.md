## 1. Topic and honest certainty framing (READ FIRST)

The genuine gap is the **serological autoimmune subtype** (anti-TS-HDS / anti-FGFR3), now absent from ch09 narrative. **This is a CONTESTED, LOW-CERTAINTY biomarker.** Phase 2 MUST NOT present it as an established diagnostic entity or a validated IVIG-selection tool. The evidence base is balanced and the challenge side is methodologically stronger.

**Honest certainty for the narrative: ~0.25–0.35, and only as a *proposed subgroup descriptor / association*, NOT diagnostic.**

The recommendation is to add the serological subtype into ch09 as an **`#open-question`/`#speculation`** (not `#hypothesis-box`/`#clinical-finding`), explicitly naming the null/reproducibility studies below. A clinical-implication claim (e.g. IVIG candidacy determined by TS-HDS/FGFR3 serostatus) is NOT supported.

## 2. How much the validity-critique undercuts the claim (quantified)

| Evidence class | Papers | What it does to the claim |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic association (support) | Antoine2015, Levine2020, Tholance2020, Murin2024 | Establishes the antibodies *exist* in a subgroup of general sensory/SFN patients; NOT that they discriminate ME/CFS |
| Fibromyalgia proxy nulls | Malik2019 (p=0.41 vs controls), Nissan2023 (LOWER than controls) | **Strongest ME/CFS-relevant negative** — closest proxy population fails to show elevation |
| Real-world specificity | Chompoopong2023 (34% no neuropathy; no immunoRx prediction) | Limited disease specificity; no predictive value |
| Assay reproducibility | Appeltshauser2025 (lab-specific cut-offs, 81% concordance) | No standardized test — fundamental weakness |
| Pathogenicity mechanism (support) | Salih2026 (mouse DRG) | Mechanism only; lineage-confounded |
| Immunomodulation evidence | Zeidman2022 (uncontrolled +) vs **Gibbons2023 (neg RCT), Geerts2021 (neg RCT), Ng2026 (low cert)** | **RCT nulls outweigh open-label**; no routine-IVIG basis |
| Independent reviews/editorial | Morelli2024, Katzberg2023 | Pathogenicity and efficacy "unresolved"/"fading" |

**Bottom line for Phase 2:** the supportive signal comes almost entirely from ONE laboratory/testing lineage (WashU/Phoenix/Saint-Louis FGFR3 panel + the Saint-Etienne/Lyon FGFR3 group), is descriptive/mechanistic, and does NOT extend to ME/CFS. Every independent-centre attempt to validate utility (Mayo real-world, Brigham, inter-lab, two RCTs, fibromyalgia proxies) is null or negative. **Do not let the presence of supportive papers pull certainty above ~0.35.** Frame as "proposed but not validated autoimmune serological SFN subtype hypothesis; multiple validity challenges."

## 3. Scope boundaries (what NOT to re-add)

- IENFD / skin-biopsy SFN gold standard — already in corpus (Oaklander2013sfn, Grayston2019sfn, josephoaklander2021mecfssfn).
- DRG-IgG passive-transfer axis (Goebel2021, Mignolet2026, Chen2026) — already integrated at `@sec:passive-transfer-igg` and `@hyp:drg-sfn-igg-mecfs`. The TS-HDS/FGFR3 serological subtype is a SEPARATE, contested claim and must NOT be conflated with the (stronger) passive-transfer DRG evidence.
- General autoantibody SFN reviews already present (SFNAutoantibodies2023, Oaklander2016autoimmuneSFN, Liu2020IVIG, Oaklander2022SFN, McAlpine2024sfnCOVID).
- Case reports of vaccination-triggered SFN with these antibodies (Finsterer 2021/2023, Mastropaolo 2023) — excluded, low value.

## 5. Suggested Phase 2 text block (draft — place in ch09 SFN "Potential Causes" / autoimmune area)

Add a paragraph under "Potential Causes of SFN in ME/CFS → Autoimmune mechanisms", alongside but clearly distinguished from the DRG-IgG passive-transfer axis:

> A further proposed autoimmune SFN subtype is defined by serum anti-TS-HDS (IgM) and anti-FGFR3 (IgG) autoantibodies, which several groups report in cryptogenic/non-length-dependent SFN and sensory neuronopathy @Antoine2015FGFR3 @Levine2020TSHDSFGFR3 @Tholance2020FGFR3. Preclinical work suggests FGFR3 antibodies may sensitise DRG neurons @Salih2026FGFR3Pathogenic. However, this serological claim is contested and NOT established. Independent real-world data show TS-HDS has limited specificity (many seropositive patients lack objective neuropathy and do not respond to immunotherapy more than seronegative patients) @Chompoopong2023TSHDS; the anti-FGFR3 assay lacks inter-laboratory standardisation @Appeltshauser2025FGFR3assay; and fibromyalgia cohorts — the closest overlapping population to ME/CFS — show no TS-HDS elevation over controls or even lower titres @Malik2019TSHDSfibromyalgia @Nissan2023TSHDSfibromyalgia. No ME/CFS-cohort study of these antibodies exists. Most importantly, a double-blind placebo-controlled RCT found no benefit of IVIG in antibody-positive SFN @Gibbons2023IVIGpilot, consistent with a null RCT in idiopathic SFN @Geerts2021IVIG and a low-certainty systematic review @Ng2026IVIGsystrev. Antibody-guided IVIG for SFN is therefore not supported by current evidence; the serological subtype should be regarded as a proposed but unvalidated hypothesis requiring biologically stratified, adequately powered trials.

Then place an `#open-question` and/or `#speculation` (certainty ~0.30) summarizing the evidence tension and the ME/CFS research gap (no cohort data), cross-ref `@sec:sfn` and `@hyp:drg-sfn-igg-mecfs`.

## 6. Phase 3/5 guardrails (MANDATORY)
- **MAX environment type: `#speculation` or `#open-question`, certainty ≤ 0.35.** Do NOT use `#hypothesis-box`/`#clinical-finding` for the serological diagnostic claim.
- **Do NOT claim IVIG/immunomodulation efficacy for antibody-positive SFN.** The RCTs and systematic review are null; only uncontrolled open-label data (Zeidman2022) are positive, and those conflict with the blinded data.
- **Do NOT present TS-HDS/FGFR3 serology as validated for ME/CFS** — no ME/CFS-cohort study exists (documented gap).
- **Do NOT conflate** the TS-HDS/FGFR3 serological subtype with the stronger IgG-DRG passive-transfer axis (`@hyp:drg-sfn-igg-mecfs`) already in ch09.
- If writing the supportive line (Antoine/Levine/Tholance/Salih), immediately pair it with the challenge line (Chompoopong/Appeltshauser/Malik/Nissan/Gibbons/Geerts/Ng) so the text is balanced.
- The negative fibromyalgia results (Malik, Nissan) are the most ME/CFS-relevant and MUST be cited.
