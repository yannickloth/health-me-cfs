## Cascade Branches

### Branch 1: Nav1.4/Sodium-Channel Hyperexcitability → Cramps/Myotonia → PEM-Relevant

*Cascade:* (full step-by-step)

1. Nav1.4 (_SCN4A_) gain-of-function → persistent muscle-fiber Na⁺ current → abnormal membrane hyperexcitability.
2. Hyperexcitability → spontaneous/triggered muscle-fiber discharges → painful cramps + myotonic discharges (Torbergsen 2015 primary channelopathy evidence).
3. Cramps/myotonia → muscle overactivity → increased ATP demand in a fatigued/energy-limited muscle.
4. If ME/CFS muscle is energy-limited (mitochondrial/metabolic), cramp-triggered overactivity compounds the energy deficit → overlapping the PEM/exertional-fatigue arm.

## Discriminating Probes

- **No clean discriminator exists** for quinine in ME/CFS: the only directly relevant drug (quinine) is unsafe (FDA boxed warning) and multi-target, so its response cannot cleanly discriminate channel vs metabolic cramp substrate. A safer sodium-channel blocker vs pacing/recovery would be a potential discriminator, but no such ME/CFS trial exists.
