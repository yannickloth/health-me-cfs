## 3. Narrative Arc

The integration should support this argument:

1. **The discrepancy is real and replicated** — documented across sleep, autonomic, cognitive, activity, and biomarker domains
2. **It is diagnosis-independent** — present in ME/CFS, POTS, hEDS, MCAS, PASC (Novak2024)
3. **It is domain-independent** — same pattern regardless of measurement type
4. **It is not explained by confounds** — twin studies control genetics; performance validity testing rules out poor effort; deconditioning does not explain orthostatic findings
5. **It reveals measurement inadequacy, not patient unreliability** — standard clinical tests are insensitive to the specific pathophysiology (provocation-dependent abnormalities, microstructural disruption, CNS-confined pathology)
6. **Partial convergence domains exist** — HGS (Paffrath2024), HRV (Friedberg2022), provocation-induced CBF decline (vanCampen2021) — indicating the discrepancy is resolvable with appropriate testing paradigms
