## Integration Strategy — INCREMENTAL, NOT REDUNDANT

**Critical:** The ME/CFS paper ALREADY extensively covers the sickness-behavior framework (ch18 sec-02 "hypothalamic integration & the sickness" + subsec-03 "persistent sickness behavior"; ch19 "persistent sickness behavior"; and bib keys Dantzer2000neural, Dantzer2007SicknessBehavior, Dantzer2008inflammation, Morris2013mecfsSickness). The humoral/neural/transport pathways (OVLT, area postrema, vagus→NTS) are already described in `ch18-symptom-producing-mechanisms.typ:37`.

**Therefore the NEW, non-redundant contributions of this review are:**
1. **The specific, named, recently-discovered neural circuits** — (a) area postrema→brainstem (sickness behavior/eating-drinking-movement suppression); (b) organum vasculosum→ventromedial preoptic area (appetite suppression + warmth + fever); (c) **median preoptic nucleus→torpor** (hypometabolic state). The median-preoptic/torpor element is GENUINELY ABSENT from the paper (grep for "median preoptic" = 0 hits).
2. **The torpor-like hypometabolic / AMPK-mTOR "metabolic state switch"** framing — reduced ATP generation as an ADAPTIVE response (not merely damage). Partially overlaps metabolic-safe-mode (ch07/ch16) but adds the torpor-neural-circuit anchor.
3. **The two-way fever/torpor preoptic switch (Machado2025)** — supports the "defective neural circuit switch" persistence hypothesis (novel).
4. **The systematic vicious-cycle persistence synthesis** (Table 2/Fig 2) — mitochondrial↔inflammation, redox↔energy, redox↔endothelial, endothelial+NK+neuroinflammation, spike-fibrin epitope.
5. **Authoritative reinforcement** of existing sickness-behavior content with Dantzer-2025 authority + the "neuroinflammation as targetable hub" treatment framing.
