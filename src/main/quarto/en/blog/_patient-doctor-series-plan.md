## Core theses

- Conflict is structural: no biomarker + contested nosology + 12-min slots (Guise2010, Deale2001, Bayliss2014, Treufeldt2024 scoping).
- Knowledge asymmetry is bidirectional: patient depth vs doctor breadth; patient research ≠ playing doctor (Bowes2012: validation + sifting, not authority challenge).
- Trust death spiral → medical homelessness = worst outcome; continuity of care IS the treatment (Clutterbuck2024, Hammer2025 "ultimately on your own", Melby2024 "no services for you").
- Doctor side: "heartbeat moment" (Ahluwalia2010); 3 patient archetypes (well-sourced / fragile-logic / desperate) with scripts; "I don't know" preserves authority (Treufeldt2024 framework; Spanoghe2026 epistemic humility).
- Caregiver role: amplifier, not advocate-at-war.
