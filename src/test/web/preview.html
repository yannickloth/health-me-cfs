Finding — Dosing response and mechanism are interdependent
Uses the green accent and a labelled field list, e.g. post-exertional?.
Rationale
Response to a medication reveals which mechanism is broken.
Caveat
Non-response may reflect dose, timing, or absorption, not the mechanism.
Next test
Cross a dose or route threshold before concluding mechanism failure.
